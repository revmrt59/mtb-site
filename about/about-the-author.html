About the Author

The true Author behind anything of lasting value in Mastering the Bible is the Holy Spirit. He inspired the Scriptures, He opens our understanding, and He reveals Jesus through the Word.

The human author prefers to remain anonymous. I have had the privilege of serving the Lord in pastoral ministry in two churches, teaching His Word and helping people grow in their relationship with Him. But my name is not important.

There is only one name that deserves to be lifted high—the name of Jesus.

“God also has highly exalted Him and given Him the name which is above every name.” — Philippians 2:9

So, for the purposes of Mastering the Bible, you can simply call me Theophilus—a lover of God.

My desire is not that you remember the person behind this resource, but that through these pages you understand the Scriptures more clearly, encounter Jesus more deeply, and discover the words of life found in His Word.
