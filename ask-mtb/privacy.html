Ask MTB Privacy Policy

Ask MTB retrieves published or approved Mastering the Bible (MTB) reference material for use in ChatGPT. MTB’s service receives the requested document identifier and any section numbers. It uses them to return the corresponding reference text. The service does not request your name, email address, ChatGPT account information, question, or chat history.

MTB’s lookup code does not log or store request bodies and does not create accounts or build user profiles. Netlify, our hosting provider, processes network requests and may retain technical information such as request timestamps, network addresses, and response status for hosting, security, and troubleshooting. Netlify’s function logs are generally available for one to seven days, depending on the hosting plan. Netlify function log retention

OpenAI handles your conversation in ChatGPT. You can manage your conversation history and data controls in ChatGPT and remove Ask MTB from your installed plugins. OpenAI Privacy Policy

If you contact MTB for support, we use the contact information and message you provide to respond. You can ask about your information or request its deletion through Ask MTB support.
