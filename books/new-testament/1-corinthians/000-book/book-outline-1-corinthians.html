1 Corinthians Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. The Church’s Calling and God’s Faithfulness (1:1–1:9)

Paul greets the Corinthians as God’s sanctified people and gives thanks for the grace and gifts they have received. Their calling into fellowship with God’s Son provides the foundation for the correction that follows.

II. Divisions Corrected by the Wisdom of the Cross (1:10–4:21)

Paul confronts rival loyalties to teachers by proclaiming Christ crucified as God’s wisdom and power. Ministers are servants accountable to God, and the Corinthians must abandon boasting and receive Paul’s fatherly correction.

III. Holiness in the Church and the Believer’s Body (5:1–6:20)

Paul addresses tolerated sexual immorality, lawsuits among believers, and misuse of the body. The church belongs to Christ, and its communal and bodily life must reflect the cleansing and purchase He has accomplished.

IV. Marriage, Singleness, and Faithfulness in One’s Calling (7:1–7:40)

In response to matters raised by the Corinthians, Paul gives counsel about marriage, separation, mixed marriages, and singleness. His guidance seeks faithfulness to the Lord and undivided devotion in their present circumstances.

V. Christian Freedom Governed by Love and Loyalty to Christ (8:1–11:1)

Paul addresses food associated with idols by placing knowledge and rights under the demands of love. His own restraint, Israel’s failures, and the Lord’s table show why believers must reject idolatry and seek others’ good and salvation.

VI. Worship and Spiritual Gifts That Build Up the Body (11:2–14:40)

Paul addresses honor in prayer and prophecy and corrects dishonor and division in the gathered church, including abuses at the Lord’s Supper. The Spirit distributes gifts within one body, but love and intelligible, orderly participation must govern their use.

VII. The Resurrection of Christ and His People (15:1–15:58)

Paul recalls the gospel and the witnesses to Christ’s resurrection, then answers denial of the future resurrection of believers. Christ’s victory guarantees the defeat of death, the transformation of His people, and the lasting value of labor in the Lord.

VIII. The Collection, Ministry Plans, and Final Appeals (16:1–16:24)

Paul arranges the collection for the saints and explains the movements of fellow workers. His closing instructions call the church to steadfastness, love, and recognition of faithful service.
