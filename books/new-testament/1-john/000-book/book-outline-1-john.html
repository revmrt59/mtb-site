1 John Book Outline

Major Movements

I. The Manifested Life and Apostolic Fellowship (1:1–1:4)

John proclaims the eternal life manifested in Jesus Christ on the basis of apostolic hearing, sight, and personal encounter. This proclamation invites shared fellowship with the Father and His Son and the fullness of joy.

II. Walk in the Light and Remain in the Truth (1:5–2:27)

God’s character as light exposes false claims to fellowship and calls for honest confession, obedience, and love, while for those who sin Jesus Christ the righteous is the Advocate with the Father and the propitiation for their sins. John reassures his hearers, warns against love for the world, and urges them to remain in the apostolic message rather than follow those who deny the Son.

III. Live as God’s Children and Discern What Comes from Him (2:28–4:6)

Abiding in Christ prepares believers for His appearing, while the Father’s love gives them the identity and hope of His children. Righteous conduct, practical love, and confession of Jesus Christ come in the flesh distinguish what comes from God from the work and deception of the devil.

IV. God’s Love and the Life Given Through His Son (4:7–5:12)

God’s sending of His Son reveals the love that believers must share and brings confidence before Him. Faith in Jesus as the Christ overcomes the world, and God’s testimony establishes that eternal life is in His Son.

V. Assurance, Prayer, and Final Certainties (5:13–5:21)

John states that he writes so believers may know they have eternal life and explains their confidence in prayer, including prayer for a sinning brother. He closes with affirmations about God’s protection, the world’s condition, and the knowledge of the true God in Jesus Christ, followed by a warning against idols.
