1 Peter Book Outline

Major Movements

I. Living Hope and the Calling of God’s Holy People (1:1–2:10)

Peter greets God’s elect pilgrims and praises Him for the salvation and living hope secured through Jesus’ resurrection. Their redemption and new birth call them to holiness, sincere love, and growth as a spiritual house built on Christ, God’s chosen cornerstone.

II. Honorable Conduct and Faithful Witness Amid Suffering (2:11–4:11)

Peter urges believers to live honorably among the Gentiles and applies this calling to public authority, household relationships, and the whole community. Christ’s suffering, victory, and lordship ground their readiness to do good, explain their hope, leave former sins, and serve one another as stewards of God’s grace.

III. Endure the Fiery Trial and Stand in God’s Grace (4:12–5:14)

Believers must not be surprised by suffering for Christ but rejoice in their share in His sufferings and entrust themselves to God. Elders must shepherd faithfully, the community must embrace humility and resist the devil, and the God of all grace will establish His people after their suffering.
