1 Thessalonians Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. Thanksgiving for the Gospel’s Work Among the Thessalonians (1:1–1:10)

Paul thanks God for the church’s faith, love, and hope. Their reception of the Spirit-empowered gospel amid affliction has made them examples of turning from idols to serve God and await His Son.

II. Paul’s Ministry, Affection, and Concern for Their Faith (2:1–3:13)

Paul recalls the integrity and parental care of his ministry and the believers’ costly reception of God’s word. He explains his separation from them, rejoices in Timothy’s report, and prays for their growth in love and holiness at Christ’s coming.

III. A Life That Pleases God in Holiness and Brotherly Love (4:1–4:12)

Paul urges the believers to abound in the conduct they have learned, especially sexual holiness and mutual love. A quiet, responsible working life belongs to their honorable witness before outsiders.

IV. Hope and Readiness at the Lord’s Return (4:13–5:11)

Paul assures the church that believers who have died will be raised at Christ’s coming, and that the living and the dead together will always be with the Lord. Because the day of the Lord will arrive unexpectedly, those who belong to the light must remain watchful, sober, and encouraged by their hope of salvation.

V. Shared Church Life and God’s Sanctifying Faithfulness (5:12–5:28)

Paul gives instructions for honoring leaders, caring for people with differing needs, and pursuing prayerful, discerning worship. He prays for their complete sanctification and closes by affirming God’s faithfulness and requesting prayer and the public reading of the letter.
