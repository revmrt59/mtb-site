1 Timothy Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. Guard the Gospel and Fulfill the Charge (1:1–1:20)

Paul directs Timothy to confront false teaching that produces speculation rather than love from a pure heart. Paul’s own reception of Christ’s mercy displays the gospel’s saving power, and Timothy must wage the good warfare with faith and a good conscience.

II. Prayer, Leadership, and Conduct in God’s Household (2:1–3:16)

Paul calls for prayer for all people because there is one God and one Mediator, Christ Jesus, who gave Himself a ransom for all; he then gives directions for the conduct of men and women and describes the character required of overseers and deacons. He explains that these instructions concern life in the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth centered on Christ.

III. A Faithful Minister Resists Error and Models Godliness (4:1–4:16)

Paul warns about departure from the faith through deceptive teaching and false restrictions on marriage and food. Timothy must nourish the church with sound doctrine, train in godliness, and give sustained attention to his life, teaching, and public ministry of the word.

IV. Honor and Responsibility Within the Church Family (5:1–6:2)

Paul directs Timothy in respectful relationships, support for genuine widows, and the appointment, support, and correction of elders. Instructions to bondservants continue the concern that conduct within the church uphold the honor of God’s name and teaching.

V. Godliness, Contentment, and the Final Charge (6:3–6:21)

Paul contrasts sound teaching and contentment with corrupt controversy and the love of money. Timothy must pursue righteousness and keep the commandment, while the wealthy are called to generosity and all entrusted truth must be guarded.
