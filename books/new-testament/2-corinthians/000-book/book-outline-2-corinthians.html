2 Corinthians Book Outline

Major Movements

I. Comfort in Affliction and Paul’s Concern for Corinth (1:1–2:13)

Paul praises God for comfort in suffering and explains the changes in his travel plans. He seeks to spare the church further grief, urges restoration of the disciplined offender, and describes his anxiety while awaiting Titus.

II. The Glory and Integrity of New Covenant Ministry (2:14–7:4)

Paul explains his ministry as service to Christ through the Spirit, with God’s power displayed in fragile human vessels. Eternal hope and Christ’s reconciling work sustain his endurance and his appeal for the Corinthians to open their hearts and live as God’s holy people.

III. Titus’s Report and the Joy of Repentance (7:5–7:16)

Paul resumes the account of his travels and describes the comfort brought by Titus’s report. The Corinthians’ godly sorrow has produced repentance, renewed earnestness, and restored confidence between them and Paul.

IV. Completing the Gift for the Saints (8:1–9:15)

Paul urges the Corinthians to complete their promised contribution, pointing to the Macedonians’ generosity and Christ’s self-giving grace. Careful arrangements protect the offering’s integrity, while willing generosity supplies need and produces thanksgiving to God.

V. Apostolic Authority and Strength Through Weakness (10:1–13:10)

Paul answers accusations about his authority and exposes false apostles who threaten the church’s faithfulness to Christ. His reluctant boasting centers on suffering, weakness, and the Lord’s sustaining power as he prepares for a visit that he hopes will bring restoration rather than severe discipline.

VI. Final Appeals and Blessing (13:11–13:14)

Paul calls the believers to restoration, unity, and peace. He closes with greetings and a blessing invoking the grace of Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit.
