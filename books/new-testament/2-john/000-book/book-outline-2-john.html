2 John Book Outline

Revision 2 • October 23, 2026

Major Movements

I. Greeting in Truth and Love (2 John 1:1–3)

The Elder greets the elect lady and her children, whom he loves in truth, together with all who have known the truth that abides in believers and will be with them forever. Grace, mercy, and peace will be with them from God the Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

II. Walking in Truth and Love (2 John 1:4–6)

The Elder rejoices greatly to have found some of her children walking in truth. He then pleads for the commandment they have had from the beginning, that we love one another, and defines love as walking according to God’s commandments.

III. Abiding in the Doctrine of Christ (2 John 1:7–11)

Many deceivers have gone out who do not confess Jesus Christ as coming in the flesh, so the readers must look to themselves, “that we do not lose those things we worked for, but that we may receive a full reward.” Whoever does not abide in the doctrine of Christ does not have God, but whoever abides in it has both the Father and the Son. The recipients are not to receive or greet the teacher who comes without this doctrine of Christ, for welcoming and endorsing such a ministry shares in its evil deeds.

IV. Hope of Fellowship Face to Face (2 John 1:12–13)

The Elder has much more to write but would rather come and speak face to face, “that our joy may be full.” The children of the elect sister send their greetings.
