BEGINNING - ( arche , Strong's G746 )

Summary:

In 2 John 1:6, *arche* refers to the foundational origin of the believers' Christian life and the original apostolic truth they received at conversion. Rather than describing the cosmic creation of the universe, John uses "beginning" to anchor his readers in the unchanged, authentic message of Christ's love and commandments. Walking in what was heard "from the beginning" safeguards believers against deceptive doctrinal novelties and ensures steadfast fellowship rooted in revealed truth.

Full Study:

1. Word Picture / Background

  • The root concept of *arche* carries the fundamental idea of primacy, origin, first cause, or supreme rule.

  • In ancient Greek culture and philosophy, *arche* denoted the starting point of a line, the headwaters of a river, the first principle of a philosophical system, or the supreme seat of governmental authority.

  • In biblical literature, *arche* serves two dominant theological categories: temporal origin (the start of time, ministry, or personal faith) and functional authority (rulers, principalities, preeminence).

  • In John's writings, *arche* functions as an essential theological benchmark, pointing believers back to unalterable reality—either the eternal existence of the Logos or the unadulterated gospel proclamation first received by the church.

2. Meaning / Lexical Definition

  • The noun *arche* encompasses three main semantic nuances across the New Testament:

  • The absolute beginning of time or creation (the ultimate temporal origin).

  • The commencement of a specific historical period, ministry, or personal experience (the initial reception of truth).

  • A position of ruler, authority, or preeminence (first place in rank or order).

  • In the specific context of 2 John 1:6, *arche* denotes the initial point of the readers' salvation and the original, uncorrupted message delivered by the apostles.

3. Usage and Word Pictures

  • The term *arche* occurs 55 times in the New Testament, with John employing it 23 times across his Gospel, Epistles, and Revelation.

  • Word pictures associated with *arche*:

  • *The Anchor*: The original apostolic teaching serves as an unshifting anchor holding the local church steady amidst shifting cultural and theological winds.

  • *The Foundation*: Just as a building's stability depends on its original cornerstone and foundation, Christian love and lifestyle depend on the original commandment received at the start.

  • *The Headwaters*: The pure stream at the mountain source before downstream pollutants distort the water.

4. Theological Implications

  • *Apostolic Truth is Unchanging*: Divine revelation is complete and foundational; true theology does not evolve into novel doctrines that contradict the original message.

  • *Love Defined by Truth*: True Christian love is not a subjective, ever-changing emotion, but obedience to the specific moral commandments revealed from the start.

  • *Protection Against Apostasy*: Spiritual maturity is measured not by seeking new, esoteric spiritual claims, but by abiding deeply in the original truth already delivered.

5. Contextual Nuance in the Passage

  • In 2 John 1:6, John addresses "the elect lady and her children" during a time when itinerant false teachers were spreading Christological errors.

  • The prepositional phrase "from the beginning" (*ap' arches*) highlights historical continuity and spiritual fidelity.

  • John reminds the church that the commandment to love one another is not a novel requirement, but the primary instruction heard from the outset of their Christian journey.

  • True spiritual progress is not found in moving beyond the original gospel, but in walking more faithfully within it.

6. Key Uses in Other Verses (NKJV)

  • John 1:1: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

  • 1 John 1:1: "That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our eyes, which we have looked upon, and our hands have handled, concerning the Word of life—"

  • 1 John 2:7: "Brethren, I write no new commandment to you, but an old commandment which you have had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word which you heard from the beginning."

  • Mark 1:1: "The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God."

  • Colossians 1:18: "And He is the head of the body, the church, who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, that in all things He may have the preeminence."

7. Other Relevant Insights

  • *Contrast with the Adversary*: In John 8:44, Jesus uses *arche* to describe Satan as a murderer "from the beginning," contrasting the ultimate origin of lies with the eternal truth of God.

  • *Pastoral Application*: Effective discipleship focuses on deepening believers' grasp of foundational gospel truths rather than chasing novel theological trends.
