East Gate Bethlehem

EG Culture - 2 John 1

Misson

Culture Statement

Elevating and Empowering Sons and Daughters to Be the Place Where Heaven Touches Earth.

Living It in 2 John 1

2 John shows us that a healthy spiritual culture must be characterized by both truth and love. John refuses to separate the two. We are called to love one another, but genuine love is defined by God and walks according to His commandments. We are also called to hold firmly to the truth about Jesus Christ without allowing concern for truth to become suspicion and finger-pointing toward everyone with whom we disagree on the non-essentials.

This chapter speaks directly to the kind of culture we want to build at East Gate. We want people who know the truth, walk in the truth, and love one another well. We want Jesus to remain at the center of what we believe and everything we pursue and do. We also want relationships in which people genuinely share life together rather than merely exchanging information or gathering in the same building.

Health: Trickle Down

Culture Statement

We are healthy when our thoughts, feelings, and desires reflect God’s. When we care for our spiritual, physical, financial, emotional, and interpersonal health, we are empowered to influence our communities for the better. Health flows from the top down and from the inside out.

Living It in 2 John 1

Healthy believers do not choose between truth and love. John joins them from the opening verses and then shows that love walks according to God's commandments. Truth without genuine love can become harsh and destructive, while what is called love separated from God's truth is not love.

Let’s be healthy by allowing the truth of what we believe impact all areas of our life, spiritual, physical, financial, emotional, and interpersonal, so that we might truly be able to influence our communities for the better

Honor: Through His Eyes

Culture Statement

Relationships are protected when we ascribe heaven’s value to the people around us. Like Jesus, we choose to interact with people, not as they deserve, but according to their God given value.

Living It in 2 John 1

John's warning about false teachers does not give us permission to dishonor people. His concern is that believers not provide support that helps serious false teaching about Jesus spread. It does not mean we become spiritual attack dogs against anyone and everyone to whom we disagree. We can treat people with dignity, kindness, and respect without agreeing with everything they believe or supporting everything they do.

A culture of honor should therefore be able to hold conviction without contempt. A culture of honor can disagree without becoming, divisive and disagreeable. We do not need to tear others down to demonstrate that we are faithful to truth as we understand it.

Fellowship: Assembly Required

Culture Statement

We are united by our pursuit of the Lord. We come together to discover His heart and partner with His will. Breaking bread is optional, but strongly encouraged.

Living It in 2 John 1

John could communicate through a letter, but he did not want written communication to replace personal relationship. He wanted to come and speak “face to face” so that their joy might be full.

East Gate should be a place where people do more than attend the same services. We come together – which means we need spend time with each other in person, serving together, praying together, and sharing life with one another, all without which, true fellowship is not possible.

Don’ hide behind video games, social media, or your keyboard. Embrace John’s wisdom of speaking “face-to-face”.

We are People of the Word

Culture Statement

The Holy Bible is the only infallible and final authority for Christian faith and life. The Holy Scriptures are timeless, inerrant and are to be read personally, discerned corporately, and walked out in community. We interpret all Scripture through the life and teachings of Jesus, trusting Holy Spirit to reveal truth and transform us as we submit ourselves fully to God’s revealed Word.

Living It in 2 John 1

John rejoiced when he found believers “walking in truth.” Knowing Scripture is important, but being People of the Word means more than knowing what the Bible says. God's truth is meant to characterize how we live in our homes, relationships, work, finances, conversations, choices, and everyday lives.

At East Gate, we do not want simply to accumulate biblical information. We want the Word of God to enter our lives deeply enough that people can see its effect in the way we live.

Presence Driven

Culture Statement

Holy Spirit is God Himself dwelling within us. He directs our decisions, shapes our communities, and equips us to supernaturally impact our surroundings. We hear, connect, and obey as He leads. We are Spirit-led and unashamed.

Living It in 2 John 1

Keeping Jesus at the Center: John warns about those who move beyond the teaching about Christ in pursuit of something presented as spiritual advancement or something new and greater. His warning is especially important for a church that values the presence and activity of the Holy Spirit. We should welcome everything God genuinely wants to do among us, but spiritual hunger must never move us away from Jesus or beyond the truth God has revealed about Him.

The Holy Spirit does not lead us away from Jesus. Genuine encounters with God will always increase our love for Jesus, deepen our surrender to Him, and cause His character to become increasingly visible in our lives. God’s Presence, as well as growing in maturity, will always draw us closer to Him.

Daily Secret Place

Culture Statement

When we set apart time and space to meet with God, Holy Spirit responds to our expectancy with His Presence. When we make the secret place our priority we become the place where Heaven touches Earth.

Living It in 2 John 1

Look to Yourselves: John warns believers about deceivers, but then tells them, “Look to yourselves.” This is an important word for leaders and for all believers. It is easy to become occupied with what is happening in other churches, ministries, movements, or people's lives while failing to pay attention to our own walk with God.

The Secret Place keeps the attention where it must begin – examining and searching our own hearts, listen for the Shepherd’s voice in our own life, responding to His correction, and walking out our own obedience.

Communication

Culture Statement

We “real talk” because we desire real relationships built on trust. We have 100% of our conversations, choosing to be authentic, honest, and open with one another. Awkward today, stronger tomorrow.

Living It in 2 John 1

John knew that some things were better discussed face to face than put in writing. Leaders should develop the same wisdom. Email, texts, and social media are useful tools, but they are often poor places for correction, counseling, conflict, or emotionally charged conversations.

East Gate leaders should model communication that protects relationships rather than merely delivers information. Honoring another person means refusing to conduct a difficult conversation or communicate unpleasant news from behind a screen, in a text, or even over the phone and instead making the time to sit together, listen carefully, speak honestly, and work towards having 100% of the conversation in person, regardless how awkward or difficult. This is truly Biblical wisdom. Awkward today, stronger tomorrow.
