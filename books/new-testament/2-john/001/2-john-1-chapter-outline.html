2 John 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

2 John’s single chapter is the whole letter. The Elder calls the elect lady and her children to walk in truth and love, to abide in the doctrine of Christ, and to refuse support to deceivers who deny Him, while he longs for the full joy of fellowship face to face.

Chapter Sections

1. 2 John 1:1–3 — The Elder Greets the Elect Lady in Truth and Love

The Elder writes to the elect lady and her children, whom he loves in truth, together with all who have known the truth that abides in believers. Grace, mercy, and peace will be with them from God the Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

2. 2 John 1:4–6 — Walking in Truth and in Love

The Elder rejoices to have found some of her children walking in truth, as the Father commanded. He pleads for the commandment they have had from the beginning, “that we love one another,” and defines love as walking according to His commandments.

3. 2 John 1:7–9 — Abide in the Doctrine of Christ

Many deceivers who do not confess Jesus Christ as coming in the flesh have gone out into the world, so the readers must look to themselves. Whoever does not abide in the doctrine of Christ does not have God, but whoever abides in it has both the Father and the Son.

4. 2 John 1:10–11 — Do Not Receive the Teacher Who Denies Christ

The church is not to receive into the house or greet anyone who comes without this doctrine. To greet such a teacher is to share in his evil deeds.

5. 2 John 1:12–13 — Hope of Speaking Face to Face

The Elder has much more to write but hopes to come and speak face to face, so that their joy may be full. The children of the elect sister send their greetings.
