Dwelling in the Word

What truth do I readily affirm but resist when it confronts something I want? (v. 4)

When I need to speak a hard truth, am I speaking because I love the person or because I want to prove I am right? (vv. 5–6)

Where am I coasting, drifting, or quietly compromising in my own walk with God? (v. 8)

Has anything that sounds newer, deeper, or more advanced begun to make Jesus less central in my life? (v. 9)

Am I confusing loving people with endorsing their beliefs, or using discernment as a reason to withhold love and kindness? (vv. 10–11)

Is there a conversation I have been avoiding or handling through a screen that God is asking me to have face to face? (v. 12)
