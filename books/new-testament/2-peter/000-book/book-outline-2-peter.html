2 Peter Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. Grow in Godliness and Make Your Calling Sure (1:1–1:11)

Peter greets believers who share the same precious faith and points to God’s power and promises as the provision for their growth. They must diligently add the qualities of a fruitful life so that their calling and election are made sure and entrance into Christ’s everlasting kingdom is richly supplied.

II. Remember the Trustworthy Witness to Christ (1:12–1:21)

Aware of his approaching departure, Peter seeks to establish the believers in remembered truth. The apostolic eyewitness of Christ’s majesty and the Spirit-given prophetic word confirm the message they must heed.

III. Recognize False Teachers and Their Certain Judgment (2:1–2:22)

False teachers will secretly bring in destructive heresies, even denying the Lord who bought them; they exploit believers and promote corrupt living. God’s past judgments demonstrate that He can rescue the godly and hold the unrighteous for judgment, while the teachers’ conduct exposes the slavery behind their promises of freedom.

IV. Await the Lord’s Coming in Holiness and Steadfastness (3:1–3:18)

Peter answers scoffers who deny the promised coming by appealing to God’s word, past judgment, and patient purpose. The coming day and promised new creation require holy conduct, diligence, discernment, and continued growth in the grace and knowledge of Jesus Christ.
