2 Thessalonians Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. Thanksgiving and Assurance of God’s Righteous Judgment (1:1–1:12)

Paul thanks God for the church’s growing faith and persevering love under persecution. He assures them of God’s coming justice and prays that their lives will honor the name of the Lord Jesus.

II. Correction About the Day of Christ (2:1–2:12)

Paul warns the believers not to be shaken by claims that the day of Christ has already come. He recalls the rebellion and revelation of the lawless one, whose deceptive activity ends when the Lord Jesus destroys him at His coming.

III. Stand Firm in the Truth and Depend on the Faithful Lord (2:13–3:5)

Paul gives thanks for God’s saving work and urges the church to hold the teaching it has received. Prayer for comfort, gospel advance, protection, and steadfast love places their endurance under the faithfulness of the Lord.

IV. Correct Disorderly Conduct and Continue Doing Good (3:6–3:18)

Paul commands the church to address those who refuse to work and live as busybodies, appealing to his own example and prior instruction. Correction must seek a brother’s recovery, and the letter closes with peace, Paul’s authenticating greeting, and grace.
