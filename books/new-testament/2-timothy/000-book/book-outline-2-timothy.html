2 Timothy Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. Rekindle the Gift and Guard the Gospel Without Shame (1:1–1:18)

Paul recalls Timothy’s sincere faith and urges him to stir up God’s gift and share in suffering for the gospel. Christ has brought life and immortality to light, and Timothy must guard the entrusted truth through the Holy Spirit despite the desertion of others.

II. Endure as a Faithful Worker and Entrust the Truth to Others (2:1–2:26)

Paul calls Timothy to strength in grace, perseverance in suffering, and transmission of the apostolic teaching to reliable people, remembering that Jesus Christ, of the seed of David, was raised from the dead and that the word of God is not chained. Timothy must handle the word rightly, reject corrupt disputes, pursue holiness, and correct opponents with gentleness.

III. Remain in Scripture and Fulfill the Ministry of the Word (3:1–4:8)

Paul describes the corrupt character and deceptive teachers of perilous times, contrasting them with the teaching and conduct Timothy has learned. Inspired Scripture equips God’s servant, and the certainty of Christ’s judgment grounds the solemn charge to preach the word as Paul nears the end of his race.

IV. Paul’s Final Requests, Confidence, and Greetings (4:9–4:22)

Paul asks Timothy to come, gives news of coworkers, and warns about opposition. Though people have deserted him, the Lord has stood with him, and Paul closes in confidence that the Lord will preserve him for His heavenly kingdom.
