2 Timothy 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

After greeting Timothy and thanking God for his genuine faith, Paul sets his final charge inside his love for him, calling Timothy to stir up God’s gift, to share in suffering for the gospel without shame, and, with the help of the Holy Spirit, to guard the gospel Paul entrusted to him. The chapter closes by contrasting those in Asia who turned away from Paul with Onesiphorus, who was not ashamed of Paul’s chain.

Chapter Sections

1. 2 Timothy 1:1–2 — Paul Greets His Beloved Son

Paul writes as an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, “according to the promise of life which is in Christ Jesus” (2 Timothy 1:1), and addresses Timothy as “a beloved son” (2 Timothy 1:2). He greets him with grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

2. 2 Timothy 1:3–5 — Thanksgiving for Timothy’s Genuine Faith

Paul thanks God as he remembers Timothy in prayer night and day, recalling his tears and longing to see him so that he may be filled with joy. He calls to mind the genuine faith that lived first in Timothy’s grandmother Lois and his mother Eunice and that Paul is persuaded now lives in Timothy also.

3. 2 Timothy 1:6–7 — Stir Up the Gift of God

On the basis of that faith, Paul reminds Timothy to stir up the gift of God he received through the laying on of Paul’s hands. Timothy can do this because God has not given “a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind” (2 Timothy 1:7).

4. 2 Timothy 1:8–12 — Sharing in Gospel Suffering Without Shame

Paul urges Timothy not to be ashamed of the testimony of the Lord or of Paul His prisoner, but to share in suffering by the power of God for the gospel of God’s grace, which was given in Christ before time began and is now revealed in the Savior who abolished death. Paul, appointed to preach that gospel, suffers without shame because he knows whom he has believed and is persuaded that Christ is able to keep Paul himself and his future, which he has entrusted to Christ, until that Day.

5. 2 Timothy 1:13–14 — Holding Fast the Sound Words and Guarding the Deposit

Timothy must hold fast the pattern of sound words he heard from Paul, “in faith and love which are in Christ Jesus” (2 Timothy 1:13). With the help of the Holy Spirit who dwells in believers, Timothy is to guard the good thing committed to him, the gospel and sound teaching Paul handed on (2 Timothy 1:14).

6. 2 Timothy 1:15–18 — Those Who Turned Away and Onesiphorus Who Stood Firm

All those in Asia, among them Phygellus and Hermogenes, have turned away from Paul, but Onesiphorus often refreshed him, was not ashamed of his chain, searched for him in Rome until he found him, and had already served him in many ways at Ephesus. Paul therefore asks the Lord to grant mercy to his household and to grant Onesiphorus mercy in that Day.
