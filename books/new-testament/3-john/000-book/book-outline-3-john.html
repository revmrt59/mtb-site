3 John Book Outline

Revision 3 November 17, 2026

Major Movements

I. Greeting and Joy in Gaius’s Faithfulness (1:1–1:4)

The Elder greets Gaius, whom he loves in truth, and prays that all may go well with Gaius’s circumstances and health, just as his soul is thriving. Brethren have reported that Gaius walks in the truth, and that report gives the Elder the greatest joy he knows as a spiritual father.

II. Support Faithful Workers and Reject Self-Seeking Opposition (1:5–1:12)

Gaius’s walk in the truth takes visible form as he faithfully welcomes traveling brethren, even those who were strangers to him, and the Elder urges him to send them on their journey in a manner worthy of God, so that he shares in their work. In contrast, Diotrephes loves to be first: he refuses to receive the Elder, speaks against the Elder and his companions with malicious words, turns away the brethren, and puts out of the church those who want to welcome them. The Elder then calls Gaius to imitate what is good and commends Demetrius, who has a good testimony from all.

III. The Hope of Personal Fellowship and Final Greetings (1:13–1:14)

The Elder hopes to see Gaius soon and speak face to face rather than continue in writing. He closes with peace, greetings from their friends, and a request that Gaius greet the friends by name.
