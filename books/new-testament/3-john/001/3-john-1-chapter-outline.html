3 John 1 Chapter Outline

Revision 3 · November 26, 2026

Chapter Focus

The Elder rejoices that Gaius walks in the truth and urges him to keep welcoming and sending the workers who went out for His name, so that believers become “fellow workers for the truth.” He then sets the self-seeking opposition of Diotrephes against the good testimony of Demetrius and closes with the hope of speaking face to face.

Chapter Sections

1. 3 John 1:1–4 — The Elder Prays for Gaius and Rejoices in His Walk

The Elder greets “the beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth” and prays that his circumstances and health will prosper as his soul prospers. Brethren have testified that Gaius walks in the truth, and the Elder has “no greater joy than to hear that my children walk in truth.”

2. 3 John 1:5–8 — Welcoming and Sending Those Who Went Out for His Name

The Elder commends Gaius for faithfully serving the brethren who were strangers to him and urges him to send them forward “in a manner worthy of God.” Because these workers went out for His name and took nothing from the Gentiles, believers ought to receive them and so become “fellow workers for the truth.”

3. 3 John 1:9–10 — Diotrephes Refuses the Elder and Shuts Out the Brethren

Diotrephes, “who loves to have the preeminence,” does not receive the Elder. He speaks against the Elder and his companions “with malicious words,” refuses the brethren, forbids those who wish to receive them, and puts them out of the church, and the Elder says he will call these deeds to mind if he comes.

4. 3 John 1:11–12 — Imitate What Is Good: Demetrius Commended

The Elder urges Gaius to imitate what is good rather than what is evil, for the one who does good is of God. He then commends Demetrius, who has “a good testimony from all, and from the truth itself,” and the Elder and those with him add their own true witness.

5. 3 John 1:13–14 — Hope of Speaking Face to Face and Final Greetings

The Elder has many things to write but would rather not continue with pen and ink, for he hopes to see Gaius shortly and “speak face to face.” He closes with peace, greetings from their friends, and a request to “greet the friends by name.”
