Colossians Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. Thanksgiving and Prayer for a Fruitful Gospel Life (1:1–1:14)

Paul gives thanks for the Colossians’ faith, love, and hope as the gospel bears fruit among them. He prays for knowledge of God’s will and worthy conduct, grounding their life in the Father’s rescue and their redemption in His Son.

II. The Preeminent Christ and Paul’s Ministry of His Mystery (1:15–2:5)

Paul proclaims Christ’s supremacy in creation, reconciliation, and the church. His ministry and struggle aim to present believers mature in Christ and firmly assured of the mystery of God; it is in Christ that all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden.

III. Remain Rooted in Christ and Reject Human Regulations (2:6–2:23)

Paul urges the church to continue in Christ rather than be taken captive by teaching grounded in human tradition. Their fullness, forgiveness, and victory are in Him, so regulations, religious judgments, and false humility cannot supply what He has already given.

IV. Live the New Life Under Christ’s Lordship (3:1–4:6)

Those raised with Christ must seek what is above, put away the old life, and put on the character of the new humanity. Christ’s peace and word govern their common life, household relationships, prayer, and wise conduct toward outsiders.

V. Messengers, Greetings, and Final Instructions (4:7–4:18)

Paul commends the messengers bringing news and encouragement and sends greetings from his fellow workers. Instructions about sharing the letter, fulfilling ministry, and remembering his chains close with grace.
