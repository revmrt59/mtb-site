Colossians 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Colossians 3 turns from Paul's warnings against human regulations to the new life that grows out of being raised with Christ. The chapter moves from the believer's settled life "hidden with Christ in God," to putting off the old ways and putting on the new, to the shared peace of the one body, and then into the household, where each relationship is lived in the Lord.

Chapter Sections

1. Colossians 3:1–4 — Raised with Christ, Seeking the Things Above

Because believers were raised with Christ, Paul calls them to seek and set their minds on "those things which are above, where Christ is, sitting at the right hand of God." Their life is already hidden with Christ, and when Christ who is their life appears, they will appear with Him in glory.

2. Colossians 3:5–11 — Putting Off the Old Life and Putting On the New Man

On that ground Paul commands believers to put to death sexual sin, evil desire, and covetousness, and to put off anger, malice, filthy language, and lying, the ways they once walked in. They have put off the old man and put on the new man, which is being renewed according to the image of its Creator, in a community where "Christ is all and in all."

3. Colossians 3:12–17 — Clothed with Love and Ruled by Christ's Peace in One Body

As "the elect of God, holy and beloved," believers put on tender mercies, humility, and patience, forgive one another as Christ forgave them, and above all put on love. The peace of God rules among those called "in one body," the word of Christ dwells richly in their teaching and singing, and they do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus with thanksgiving.

4. Colossians 3:18–25 — Wives, Husbands, Children, Fathers, and Bondservants Under the Lord

Paul speaks to wives and husbands, children and fathers, and bondservants, meaning enslaved people, and he places each relationship under the Lord. Enslaved believers are to work sincerely and heartily because "you serve the Lord Christ" and will receive the inheritance from Him; the paired command to masters in 4:1 completes this instruction.
