Ephesians Book Outline

Major Movements

I. God’s Purpose and Power Revealed in Christ (1:1–1:23)

After greeting the saints, Paul praises God for the spiritual blessings given in Christ according to His eternal purpose. He prays that believers will know their hope, inheritance, and the power displayed in Christ’s resurrection and exaltation as Head over all things to the church.

II. God Creates One New People Through Christ (2:1–3:21)

God makes the spiritually dead alive by grace and reconciles Jews and Gentiles to Himself in one body through the cross. Paul explains his stewardship of this revealed mystery and prays that the church will be strengthened to know Christ’s love and be filled with God’s fullness.

III. Walking Worthy in Unity and Holiness (4:1–5:21)

Paul urges the church to preserve the Spirit’s unity and grow together through Christ’s gifts. Their new identity requires putting off the old way of life and walking in love, light, and wisdom as people filled with the Spirit.

IV. Christ’s Lordship in Household Relationships (5:22–6:9)

Paul applies the life of submission introduced in 5:21 to wives and husbands, children and parents, and servants and masters. Each relationship is placed under accountability to Christ, and marriage is related to His self-giving love for the church.

V. Standing in God’s Strength and Receiving Final Encouragement (6:10–6:24)

The church must stand against spiritual evil by taking up God’s armor and persevering in prayer. Paul requests prayer for bold proclamation, sends Tychicus to encourage them, and closes with peace, love, and grace.
