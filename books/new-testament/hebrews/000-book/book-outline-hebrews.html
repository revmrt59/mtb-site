Hebrews Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. God’s Son and the Great Salvation He Brings (1:1–2:18)

God’s Son surpasses the angels as the divine King through whom God has spoken and accomplished purification for sins. By sharing humanity and suffering death, Jesus brings many sons to glory, defeats the devil’s power, and becomes a merciful and faithful High Priest.

II. Hear God’s Voice and Enter His Rest Through Faith (3:1–4:13)

Jesus is worthy of greater glory than Moses, and His people must remain faithful to Him. Israel’s wilderness unbelief warns against hardened hearts, while God’s continuing promise of rest summons believers to diligent faith before the God who knows them completely.

III. Jesus’ Perfect Priesthood and the Better Covenant (4:14–10:18)

Jesus’ sympathetic priesthood gives believers confidence to approach God, while a solemn warning against falling away and a strong encouragement to hope press them on to maturity. His enduring priesthood, heavenly ministry, and once-for-all offering accomplish what the former covenant’s repeated sacrifices could not: cleansing the conscience, securing redemption, and perfecting those being sanctified.

IV. Draw Near and Endure by Faith (10:19–12:29)

Access through Jesus’ blood leads to a call for confident worship, steadfast confession, and mutual encouragement, accompanied by a solemn warning against willful rejection. The examples of faith and the supreme example of Jesus summon believers to endurance, acceptance of God’s discipline, holiness, and grateful worship in His unshakable kingdom.

V. Faithful Conduct and the Closing Appeal (13:1–13:25)

The final exhortations apply persevering faith to love, hospitality, marriage, contentment, leadership, and allegiance to Jesus. A prayer to the God of peace, personal news, and final greetings close this word of exhortation.
