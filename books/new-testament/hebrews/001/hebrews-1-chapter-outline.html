Hebrews 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Hebrews 1 opens with God’s final word spoken in His Son, the heir, Creator, and Sustainer of all things, who purged our sins and sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high. A chain of Old Testament quotations then sets the Son far above the angels and prepares for the warning that His message must be heeded (Hebrews 2:1–4).

Chapter Sections

1. Hebrews 1:1–4 — God Has Spoken in His Son

The God who spoke “at various times and in various ways” to the fathers by the prophets “has in these last days spoken to us by His Son” (Hebrews 1:1–2). The Son is heir of all things, the One through whom God made the worlds and who upholds all things by the word of His power, the brightness of God’s glory and the express image of His person, and after purging our sins He sat down at God’s right hand, having become so much better than the angels.

2. Hebrews 1:5–6 — The Son and Firstborn Whom Angels Worship

No angel was ever addressed as God addresses the Son: Psalm 2:7 declares His sonship, “You are My Son, Today I have begotten You” (Hebrews 1:5), and 2 Samuel 7:14 gives God’s father-to-son promise, “I will be to Him a Father, And He shall be to Me a Son” (Hebrews 1:5). When God brings the firstborn into the world, He commands, “Let all the angels of God worship Him” (Hebrews 1:6).

3. Hebrews 1:7–12 — Serving Angels and the Eternal, Unchanging Son

God makes His angels spirits and His ministers a flame of fire, but to the Son He says, “Your throne, O God, is forever and ever” (Hebrews 1:8), and anoints Him above His companions. Hebrews then applies Psalm 102 to the Son as the LORD who laid the foundation of the earth and remains when creation wears out like a garment: “You are the same, And Your years will not fail” (Hebrews 1:12).

4. Hebrews 1:13–14 — The Enthroned Son and the Ministering Angels

The final quotation, from Psalm 110:1, invites the Son alone to sit at God’s right hand until His enemies are made His footstool. The angels, by contrast, are “ministering spirits sent forth to minister for those who will inherit salvation” (Hebrews 1:14).
