Hebrews 8 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Hebrews 8 states the main point of the argument: believers have a High Priest seated in heaven who ministers in the true tabernacle, of which the earthly sanctuary was only a copy and shadow. His more excellent ministry rests on the better covenant God promised through Jeremiah, in which He writes His laws on hearts, makes Himself known to all His people, and forgives their sins, making the first covenant obsolete.

Chapter Sections

1. Hebrews 8:1–5 — The Main Point: A High Priest in the Heavens

“Now this is the main point of the things we are saying: We have such a High Priest” (Hebrews 8:1), seated at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens and ministering in the true tabernacle which the Lord erected. The earthly priests serve “the copy and shadow of the heavenly things” (Hebrews 8:5), made according to the pattern God showed Moses on the mountain.

2. Hebrews 8:6–7 — Mediator of a Better Covenant

Jesus has obtained a more excellent ministry because He is Mediator of “a better covenant, which was established on better promises” (Hebrews 8:6). If the first covenant had been faultless, no place would have been sought for a second.

3. Hebrews 8:8–12 — God’s Promise of a New Covenant

Finding fault with the people, God announced through Jeremiah a new covenant with the house of Israel and the house of Judah, unlike the covenant their fathers did not continue in after He led them out of Egypt. In it God will put His laws in their mind and write them on their hearts, be their God, cause all of them to know Him from the least to the greatest, and remember their sins and lawless deeds no more.

4. Hebrews 8:13 — The First Covenant Made Obsolete

By speaking of “A new covenant” (Hebrews 8:13), God has made the first obsolete. What is becoming obsolete and growing old “is ready to vanish away” (Hebrews 8:13).
