Hebrews 10 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Hebrews 10 completes the argument that Jesus’ single offering of His own body accomplished what the law’s repeated sacrifices never could, perfecting forever those who are being sanctified and bringing the forgiveness Jeremiah promised. On that basis the chapter calls believers to draw near, hold fast, and encourage one another, warns gravely against willful rejection of the Son, and urges them to remember their earlier endurance and keep their confidence until He comes.

Chapter Sections

1. Hebrews 10:1–4 — A Shadow That Could Not Take Away Sins

The law has only a shadow of the good things to come, so its sacrifices, offered year by year, can never make worshipers perfect; if they could, they would have ceased. Instead they bring a reminder of sins every year, “For it is not possible that the blood of bulls and goats could take away sins” (Hebrews 10:4).

2. Hebrews 10:5–10 — A Body Prepared to Do God’s Will

Coming into the world, the Son speaks the words of Psalm 40 in the Greek form Hebrews quotes: “a body You have prepared for Me” (Hebrews 10:5) and “Behold, I have come to do Your will, O God” (Hebrews 10:9). He takes away the first, the sacrifices offered according to the law, to establish the second, the doing of God’s will through the offering of His own body, and by that will “we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all” (Hebrews 10:10).

3. Hebrews 10:11–14 — One Sacrifice, and He Sat Down

Every priest stands daily offering the same sacrifices that can never take away sins, but Jesus offered one sacrifice for sins forever and sat down at the right hand of God, waiting till His enemies are made His footstool. “For by one offering He has perfected forever those who are being sanctified” (Hebrews 10:14).

4. Hebrews 10:15–18 — The Spirit Witnesses to Forgiveness

The Holy Spirit bears witness through Jeremiah’s promise that God will put His laws into His people’s hearts and write them in their minds, and that He will remember their sins and lawless deeds no more. “Now where there is remission of these, there is no longer an offering for sin” (Hebrews 10:18).

5. Hebrews 10:19–25 — Let Us Draw Near, Hold Fast, and Consider One Another

Having boldness to enter the Holiest by the blood of Jesus, through the new and living way He consecrated through the veil of His flesh, and having a High Priest over the house of God, believers are to draw near with a true heart in full assurance of faith, their hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience and their bodies washed with pure water. They are to hold fast the confession of their hope, for He who promised is faithful, and to consider one another to stir up love and good works, not forsaking the assembling of themselves together as they see the Day approaching.

6. Hebrews 10:26–31 — No Sacrifice Left for Willful Rejection

If we sin willfully after receiving the knowledge of the truth, no sacrifice for sins remains, only a fearful expectation of judgment. If rejecting Moses’ law brought death, much worse punishment awaits the one who has trampled the Son of God underfoot, counted the blood of the covenant a common thing, and insulted the Spirit of grace, for “It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God” (Hebrews 10:31).

7. Hebrews 10:32–39 — Do Not Cast Away Your Confidence

The hearers are to recall the former days when they endured public reproach, stood with those who suffered, and joyfully accepted the plundering of their goods, knowing they had a better and enduring possession in heaven. “Therefore do not cast away your confidence, which has great reward” (Hebrews 10:35), for He who is coming will come, and “we are not of those who draw back to perdition, but of those who believe to the saving of the soul” (Hebrews 10:39).
