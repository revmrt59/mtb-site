Verse-by-Verse
Hebrews 11:1
Hebrews 11:1
NKJV

Hebrews 11:1 Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.(NKJV)

NLT

Hebrews 11:1 Faith shows the reality of what we hope for; it is the evidence of things we cannot see. (NLT)

Main Idea

“Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.” Faith trusts God for who He has revealed Himself to be in His Word, and because it rests on His character and promises, it gives the things hoped for and His unseen future real weight in how believers live now.

Verse Explanation

“Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.” Faith trusts God for who He has revealed Himself to be in His Word, and because it rests on His character and promises, it gives the things hoped for and His unseen future real weight in how believers live now.

“the substance of things hoped for” Faith trusts in who God has revealed Himself to be in His word. Because His character and promise are sure, the future He promises has real weight in the present.

“the evidence of things not seen” Faith gives conviction about what remains unseen because it takes God at His word. The examples that follow show that conviction in worship, choices, obedience, suffering, and endurance.

Study Notes

The Greek word translated substance here is hypostasis. In Hebrews 1:3 it refers to God’s own being, while in Hebrews 3:14 it is translated confidence. Here it carries the assurance that gives what is hoped for a firm footing in the present.
