Luke Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. The Certainty of the Account and the Coming of the Savior (1:1–2:52)

Luke introduces his orderly account and recounts the announced births of John and Jesus. The birth, presentation, and childhood of Jesus establish Him as the promised Savior whose coming brings joy to Israel and hope to the nations.

II. The Preparation and Commissioning of Jesus (3:1–4:13)

John calls Israel to repentance and points to the One who will baptize with the Holy Spirit and fire. Jesus is baptized, identified as God’s beloved Son, traced through His genealogy to Adam, and tested in the wilderness.

III. Jesus’ Ministry in Galilee and the Revelation of His Identity (4:14–9:50)

Jesus announces His Spirit-anointed mission and reveals the kingdom through teaching, forgiveness, healing, and deliverance. He gathers and sends disciples, while growing opposition and unfolding revelation lead to Peter’s confession, the prediction of His death, and the transfiguration.

IV. Jesus Journeys Toward Jerusalem and Forms His Disciples (9:51–19:27)

Jesus resolutely turns toward Jerusalem and teaches what it means to receive God’s kingdom and follow Him. Along the way, His encounters and parables reveal God’s mercy toward the lost, confront misplaced confidence, and prepare His followers for the kingdom’s fulfillment.

V. Jesus’ Arrival and Teaching in Jerusalem (19:28–21:38)

Jesus enters Jerusalem as King, weeps over the city, and cleanses the temple. He answers His opponents and teaches about Jerusalem’s coming devastation, the coming of the Son of Man, and the need for watchfulness.

VI. Jesus’ Suffering, Resurrection, and Commission to the Nations (22:1–24:53)

Jesus shares the Passover with His disciples, is betrayed and crucified, and rises from the dead. He opens the Scriptures and His followers’ understanding, commissions the proclamation of repentance and forgiveness to all nations, and ascends after promising power from on high.
