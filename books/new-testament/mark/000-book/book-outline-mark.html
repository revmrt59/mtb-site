Mark Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. The Preparation for Jesus’ Ministry (1:1–1:13)

John prepares the way, Jesus is baptized, and the Father identifies Him as His beloved Son. Jesus’ temptation in the wilderness precedes His public proclamation of God’s kingdom.

II. Jesus Reveals His Authority and Calls Disciples (1:14–8:26)

Jesus announces the kingdom, calls disciples, and demonstrates His authority through teaching, healing, deliverance, and forgiveness. Crowds gather and opposition grows, while His disciples struggle to understand who He is and what His works reveal.

III. The Messiah’s Path of Suffering and Discipleship (8:27–10:52)

Peter confesses Jesus as the Christ, but Jesus explains that His mission leads through rejection, death, and resurrection. On the journey toward Jerusalem, He teaches His followers to take up the cross and understand greatness through humble service.

IV. Jesus Confronts Jerusalem and Teaches About What Is Coming (11:1–13:37)

Jesus enters Jerusalem, judges the temple’s corruption, and answers challenges to His authority. He exposes false leadership and prepares His disciples for the temple’s destruction, coming distress, and the return of the Son of Man.

V. Jesus’ Death, Resurrection, and the Sending of His Followers (14:1–16:20)

Jesus is betrayed, condemned, crucified, and buried, giving His life in fulfillment of His stated mission. The empty tomb and resurrection appearances lead to the commission to preach the gospel and the Lord’s continuing work with His messengers.
