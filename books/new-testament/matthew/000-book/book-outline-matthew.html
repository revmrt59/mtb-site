Matthew Book Outline

Revision 1 • November 5, 2026

Major Movements

I. The Coming of the King (1:1–4:16)

Matthew establishes Jesus’ identity as the promised Messiah and beloved Son of God through His ancestry, birth, preservation, baptism, and temptation. The opening movement prepares for His public announcement of the kingdom.

II. The Revelation of the King and His Kingdom (4:17–16:20)

Jesus proclaims the kingdom of heaven and reveals its character through teaching, mighty works, and the calling and training of disciples. As opposition grows, the question of His identity comes to the forefront and reaches a decisive confession when Peter declares Him the Christ, the Son of the living God.

III. The Way of the King: Cross, Resurrection, and Commission (16:21–28:20)

Jesus begins to teach openly about His coming suffering, death, and resurrection and prepares His disciples to follow Him on the way of the cross. In Jerusalem He confronts opposition, gives His life, rises from the dead, and commissions His followers under His universal authority.
