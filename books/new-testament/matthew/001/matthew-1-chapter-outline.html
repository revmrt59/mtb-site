Matthew 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 1 introduces Jesus Christ as the Son of David and the Son of Abraham through a structured genealogy, then tells of His conception by the Holy Spirit and His birth, revealing Him as the Savior who will save His people from their sins and as Immanuel, “God with us.”

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 1:1–17 — The Genealogy of Jesus Christ

Matthew traces Jesus’ ancestry from Abraham through David and the deportation to Babylon to Joseph, the husband of Mary, of whom Jesus was born. He arranges the generations in three groups of fourteen.

2. Matthew 1:18–25 — The Birth of Jesus Christ

Before Mary and Joseph come together, she is found with child of the Holy Spirit, and an angel of the Lord tells Joseph, a just man, to take her as his wife and to call the Son Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins. Matthew identifies this as the fulfillment of the prophecy that the virgin would bear a Son called Immanuel, and Joseph obeys and names the child Jesus.
