Matthew 2 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 2 shows the newborn King of the Jews receiving worship from wise men from the East while King Herod seeks to destroy Him. Through Scripture, a star, and messages given in dreams, God brings worshipers to His Son, preserves the child through the flight to Egypt and the slaughter at Bethlehem, and settles Him in Nazareth, and three times Matthew says that these events fulfilled what was spoken by the prophets.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 2:1–12 — Wise Men from the East Worship the Newborn King

Wise men come to Jerusalem asking for the one “born King of the Jews,” and Herod learns from the chief priests and scribes that the Christ is to be born in Bethlehem, as the prophet wrote. The star leads the wise men to the house where they find the young Child with Mary, and they fall down and worship Him, offer gold, frankincense, and myrrh, and return home another way after God warns them in a dream not to go back to Herod.

2. Matthew 2:13–15 — The Family Escapes to Egypt

An angel of the Lord warns Joseph in a dream that Herod will seek the young Child to destroy Him, and Joseph takes the Child and His mother by night to Egypt. They stay there until Herod’s death, fulfilling the word, “Out of Egypt I called My Son” (Matthew 2:15).

3. Matthew 2:16–18 — Herod Kills the Boys of Bethlehem

When Herod realizes that the wise men have evaded him by returning home another way, he is exceedingly angry and kills all the male children in and around Bethlehem who are two years old and under. Matthew says that Jeremiah’s word about Rachel weeping for her children and refusing to be comforted was then fulfilled.

4. Matthew 2:19–23 — The Return from Egypt and the Settlement in Nazareth

After Herod dies, an angel tells Joseph to take the Child and His mother back to the land of Israel, and Joseph obeys. Afraid of Archelaus and warned again in a dream, Joseph settles the family in Nazareth of Galilee, and Matthew says this fulfills the prophets’ word, “He shall be called a Nazarene” (Matthew 2:23).
