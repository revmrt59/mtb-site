Matthew 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 3 brings John the Baptist onto the scene as the voice Isaiah foretold, calling Israel to repent because the kingdom of heaven is at hand and warning that the One coming after him will baptize with the Holy Spirit and fire. The chapter then reaches its center as Jesus comes to be baptized, the Spirit descends upon Him, and the Father declares from heaven, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Matthew 3:17).

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 3:1–6 — John Prepares the Way of the Lord

John the Baptist preaches in the wilderness of Judea, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” (Matthew 3:2), and Matthew identifies him as the voice Isaiah foretold, crying, “Prepare the way of the LORD” (Matthew 3:3). People from Jerusalem, Judea, and the region around the Jordan go out to him and are baptized, confessing their sins.

2. Matthew 3:7–12 — John Warns of Judgment and Points to the Coming One

When many Pharisees and Sadducees come to his baptism, John demands fruits worthy of repentance, warns them not to rely on having Abraham as their father, and announces that the ax is already laid to the root of the trees. He declares that the mightier One coming after him will baptize with the Holy Spirit and fire, gathering His wheat into the barn and burning the chaff with unquenchable fire.

3. Matthew 3:13–17 — The Baptism of Jesus and the Father’s Declaration

Jesus comes from Galilee to be baptized, and when John tries to prevent Him, Jesus answers that it is fitting for them “to fulfill all righteousness” (Matthew 3:15). As Jesus comes up from the water, the heavens are opened, the Spirit of God descends like a dove upon Him, and a voice from heaven declares Him the Father’s beloved Son.
