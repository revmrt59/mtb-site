Matthew 6 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 6 continues the Sermon on the Mount by turning from conduct toward others to devotion before the Father. Jesus warns against giving, praying, and fasting to be seen by people, teaches His disciples how to pray, and then calls them to lay up treasure in heaven, serve God rather than money, and trust their heavenly Father instead of worrying, seeking first the kingdom of God and His righteousness.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 6:1–4 — Giving in Secret

Jesus warns His hearers not to do their charitable deeds before men to be seen by them, as the hypocrites do when they sound a trumpet. Their giving is to be in secret, and their Father who sees in secret will reward them openly.

2. Matthew 6:5–15 — Prayer and the Lord’s Prayer

Jesus tells His hearers not to pray like the hypocrites who want to be seen or like the heathen who use vain repetitions, for their Father knows what they need before they ask. He gives them a pattern of prayer, beginning “Our Father in heaven, Hallowed be Your name” (Matthew 6:9), and then explains that if they forgive others, their heavenly Father will forgive them, but if they do not forgive others, neither will their Father forgive their trespasses.

3. Matthew 6:16–18 — Fasting in Secret

Jesus tells His hearers not to fast with a sad countenance like the hypocrites, who disfigure their faces to be seen. Instead they are to anoint their heads and wash their faces so that their fasting is seen by their Father in secret, who will reward them openly.

4. Matthew 6:19–24 — Treasure in Heaven and Undivided Service

Jesus tells His hearers to lay up treasures in heaven rather than on earth, “For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also” (Matthew 6:21). He describes the eye as the lamp of the body and declares that no one can serve two masters: “You cannot serve God and mammon” (Matthew 6:24).

5. Matthew 6:25–34 — Do Not Worry; Seek First the Kingdom

Pointing to the birds of the air and the lilies of the field, Jesus assures His hearers that their heavenly Father knows they need food, drink, and clothing, so they should not worry about these things. Instead they are to “seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness” (Matthew 6:33), and all these things will be added to them, so they need not worry about tomorrow.
