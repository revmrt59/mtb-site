Matthew 7 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 7 brings the Sermon on the Mount to its close as Jesus teaches His disciples to examine themselves before correcting a brother, to use discernment with what is holy, to ask confidently of their good Father, and to do for others what they would want done for them. He then presses every hearer toward a decision through contrasting gates, trees, claims, and builders, and Matthew closes the Sermon by recording the crowds’ astonishment at His authority.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 7:1–6 — Judging Others and Exercising Discernment

Jesus forbids hypocritical judgment, warning that the measure we use will be measured back to us, and tells the hypocrite to remove the plank from his own eye first so that he can see clearly to remove the speck from his brother’s eye. He then warns against giving what is holy to the dogs or casting pearls before swine, since what is holy and precious may be trampled underfoot and the one who offers it may be torn in pieces.

2. Matthew 7:7–12 — Ask, Seek, Knock, and the Golden Rule

Jesus invites His hearers to ask, seek, and knock, assuring them that if earthly fathers know how to give good gifts to their children, their Father in heaven will much more give good things to those who ask Him. He then calls them to do to others whatever they want others to do to them, for this is the Law and the Prophets.

3. Matthew 7:13–14 — The Narrow and Wide Gates

Jesus urges His hearers to enter by the narrow gate. The wide gate and broad way lead to destruction and many go in by it, while the narrow gate and difficult way lead to life and few find it.

4. Matthew 7:15–20 — Recognizing False Prophets by Their Fruit

Jesus warns against false prophets who come in sheep’s clothing but inwardly are ravenous wolves. As a tree is known by the fruit it bears, His disciples will know these prophets by their fruits, and every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.

5. Matthew 7:21–23 — Doing the Father’s Will

Jesus declares that not everyone who calls Him “Lord, Lord” will enter the kingdom of heaven, but the one who does the will of His Father in heaven. On that day many will claim to have prophesied, cast out demons, and done many wonders in His name, yet He will declare to them, “I never knew you,” and send them away as those who practice lawlessness.

6. Matthew 7:24–29 — The Wise and Foolish Builders

Jesus compares the one who hears His sayings and does them to a wise man whose house on the rock stands through rain, floods, and wind, and the one who hears but does not do them to a foolish man whose house on the sand falls. Matthew then closes the Sermon by recording that the people were astonished at His teaching, for He taught as one having authority and not as the scribes.
