Matthew 8 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 8 shows the authority heard in the Sermon on the Mount at work in Jesus’ deeds as He cleanses a leper, heals a centurion’s servant by His word, and heals many sick and demon-possessed people, fulfilling Isaiah’s word about the Servant. Between these works He confronts would-be followers with the cost of following Him, stills a great storm, and casts demons out of two men in the country of the Gergesenes, whose city then begs Him to leave.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 8:1–4 — Jesus Cleanses a Leper

As great multitudes follow Jesus down from the mountain, a leper worships Him and says, “Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean” (Matthew 8:2). Jesus touches him and he is cleansed, and Jesus tells him to tell no one but to show himself to the priest and offer the gift Moses commanded as a testimony.

2. Matthew 8:5–13 — The Centurion’s Faith and the Healing of His Servant

A centurion in Capernaum asks Jesus to heal his paralyzed servant and says that Jesus need only speak a word, since he too knows what it is to be under authority and to give commands. Jesus marvels at his faith, declares that many will come from east and west to sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven while the sons of the kingdom are cast out, and heals the servant that same hour.

3. Matthew 8:14–17 — Healings at Peter’s House

Jesus touches the hand of Peter’s wife’s mother, the fever leaves her, and she rises and serves them. That evening He casts out spirits with a word and heals all who are sick, fulfilling Isaiah’s word, “He Himself took our infirmities And bore our sicknesses” (Matthew 8:17).

4. Matthew 8:18–22 — The Cost of Following Jesus

When a scribe promises to follow Jesus wherever He goes, Jesus answers that “the Son of Man has nowhere to lay His head” (Matthew 8:20). When another disciple asks first to go and bury his father, Jesus replies, “Follow Me, and let the dead bury their own dead” (Matthew 8:22).

5. Matthew 8:23–27 — Jesus Calms the Storm

A great tempest arises while Jesus sleeps in the boat, and His disciples wake Him, crying, “Lord, save us! We are perishing!” (Matthew 8:25). He rebukes their little faith, then rebukes the winds and the sea, and there is a great calm, so the men marvel and ask, “Who can this be, that even the winds and the sea obey Him?” (Matthew 8:27).

6. Matthew 8:28–34 — Jesus Delivers Two Demon-Possessed Men

In the country of the Gergesenes two exceedingly fierce demon-possessed men meet Jesus, and the demons cry out, “What have we to do with You, Jesus, You Son of God?” (Matthew 8:29). At His word the demons go into a herd of swine, which runs into the sea and perishes, and the whole city comes out and begs Jesus to depart from their region.
