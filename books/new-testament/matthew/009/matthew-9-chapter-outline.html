Matthew 9 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 9 completes the account of Jesus’ mighty works by showing His authority to forgive sins, His call of Matthew the tax collector, and His mercy toward sinners. The chapter continues with the question about fasting, the raising of a ruler’s daughter, and the healing of a woman, two blind men, and a mute man, and it ends with Jesus moved with compassion for the multitudes and telling His disciples to pray for laborers in the harvest.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 9:1–8 — Jesus Forgives and Heals a Paralytic

When people bring Him a paralytic, Jesus says, “Son, be of good cheer; your sins are forgiven you” (Matthew 9:2), and some scribes accuse Him of blasphemy. To show “that the Son of Man has power on earth to forgive sins” (Matthew 9:6), Jesus commands the man to rise and go home, and the multitudes glorify God.

2. Matthew 9:9–13 — Jesus Calls Matthew and Eats with Sinners

Jesus calls Matthew from the tax office with the words “Follow Me” (Matthew 9:9), and many tax collectors and sinners sit down with Him and His disciples. When the Pharisees object, Jesus answers that the sick need a physician and tells them to learn what this means: “I desire mercy and not sacrifice” (Matthew 9:13).

3. Matthew 9:14–17 — The Bridegroom, New Cloth, and New Wine

When John’s disciples ask why Jesus’ disciples do not fast, He answers that the friends of the bridegroom cannot mourn while the bridegroom is with them, though they will fast when He is taken away. He adds that no one puts unshrunk cloth on an old garment or new wine into old wineskins, but new wine is put into new wineskins and both are preserved.

4. Matthew 9:18–26 — A Ruler’s Daughter Raised and a Woman Healed

A ruler asks Jesus to come and lay His hand on his daughter, who has just died, and on the way a woman with a flow of blood for twelve years touches the hem of His garment, and Jesus tells her, “Be of good cheer, daughter; your faith has made you well” (Matthew 9:22). Jesus then enters the ruler’s house, takes the girl by the hand, and she arises, and the report goes out into all that land.

5. Matthew 9:27–31 — Two Blind Men Receive Their Sight

Two blind men follow Jesus, crying out, “Son of David, have mercy on us!” (Matthew 9:27), and He asks whether they believe He is able to do this. He touches their eyes, saying, “According to your faith let it be to you” (Matthew 9:29), and their eyes are opened, but though He sternly warns them to tell no one, they spread the news about Him.

6. Matthew 9:32–34 — A Mute Man Delivered and Two Responses

When a mute, demon-possessed man is brought to Jesus, the demon is cast out and the mute man speaks. The multitudes marvel that nothing like this was ever seen in Israel, but the Pharisees say that He casts out demons by the ruler of the demons.

7. Matthew 9:35–38 — Compassion for the Multitudes and Prayer for Laborers

Jesus goes about all the cities and villages teaching, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease. Moved with compassion because the multitudes are weary and scattered “like sheep having no shepherd” (Matthew 9:36), He tells His disciples to pray the Lord of the harvest to send out laborers into His harvest.
