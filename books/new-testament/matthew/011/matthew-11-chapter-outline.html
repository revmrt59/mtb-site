Matthew 11 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 11 marks the turn from Jesus’ instructions to the Twelve to a movement of rejection and recognition, as Jesus answers John the Baptist’s question from prison by pointing to His works, honors John as the promised messenger, and rebukes a generation and its cities for refusing to repent. The chapter ends as Jesus thanks the Father for revealing these things to babes and invites all who labor and are heavy laden to come to Him for rest.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 11:1 — Jesus Finishes Instructing the Twelve

When Jesus finishes commanding His twelve disciples, He departs to teach and to preach in their cities. The verse closes the discourse of Matthew 10 and returns the narrative to Jesus’ own ministry.

2. Matthew 11:2–6 — John’s Question from Prison

Hearing in prison about the works of Christ, John sends his disciples to ask, “Are You the Coming One, or do we look for another?” (Matthew 11:3). Jesus tells them to report what they hear and see, that the blind see, the lame walk, the dead are raised, and the poor have the gospel preached to them, and He adds, “And blessed is he who is not offended because of Me” (Matthew 11:6).

3. Matthew 11:7–15 — Jesus Honors John the Baptist

Jesus asks the multitudes what they went out to the wilderness to see and declares that John is more than a prophet, the messenger of whom it is written who prepares His way. No one born of women is greater than John, yet he who is least in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he, and if they are willing to receive it, John is the Elijah who is to come.

4. Matthew 11:16–19 — This Generation Rejects Both John and Jesus

Jesus likens this generation to children in the marketplaces who complain that others will neither dance to the flute nor mourn to a lament. They say John has a demon because he came neither eating nor drinking, and they call the Son of Man a glutton, a winebibber, and a friend of tax collectors and sinners, but wisdom is justified by her children.

5. Matthew 11:20–24 — Woes on the Unrepentant Cities

Jesus rebukes the cities where most of His mighty works were done because they did not repent. He declares that the judgment will be more tolerable for Tyre, Sidon, and Sodom than for Chorazin, Bethsaida, and Capernaum.

6. Matthew 11:25–30 — The Son Reveals the Father and Gives Rest

Jesus thanks the Father for hiding these things from the wise and prudent and revealing them to babes, and He declares that no one knows the Father except the Son and the one to whom the Son wills to reveal Him. He then invites, “Come to Me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest” (Matthew 11:28), and He adds that those who take His yoke upon them and learn from Him, for He is gentle and lowly in heart, will find rest for their souls.
