Matthew 15 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 15 shows Jesus answering the scribes and Pharisees from Jerusalem, who accuse His disciples of breaking the tradition of the elders, by exposing traditions that set aside God’s command and by teaching that what defiles a person comes from the heart. He then grants the plea of a Canaanite woman because of her great faith, heals great multitudes by the Sea of Galilee, and feeds four thousand men, besides women and children, with seven loaves and a few little fish.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 15:1–9 — Tradition and the Commandment of God

Scribes and Pharisees from Jerusalem ask why Jesus’ disciples transgress the tradition of the elders by eating with unwashed hands. Jesus answers that they transgress God’s command to honor father and mother for the sake of their tradition, and He applies Isaiah’s words to them: “These people draw near to Me with their mouth, And honor Me with their lips, But their heart is far from Me” (Matthew 15:8).

2. Matthew 15:10–20 — What Comes from the Heart Defiles

Jesus tells the multitude that what goes into the mouth does not defile a man, but what comes out of the mouth does, and He calls the Pharisees blind leaders of the blind. When Peter asks Him to explain, He teaches that out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, and other sins that defile a person, but to eat with unwashed hands does not defile.

3. Matthew 15:21–28 — The Canaanite Woman’s Great Faith

In the region of Tyre and Sidon a woman of Canaan cries out, “Have mercy on me, O Lord, Son of David!” (Matthew 15:22), because her daughter is severely demon-possessed, and Jesus says He was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Israel and that it is not good to take the children’s bread and throw it to the little dogs. When she answers that even the little dogs eat the crumbs from their masters’ table, Jesus says, “O woman, great is your faith!” (Matthew 15:28), and her daughter is healed from that very hour.

4. Matthew 15:29–31 — Jesus Heals Great Multitudes by the Sea of Galilee

Jesus goes up on a mountain by the Sea of Galilee, and great multitudes bring the lame, blind, mute, maimed, and many others and lay them at His feet, and He heals them. The multitude marvels at what they see, “and they glorified the God of Israel” (Matthew 15:31).

5. Matthew 15:32–39 — Jesus Feeds the Four Thousand

Moved with compassion for the multitude who have continued with Him three days and now have nothing to eat, Jesus takes seven loaves and a few little fish, gives thanks, and feeds four thousand men, besides women and children. Seven large baskets of fragments are left over, and Jesus sends the multitude away and goes by boat to the region of Magdala.
