Matthew 16 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 16 contains the Gospel’s second great turning point. After warning His disciples against the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees, Jesus asks who they say He is, Peter confesses, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God” (Matthew 16:16), and Jesus promises to build His church; then, from that time, Jesus begins to show His disciples that He must suffer, be killed, and be raised, and He calls everyone who would follow Him to deny himself and take up his cross.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 16:1–4 — The Demand for a Sign from Heaven

The Pharisees and Sadducees test Jesus by asking Him to show them a sign from heaven. He answers that they can discern the face of the sky but not the signs of the times, and that no sign will be given to a wicked and adulterous generation except the sign of the prophet Jonah.

2. Matthew 16:5–12 — Beware the Leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees

When Jesus warns His disciples to beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees, they think He is speaking about bread they forgot to bring. He reminds them of the five thousand and the four thousand fed with a few loaves, and they understand that He was warning them against the doctrine of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

3. Matthew 16:13–20 — Peter’s Confession and the Promise of the Church

In the region of Caesarea Philippi Jesus asks His disciples who they say He is, and Peter answers, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God” (Matthew 16:16). Jesus declares that the Father revealed this to Peter, says, “on this rock I will build My church” (Matthew 16:18), promises that the gates of Hades will not prevail against it, gives Peter the keys of the kingdom of heaven, and commands the disciples to tell no one that He is the Christ.

4. Matthew 16:21–23 — Jesus Foretells His Death and Rebukes Peter

From that time Jesus begins to show His disciples that He must go to Jerusalem, suffer many things from the elders, chief priests, and scribes, be killed, and be raised the third day. When Peter rebukes Him, Jesus answers, “Get behind Me, Satan! You are an offense to Me” (Matthew 16:23), for Peter is not mindful of the things of God but the things of men.

5. Matthew 16:24–28 — Taking Up the Cross to Follow Jesus

Jesus tells His disciples that anyone who desires to come after Him must “deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow Me” (Matthew 16:24), for whoever loses his life for His sake will find it. He declares that the Son of Man will come in the glory of His Father with His angels to reward each according to his works, and that some standing there will not taste death till they see the Son of Man coming in His kingdom.
