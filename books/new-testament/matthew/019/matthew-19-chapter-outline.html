Matthew 19 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 19 follows Jesus from Galilee into the region of Judea beyond the Jordan as His teaching on the road to Jerusalem reaches marriage, children, and wealth. He answers the Pharisees’ test about divorce from God’s design in creation, welcomes little children, and calls a rich young man to sell what he has and follow Him, then teaches His disciples that salvation is possible only with God and promises that those who have left everything for His name will receive a hundredfold and inherit eternal life.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 19:1–2 — Jesus Departs from Galilee for Judea

When Jesus finishes these sayings, He leaves Galilee and comes to the region of Judea beyond the Jordan. Great multitudes follow Him, and He heals them there.

2. Matthew 19:3–12 — Marriage, Divorce, and Those Who Remain Unmarried

When the Pharisees test Jesus by asking whether a man may divorce his wife for just any reason, He points them to the Creator who made them male and female and joined the two as one flesh, declaring, “what God has joined together, let not man separate” (Matthew 19:6). He explains that Moses permitted divorce because of the hardness of their hearts, states that whoever divorces his wife except for sexual immorality and marries another commits adultery, and then teaches His disciples about those who remain unmarried for the sake of the kingdom of heaven.

3. Matthew 19:13–15 — Jesus Blesses the Little Children

When little children are brought to Jesus so that He might put His hands on them and pray, the disciples rebuke those who bring them. Jesus says, “Let the little children come to Me, and do not forbid them; for of such is the kingdom of heaven” (Matthew 19:14), and He lays His hands on them.

4. Matthew 19:16–22 — The Rich Young Man

A young man asks Jesus what good thing he must do to have eternal life, and Jesus points him to the One who is good and to the commandments. When the young man says he has kept them all, Jesus tells him to sell what he has, give to the poor, and follow Him, and the young man goes away sorrowful, for he has great possessions.

5. Matthew 19:23–30 — Possible with God and the Reward of Those Who Follow

Jesus tells His disciples that it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God, and when they ask who then can be saved, He answers, “With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible” (Matthew 19:26). When Peter says they have left all to follow Him, Jesus promises that in the regeneration, the coming renewal when the Son of Man sits on the throne of His glory, the Twelve will sit on twelve thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel, and He promises everyone who has left family or lands for His name a hundredfold and eternal life, and adds that many who are first will be last, and the last first.
