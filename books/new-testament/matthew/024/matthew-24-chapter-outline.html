Matthew 24 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 24 begins the fifth of Jesus’ major discourses as He leaves the temple, foretells that not one stone will be left upon another, and answers His disciples’ questions on the Mount of Olives about these things, His coming, and the end of the age. He warns of deception, persecution, and the abomination of desolation, describes the coming of the Son of Man in power and great glory, and calls His disciples to watch and be ready as faithful servants, since no one knows the day or the hour.

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 24:1–2 — Jesus Foretells the Temple’s Destruction

As Jesus departs from the temple, His disciples show Him its buildings. Jesus answers, “Assuredly, I say to you, not one stone shall be left here upon another, that shall not be thrown down” (Matthew 24:2).

2. Matthew 24:3–14 — The Beginning of Sorrows and the Gospel to All Nations

On the Mount of Olives the disciples ask when these things will be and what will be the sign of His coming and of the end of the age, and Jesus warns them not to be deceived by those who come in His name. Wars, famines, pestilences, and earthquakes are the beginning of sorrows, His disciples will be hated and killed for His name’s sake, and “this gospel of the kingdom will be preached in all the world as a witness to all the nations, and then the end will come” (Matthew 24:14).

3. Matthew 24:15–28 — The Abomination of Desolation and the Great Tribulation

When they see the abomination of desolation spoken of by Daniel standing in the holy place, those in Judea are to flee to the mountains without delay, for then there will be great tribulation, such as has not been since the beginning of the world. Jesus warns them not to believe false christs and false prophets who show great signs, for the coming of the Son of Man will be as visible as lightning flashing from the east to the west.

4. Matthew 24:29–31 — The Coming of the Son of Man

Immediately after the tribulation of those days, the sun and moon will be darkened, the stars will fall, and the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven. All the tribes of the earth will mourn and see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory, and He will send His angels with a great sound of a trumpet to gather His elect from the four winds.

5. Matthew 24:32–35 — The Lesson of the Fig Tree

Jesus tells His disciples that as the fig tree’s tender branches and leaves show that summer is near, so when they see all these things they should know that it is near, at the doors. He declares that this generation will by no means pass away till all these things take place, and “Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will by no means pass away” (Matthew 24:35).

6. Matthew 24:36–44 — No One Knows the Day or the Hour

Jesus declares that no one knows the day and hour, not even the angels of heaven, but His Father only, and that the coming of the Son of Man will be like the days of Noah, when people went on with ordinary life until the flood came and took them all away. One will be taken and another left, so His disciples must watch and be ready, “for the Son of Man is coming at an hour you do not expect” (Matthew 24:44).

7. Matthew 24:45–51 — The Faithful Servant and the Evil Servant

Jesus describes the faithful and wise servant whom his master finds giving the household food in due season and makes ruler over all his goods. The evil servant who says, “My master is delaying his coming” (Matthew 24:48), and begins to beat his fellow servants will be surprised by his master’s return, cut in two, and appointed his portion with the hypocrites, where “There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth” (Matthew 24:51).
