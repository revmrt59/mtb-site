Matthew 27:1
Matthew 27:1
NKJV

Matthew 27:1 When morning came, all the chief priests and elders of the people plotted against Jesus to put Him to death.(NKJV)

NLT

Matthew 27:1 Very early in the morning the leading priests and the elders of the people met again to lay plans for putting Jesus to death. (NLT)

Main Idea

When morning comes, all the chief priests and elders of the people plot against Jesus to have Him put to death. The council formalizes the verdict reached during the night.

Verse Explanation

When morning comes, all the chief priests and elders of the people plot against Jesus to have Him put to death. The council formalizes the verdict reached during the night.

“When morning came,”

The night trial is over. At daybreak the council meets again.

“all the chief priests and elders of the people plotted against Jesus to put Him to death.”

They make their formal decision. They are determined to have Jesus executed.

Study Notes

Jewish law did not allow capital trials at night. The morning meeting may have been held to make the verdict official.

Main Idea

The purchase of the field fulfilled what was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet: they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price set on Him by the children of Israel. Verse Explanation

The purchase of the field fulfilled what was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet: they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price set on Him by the children of Israel. Matthew draws together Jeremiah’s themes of the potter and the field with the thirty pieces of silver in Zechariah 11 to show God’s Word fulfilled in this shameful transaction.

“Then was fulfilled what was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet, saying,”

Matthew sees these events as fulfilling Scripture.

“And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the value of Him who was priced, whom they of the children of Israel priced,”

The words combine Zechariah 11:12–13, where thirty pieces of silver are thrown to the potter, with themes from Jeremiah. Jeremiah went to the potter’s house (Jeremiah 18), bought a field (Jeremiah 32), and spoke of judgment in a valley where burials would take place (Jeremiah 19). Matthew names Jeremiah.

Study Notes

In the Hebrew scroll order, Jeremiah stood first among the Latter Prophets.

Matthew 27:60 and laid it in his new tomb which he had hewn out of the rock; and he rolled a large stone against the door of the tomb, and departed.(NKJV)

NLT

Matthew 27:60 He placed it in his own new tomb, which had been carved out of the rock. Then he rolled a great stone across the entrance and left. (NLT)

Main Idea

Joseph lays Jesus’ body in his own new tomb cut from the rock, rolls a large stone against the entrance to close it, and leaves. Jesus is truly dead and buried in a known tomb, as Isaiah 53:9 foretold of a burial with the rich.

Verse Explanation

Joseph lays Jesus’ body in his own new tomb cut from the rock, rolls a large stone against the entrance to close it, and leaves. Jesus is truly dead and buried in a known tomb, as Isaiah 53:9 foretold of a burial with the rich.

“and laid it in his new tomb which he had hewn out of the rock;”

He places the body in his own new tomb, cut into rock.

“and he rolled a large stone against the door of the tomb, and departed.”

He rolls a large stone over the entrance.

Study Notes

Such stones were often disk-shaped and rolled along a groove.

Main Idea

The chief priests and Pharisees go and make the tomb secure, sealing the stone and posting the guard. Every human precaution is taken, setting the stage for God’s power to overturn them all.

Verse Explanation

The chief priests and Pharisees go and make the tomb secure, sealing the stone and posting the guard. Every human precaution is taken, setting the stage for God’s power to overturn them all.

“So they went and made the tomb secure, sealing the stone and setting the guard.”

They seal the stone and post soldiers. No human power could open it.
