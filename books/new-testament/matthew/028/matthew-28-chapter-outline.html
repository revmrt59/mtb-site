Matthew 28 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Matthew 28 brings the Gospel to its close as an angel announces to the women at the empty tomb that Jesus is risen, and the risen Jesus meets them and sends them to His brethren. While the chief priests pay the guards to spread a false report, the eleven disciples meet Jesus on a mountain in Galilee, where He declares that all authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Him, sends them to make disciples of all the nations, and promises, “I am with you always, even to the end of the age” (Matthew 28:20).

Chapter Sections

1. Matthew 28:1–10 — The Empty Tomb and the Risen Jesus

As the first day of the week dawns, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary come to see the tomb, and after a great earthquake an angel who has rolled back the stone tells them, “He is not here; for He is risen, as He said” (Matthew 28:6). As they run to tell His disciples, Jesus meets them, they hold Him by the feet and worship Him, and He sends them to tell His brethren to go to Galilee, where they will see Him.

2. Matthew 28:11–15 — The Guards’ Report and the False Story

Some of the guards report to the chief priests all that has happened, and after consulting with the elders, the chief priests give the soldiers a large sum of money to say that His disciples came at night and stole Him away while they slept. The soldiers take the money and do as they are instructed, and this saying is commonly reported among the Jews until this day.

3. Matthew 28:16–20 — The Great Commission

The eleven disciples go to the mountain in Galilee that Jesus appointed, and when they see Him they worship Him, though some doubt. Jesus declares, “All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth” (Matthew 28:18), commands them to make disciples of all the nations, “baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit” (Matthew 28:19), and teaching them to observe all that He has commanded, and promises to be with them always, even to the end of the age.
