Philemon 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Philemon’s single chapter is the whole letter. After thanking God for Philemon’s love, Paul appeals for love’s sake on behalf of Onesimus, asks Philemon to receive him no longer as a slave but as a beloved brother and as he would receive Paul, offers to bear whatever wrong or debt there may be, and closes with greetings and grace.

Chapter Sections

1. Philemon 1:1–3 — Greeting to Philemon and the Church in His House

Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, writes with Timothy to Philemon, Apphia, Archippus, and the church in Philemon’s house. He greets them with grace and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

2. Philemon 1:4–7 — Thanksgiving for Philemon’s Love and Faith

Paul thanks God for Philemon’s love and faith toward the Lord Jesus and all the saints, and prays that the sharing of his faith may become effective. He has great joy because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed by Philemon.

3. Philemon 1:8–11 — Paul Appeals for Love’s Sake on Behalf of Onesimus

Though Paul could be bold in Christ to command, he appeals for love’s sake as Paul the aged and now a prisoner of Jesus Christ. He appeals for his son Onesimus, begotten in his chains, once unprofitable but now profitable to Philemon and to Paul.

4. Philemon 1:12–16 — No Longer as a Slave but a Beloved Brother

Paul sends Onesimus back as his own heart, though he wished to keep him, because he wants Philemon’s good deed to be voluntary. Perhaps Onesimus departed for a while so that Philemon might receive him forever, no longer as a slave but more than a slave, a beloved brother.

5. Philemon 1:17–20 — Receive Him as You Would Me

If Philemon counts Paul a partner, he is to receive Onesimus as he would receive Paul. Writing with his own hand, Paul promises to repay whatever wrong or debt there may be, reminds Philemon that he owes Paul even his own self, and asks him to refresh his heart in the Lord.

6. Philemon 1:21–22 — Confidence in Philemon and Hope of a Visit

Paul writes confident of Philemon’s obedience, knowing that he will do even more than Paul says. He asks Philemon to prepare a guest room, trusting that through their prayers he will be granted to them.

7. Philemon 1:23–25 — Greetings and Grace

Epaphras, Paul’s fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends greetings, as do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke. Paul closes with the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ upon their spirit.
