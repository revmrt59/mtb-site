Philippians Book Outline

Major Movements

I. Gospel Partnership and Christ Magnified in Paul’s Circumstances (1:1–1:26)

Paul thanks God for the Philippians’ partnership and prays for their growth in discerning love. His imprisonment has advanced the gospel, and his confidence that Christ will be magnified shapes his outlook on both life and death.

II. A Life Worthy of the Gospel Through Christlike Humility (1:27–2:30)

Paul urges the church to stand united amid opposition and reject selfish ambition by sharing the mind of Christ. Jesus’ humiliation and exaltation ground their obedient witness, while Timothy and Epaphroditus provide concrete examples of self-giving service.

III. Knowing Christ and Pressing Toward the Heavenly Goal (3:1–4:1)

Paul warns against confidence in the flesh and counts his former advantages as loss compared with knowing Christ. He pursues the goal of resurrection life and calls the church to follow faithful examples while awaiting the Savior who will transform their bodies.

IV. Steadfast Joy, Shared Peace, and Generous Partnership (4:2–4:23)

Paul addresses a personal division and urges rejoicing, prayer, disciplined thought, and faithful practice. He thanks the church for its gift, explains contentment through Christ’s strength, and closes with confidence in God’s provision and final greetings.
