Titus Book Outline

Revision 1 • October 29, 2026

Major Movements

I. Paul’s Commission and Greeting to Titus (1:1–1:4)

Paul identifies his service in relation to faith, truth, godliness, and the hope of eternal life promised by the truthful God. He greets Titus as his true son in their common faith.

II. Establish Faithful Elders and Silence Corrupt Teachers (1:5–1:16)

Titus must complete the work in Crete by appointing elders whose character and teaching are sound. Such leaders must be able to exhort and refute those whose deceptive teaching and corrupt conduct are upsetting households.

III. Teach the Conduct That Flows from Saving Grace (2:1–3:11)

Paul directs Titus to teach conduct fitting sound doctrine across the church’s different groups and relationships, as the grace that has appeared teaches believers to live godly lives while looking for the blessed hope and glorious appearing of Christ. His redeeming work and the Spirit’s renewing work ground lives of holiness, gentleness, and good works, while foolish disputes and divisive people require firm correction.

IV. Arrange the Continuing Work and Close with Grace (3:12–3:15)

Paul gives travel and support instructions for Titus and other workers. He returns to the need for fruitful good works and concludes with greetings and grace.
