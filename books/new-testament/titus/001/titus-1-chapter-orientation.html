Chapter at a Glance

Purpose & Importance

Purpose

To equip Titus to establish faithful, biblically governed churches by appointing elders of proven character and by confronting teachers whose false teaching and immoral behavior threaten the gospel.
Why It Matters: Leadership and doctrine determine a congregation's spiritual health and public credibility; when leaders embody Christlike character and defend sound teaching, the church both grows in holiness and preserves a credible witness to the surrounding community.
