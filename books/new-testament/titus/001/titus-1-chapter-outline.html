Titus 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Titus 1 opens with Paul’s greeting, rooted in God’s promise of eternal life, and then charges Titus to set the churches of Crete in order by appointing qualified elders who hold fast the faithful word and can convict those who oppose it.

Chapter Sections

1. Titus 1:1–4 — Greeting to Titus

Paul, a bondservant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, writes for the faith of God’s elect and the acknowledgment of the truth which accords with godliness, in hope of eternal life promised before time began. He greets Titus, a true son in their common faith.

2. Titus 1:5–9 — Appointing Qualified Elders

Paul left Titus in Crete to set in order the things that are lacking and appoint elders in every city. An elder must be blameless in his household and as God’s steward, free of self-will and greed, marked by godly character, and holding fast the faithful word so that he can both exhort and convict.

3. Titus 1:10–16 — Rebuking False Teachers in Crete

Many insubordinate talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision, are subverting whole households for dishonest gain. Titus must rebuke them sharply so that they may be sound in the faith, for they profess to know God but deny Him by their works.
