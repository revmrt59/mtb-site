Summary:

MAINTAIN - ( proistemi , Strong’s [4291]) Summary: To stand at the head of, to preside over, or to practice. It suggests a leadership role or a diligent devotion to an activity, essentially "championing" a cause or activity as a primary occupation.

Full Study:

1. Greek Roots / Derivation Derived from the preposition pro, meaning "before" or "in front of," and the verb histemi, meaning "to stand." It literally translates to "to stand before" or "to stand at the head of."

2. Meaning / Lexical Definition To set over, to preside, to rule, to govern, or to manage. In the context of activities or professions, it means to practice, to follow, or to be occupied with. It carries the idea of being a protector or a champion of something, standing in front of it to promote and preserve it.

3. Usage in Greek Culture & Word Pictures - The Household Manager: In secular Greek, it described the master of a house who "stood before" his family and servants to provide direction, protection, and order. - The Military Commander: It pictured a general who stands at the front of his troops to lead them into battle, representing visible leadership and initiative. - The Professional Practitioner: It was used for someone who "professed" a trade or craft, standing at the head of their business to ensure its success and quality.

4. Theological Implications The word is frequently used in the New Testament for leadership within the church, emphasizing that leaders must "stand before" the congregation with diligence. It implies that spiritual authority is not just a title but a functional role of managing and caring for the people of God. When applied to behavior, it suggests that a believer's commitment to "good works" should be as diligent as a leader's commitment to their duties.

5. Contextual Nuance in Titus 3:14 In the NKJV of Titus 3:14, Paul writes, "And let our people also learn to maintain (proistastai) good works..." It suggests that Christians should not merely "do" good works occasionally, but they should "stand at the head" of them, making them their primary concern and profession. It identifies a proactive stance-not waiting for opportunities but leading the way in doing what is beneficial for others.

6. Key Uses in Other Verses (NKJV) - Romans 12:8: “...he who leads (proistemi), with diligence; he who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.” - 1 Thessalonians 5:12: “...recognize those who labor among you, and are over (proistemi) you in the Lord and admonish you,” - 1 Timothy 3:4: “one who rules (proistemi) his own house well, having his children in submission with all reverence” - 1 Timothy 5:17: “Let the elders who rule (proistemi) well be counted worthy of double honor...” - Titus 3:8: “...that those who have believed in God should be careful to maintain (proistemi) good works.”

7. Other Relevant Insights This word highlights the "entrepreneurial" spirit of the Christian life; a believer is called to be a "promoter" of goodness. It suggests that maintaining good works requires the same level of management and oversight that one would give to a business or a family. By "standing before" good works, the believer becomes a visible evidence of the "faithful saying" of the Gospel, demonstrating its practical value.
