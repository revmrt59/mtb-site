Summary:

ABUNDANTLY - ( plousios , Strong’s [4146]) Summary: Abundantly, copiously, or in great measure. It describes a distribution that is not only sufficient but overflowing, reflecting the vast resources of the Giver.

Full Study:

1. Greek Roots / Derivation Derived from the adjective plousios, which comes from ploutos, meaning "wealth" or "abundance." It describes the manner in which an action is performed-specifically, an action done with the generosity and scale of a wealthy benefactor.

2. Meaning / Lexical Definition Richly, abundantly, or copiously. It signifies a supply that far exceeds the minimum requirement, characterized by a fullness that leaves nothing lacking. In the New Testament, it consistently refers to the lavish nature of God's spiritual and physical provisions.

3. Usage in Greek Culture & Word Pictures - The Wealthy Patron: In secular Greek, it described the way a rich citizen would fund a public festival or a theatrical production, providing everything necessary with excellence and without counting the cost. - The Overflowing Granary: It pictured a storehouse so full that the grain spills out, representing a harvest that is greater than the capacity to contain it. - The Unstinting Supply: It identifies a quality of giving that is not restricted by scarcity but is fueled by an inexhaustible source.

4. Theological Implications This word highlights the character of God as the infinite Source of all good things, who does not give "by measure." It underscores the sufficiency of the Gospel and the work of Christ, providing a salvation that is complete and an empowerment that is more than adequate for every trial. "Richly" serves as a bridge between God's internal nature (kindness and love) and His external actions (pouring out and giving).

5. Contextual Nuance in Titus 3:6 In Titus 3:6, it modifies the verb "poured out" (ekcheo), describing the volume of the Holy Spirit given to the believer. It emphasizes that the transition from the "foolish" past to the "justified" present is supported by a massive infusion of divine presence. This "rich" supply ensures that the believer has everything necessary for life and godliness, leaving no room for spiritual poverty.

6. Key Uses in Other Verses (NKJV) - Colossians 3:16: “Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly (plousios) in all wisdom...” - 1 Timothy 6:17: “...nor trust in uncertain riches but in the living God, who gives us richly (plousios) all things to enjoy.” - 2 Peter 1:11: “for so an entrance will be supplied to you richly (plousios) into the everlasting kingdom...”

7. Other Relevant Insights This word corrects any notion of a "stingy" or "reluctant" God; He gives with a joy that matches His wealth. It implies that the believer should never live in a state of spiritual "bankruptcy," as the supply of the Spirit is constantly available in abundance. The adverbial form focuses on the manner of God's grace, showing that the quality of the gift is matched by the quantity of the delivery.
