1 Chronicles 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 1 opens Chronicles by setting Israel’s ancestry within the history of all humanity. It traces humanity from Adam through Noah’s sons to Abraham, records Abraham’s related families, and then turns to Esau, Seir, and the kings and chiefs of Edom.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Chronicles 1:1–4 — From Adam to Noah and His Sons

The record begins with Adam and traces the line through Seth, Enoch, and Methuselah to Noah, naming his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

2. 1 Chronicles 1:5–23 — The Families of Noah’s Sons

The descendants of Japheth, Ham, and Shem are listed, with brief notices of Nimrod, who began to be a mighty one on the earth, and of Peleg, in whose days the earth was divided.

3. 1 Chronicles 1:24–27 — From Shem to Abraham

The line narrows from Shem through Eber, Peleg, and Terah to Abram, who is Abraham.

4. 1 Chronicles 1:28–34 — The Families of Abraham

Abraham’s sons Isaac and Ishmael are named, followed by the sons of Ishmael and the sons born to Keturah. The section closes by returning to Isaac, whose sons were Esau and Israel.

5. 1 Chronicles 1:35–42 — The Families of Esau and Seir

The sons of Esau and his sons Eliphaz and Reuel are listed, followed by the families of Seir.

6. 1 Chronicles 1:43–54 — The Kings and Chiefs of Edom

The kings who reigned in Edom before any king reigned over the children of Israel are listed in succession, followed by the chiefs of Edom.
