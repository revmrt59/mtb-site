1 Chronicles 2 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 2 names all the sons of Israel and then turns to Judah, tracing the line from Judah through Perez and Hezron to Jesse and David within the wider families of Hezron’s sons Jerahmeel and Caleb.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Chronicles 2:1–2 — The Sons of Israel

The twelve sons of Israel are named, from Reuben to Asher.

2. 1 Chronicles 2:3–8 — The Sons of Judah

Judah’s sons are listed, including Er, who was wicked in the sight of the LORD, and Perez and Zerah, born to him by Tamar. The line of Zerah includes Achar, the troubler of Israel.

3. 1 Chronicles 2:9–17 — The Line from Hezron to David

Hezron’s sons are named, and through Ram the line runs to Nahshon, Boaz, Obed, and Jesse. Jesse’s sons are listed with David the seventh, followed by their sisters Zeruiah and Abigail and their sons.

4. 1 Chronicles 2:18–24 — The Family of Caleb the Son of Hezron

Caleb’s descendants include Hur, Uri, and Bezalel. Hezron’s later children and the towns of Jair are also recorded.

5. 1 Chronicles 2:25–41 — The Family of Jerahmeel

Jerahmeel’s descendants are traced, including Sheshan, who had no sons and gave his daughter as wife to Jarha, his Egyptian servant, through whom the line continued.

6. 1 Chronicles 2:42–55 — Further Descendants of Caleb

More of Caleb’s descendants are listed, many named as the fathers of towns in Judah, ending with the families of the scribes who dwelt at Jabez.
