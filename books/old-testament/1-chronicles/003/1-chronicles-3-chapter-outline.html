1 Chronicles 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 3 records David’s household, the royal line from Solomon through Josiah and the last kings of Judah, and the continuation of David’s family after the exile.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Chronicles 3:1–9 — The Sons of David

David’s sons born in Hebron, where he reigned seven years and six months, and those born in Jerusalem, where he reigned thirty-three years, are named, together with Tamar their sister.

2. 1 Chronicles 3:10–16 — The Royal Line from Solomon

The line of kings runs from Solomon’s son Rehoboam to Josiah, followed by the sons of Josiah and the sons of Jehoiakim.

3. 1 Chronicles 3:17–24 — David’s Descendants After the Exile

The family continues through Jeconiah, Shealtiel, Pedaiah, and Zerubbabel to later generations, showing that David’s house survived the loss of the throne.
