1 Chronicles 4 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 4 remembers Judah’s wider households and then Simeon’s families, towns, and later expansion. Within these lists the prayer of Jabez gives a focused example of dependence on the God of Israel, and notices of craftsmen and potters honor the community’s ordinary work.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Chronicles 4:1–8 — Further Families of Judah

The genealogy returns to Judah’s other families, listing the descendants of Shobal and Hur and of Ashhur the father of Tekoa by his two wives.

2. 1 Chronicles 4:9–10 — The Prayer of Jabez

Jabez, more honorable than his brothers and named because his mother bore him in pain, calls on the God of Israel to bless him, enlarge his territory, keep His hand with him, and keep him from evil. God grants him what he requested.

3. 1 Chronicles 4:11–23 — Clans, Craftsmen, and Potters of Judah

The record continues with Judah’s families, including Othniel, the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh, Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh, the house of the linen workers, and the potters who dwelt with the king for his work.

4. 1 Chronicles 4:24–33 — Simeon’s Families and Towns

Simeon’s sons and descendants are listed, with the note that their family did not multiply like the children of Judah. They dwelt at Beersheba, Ziklag, and other cities until the reign of David, together with their villages.

5. 1 Chronicles 4:34–43 — Simeon’s Expansion

Leaders of Simeon’s families seek pasture for their flocks near Gedor and, in the days of Hezekiah, attack and utterly destroy the inhabitants and settle in their place. Five hundred men go to Mount Seir, defeat the rest of the Amalekites who had escaped, and remain there at the time of the record.
