1 Samuel 2 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 2 sets Hannah’s prophetic song beside the corruption of Eli’s sons, contrasting the God who brings low and lifts up with priests who despise His offering. While the boy Samuel grows before the LORD, a man of God announces judgment on Eli’s house.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 2:1–10 — Hannah’s Song of the LORD Who Brings Low and Lifts Up

Hannah rejoices in the LORD’s salvation, His incomparable holiness, and His sovereign reversals, in which the proud are humbled and the poor are raised from the dust. She ends by declaring that He will give strength to His king and “exalt the horn of His anointed.”

2. 1 Samuel 2:11–17 — The Contempt of Eli’s Sons

Elkanah returns home while the child ministers to the LORD before Eli. Eli’s sons, who did not know the LORD, take the people’s sacrifices by force, and their sin is very great before the LORD.

3. 1 Samuel 2:18–21 — Samuel Ministers and Hannah Is Blessed

Samuel ministers before the LORD in a linen ephod, and Hannah brings him a little robe each year. Eli blesses Elkanah and Hannah, the LORD gives her three sons and two daughters, and Samuel grows before the LORD.

4. 1 Samuel 2:22–26 — Eli’s Rebuke Goes Unheeded

The aged Eli hears of all his sons’ evil and rebukes them, but they do not heed their father, because the LORD desired to kill them. In contrast, the child Samuel grows in stature and in favor with the LORD and with men.

5. 1 Samuel 2:27–36 — A Man of God Announces Judgment on Eli’s House

A man of God charges Eli with honoring his sons above the LORD and announces that his house will lose its privileged place, with the death of Hophni and Phinehas on one day as the sign. The LORD will raise up for Himself a faithful priest and build him a sure house.
