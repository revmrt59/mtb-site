1 Samuel 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 3 tells how the LORD calls the boy Samuel in a time when His word was rare, entrusts him with the word of judgment against Eli’s house, and establishes him as a prophet whom all Israel recognizes.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 3:1–10 — The LORD Calls Samuel

In days when the word of the LORD was rare, the LORD calls Samuel three times, and each time he runs to Eli. Eli perceives that the LORD is calling, and when the LORD comes and stands and calls again, Samuel answers, “Speak, for Your servant hears.”

2. 1 Samuel 3:11–14 — The Word Against Eli’s House

The LORD tells Samuel that He will perform all He has spoken against Eli’s house, because Eli knew of his sons’ iniquity and did not restrain them. The iniquity of Eli’s house will not be atoned for by sacrifice or offering forever.

3. 1 Samuel 3:15–18 — Samuel Tells Eli Everything

In the morning Samuel is afraid to tell Eli the vision, but at Eli’s insistence he tells him everything and hides nothing. Eli submits: “It is the LORD. Let Him do what seems good to Him.”

4. 1 Samuel 3:19–21 — Samuel Established as a Prophet of the LORD

The LORD is with Samuel and lets none of his words fall to the ground, and all Israel from Dan to Beersheba knows that he has been established as a prophet of the LORD. The LORD appears again in Shiloh and reveals Himself to Samuel by His word.
