1 Samuel 4 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 4 records the fulfillment of the LORD’s word against Eli’s house: Israel, treating the ark as a means of securing victory, is defeated, the ark is captured, Eli’s sons die, and Eli falls dead at the news. Ichabod’s birth names the catastrophe.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 4:1–11 — Israel Defeated and the Ark of God Captured

After Israel is defeated by the Philistines, the elders send to Shiloh for the ark, accompanied by Hophni and Phinehas, expecting it to secure victory. The Philistines defeat Israel again, capture the ark of God, and kill Eli’s two sons.

2. 1 Samuel 4:12–18 — Eli Hears the News and Dies

A Benjamite runner brings the news to Shiloh, where the aged, blind Eli sits by the wayside, his heart trembling for the ark. When the messenger mentions the ark of God, Eli falls backward and dies, having judged Israel forty years.

3. 1 Samuel 4:19–22 — Ichabod: The Glory Has Departed

Phinehas’s wife goes into labor at the news and dies after giving birth. She names her son Ichabod, saying, “The glory has departed from Israel!” because the ark of God has been captured.
