1 Samuel 5 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 5 shows the LORD humbling Dagon and striking the Philistine cities while the ark is in their land, without any Israelite army, until the Philistines plead for the ark to be sent away.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 5:1–5 — Dagon Falls Before the Ark

The Philistines bring the ark from Ebenezer to Ashdod and set it beside Dagon. On two mornings Dagon lies fallen on its face before the ark, the second time with its head and hands broken off.

2. 1 Samuel 5:6–12 — The Hand of the LORD Heavy on the Philistine Cities

The hand of the LORD is heavy on Ashdod, striking its people with tumors, and the ark is sent on to Gath and then Ekron, bringing deadly destruction wherever it goes. The Philistines gather their lords and plead for the ark of the God of Israel to be sent back to its own place.
