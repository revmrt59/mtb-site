1 Samuel 6 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 6 tells how the Philistines return the ark with a trespass offering and how the LORD directs it home to Beth Shemesh, where joyful welcome turns to fear when He strikes those who looked into it.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 6:1–9 — The Philistines Plan the Ark’s Return

After seven months the Philistine priests and diviners advise sending the ark back with golden images of tumors and rats as a trespass offering. They set a test: if untrained milk cows carry it toward Beth Shemesh, the God of Israel has done this great evil to them.

2. 1 Samuel 6:10–18 — The Ark Comes to Beth Shemesh

The cows go straight along the road to Beth Shemesh, lowing as they go, and the reapers rejoice to see the ark. The Levites set it on the large stone, offerings are made to the LORD, and the narrator records the golden tumors and rats returned by the Philistine cities.

3. 1 Samuel 6:19–21 — Judgment at Beth Shemesh

The LORD strikes the men of Beth Shemesh because they looked into the ark of the LORD. They ask who is able to stand before this holy LORD God and send to Kirjath Jearim to take the ark.
