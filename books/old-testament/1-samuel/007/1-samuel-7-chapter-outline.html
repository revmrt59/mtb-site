1 Samuel 7 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 7 moves from the ark’s long stay at Kirjath Jearim to Israel’s return to the LORD under Samuel, the LORD’s thunderous deliverance at Mizpah, and a summary of Samuel’s lifelong judgeship.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 7:1–2 — The Ark at Kirjath Jearim

The men of Kirjath Jearim bring the ark into the house of Abinadab and consecrate Eleazar his son to keep it. Twenty years pass, and all the house of Israel laments after the LORD.

2. 1 Samuel 7:3–6 — Israel Returns to the LORD at Mizpah

Samuel calls Israel to return to the LORD with all their hearts and put away the foreign gods, and they serve the LORD only. At Mizpah they pour out water before the LORD, fast, and confess their sin, and Samuel judges Israel there.

3. 1 Samuel 7:7–14 — The LORD Thunders and Samuel Raises Ebenezer

When the Philistines come up against Israel, Samuel offers a burnt offering and cries out to the LORD, who thunders against the Philistines and routs them. Samuel sets up a stone named Ebenezer, saying, “Thus far the LORD has helped us,” and the captured cities are restored to Israel.

4. 1 Samuel 7:15–17 — Samuel Judges Israel

Samuel judges Israel all the days of his life, going on a yearly circuit to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah. He always returns to his home at Ramah, where he judges Israel and builds an altar to the LORD.
