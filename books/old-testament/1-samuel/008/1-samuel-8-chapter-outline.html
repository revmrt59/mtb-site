1 Samuel 8 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 8 records Israel’s demand for a king “like all the nations,” which the LORD names as a rejection of His own reign. After a solemn warning about the ways of the king they will have, the LORD directs Samuel to heed their voice.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 8:1–5 — The Elders Demand a King

Samuel’s sons, appointed as judges, turn aside after dishonest gain and pervert justice. The elders of Israel come to Samuel at Ramah and ask for a king to judge them like all the nations.

2. 1 Samuel 8:6–9 — The LORD Names the Demand a Rejection of His Reign

The request displeases Samuel, and he prays to the LORD. The LORD tells him to heed the people, for they have rejected Him from reigning over them, but to forewarn them solemnly about the behavior of the king.

3. 1 Samuel 8:10–18 — The Behavior of the King

Samuel tells the people how the king will take their sons, daughters, fields, harvests, servants, and flocks for his own use. In that day they will cry out because of the king they have chosen, and the LORD will not hear them.

4. 1 Samuel 8:19–22 — The People Insist on a King

The people refuse to obey Samuel’s voice and insist on a king so that they may be like all the nations. The LORD tells Samuel to heed their voice and make them a king, and Samuel sends the people home.
