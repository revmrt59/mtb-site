1 Samuel 9 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 9 tells how the LORD leads Saul, while he searches for his father’s lost donkeys, to Samuel, whom the LORD has already told to anoint this Benjamite as commander over His people. Ordinary events become the setting of God’s appointment.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 9:1–2 — Saul the Son of Kish

Kish, a mighty man of Benjamin, has a son named Saul, choice and handsome, taller than anyone among the people.

2. 1 Samuel 9:3–14 — The Search for the Lost Donkeys

Saul and his servant search far and wide for Kish’s donkeys, and at the servant’s suggestion they go to inquire of the man of God in the land of Zuph. Young women direct them to the seer, and as they enter the city they meet Samuel coming out toward the high place.

3. 1 Samuel 9:15–21 — The LORD Reveals Saul to Samuel

The day before, the LORD had told Samuel that He would send him a man from Benjamin to anoint as commander over His people, to save them from the Philistines. Samuel tells Saul that the donkeys are found and speaks of Israel’s desire, and Saul protests the lowliness of his tribe and family.

4. 1 Samuel 9:22–27 — Saul Honored at Samuel’s Table

Samuel seats Saul at the head of the guests and has the reserved portion set before him, then speaks with him on the housetop. At dawn he sends the servant ahead so that he may make known to Saul the word of God.
