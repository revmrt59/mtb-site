1 Samuel 10 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 10 records Saul’s private anointing, the signs and the Spirit that confirm his calling, and his public selection by lot at Mizpah. Saul is genuinely chosen by God, even as some despise him.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 10:1–8 — Samuel Anoints Saul and Foretells the Signs

Samuel anoints Saul with a flask of oil as commander over the LORD’s inheritance and foretells three signs, ending with the Spirit of the LORD coming upon him. He tells Saul to go down to Gilgal and wait seven days until Samuel comes.

2. 1 Samuel 10:9–16 — Another Heart and the Spirit upon Saul

God gives Saul another heart, the signs come to pass, and the Spirit of God comes upon him so that he prophesies among the prophets, giving rise to a proverb. To his uncle Saul tells of the donkeys but says nothing of the kingdom.

3. 1 Samuel 10:17–27 — Saul Chosen by Lot at Mizpah

At Mizpah Samuel reminds Israel that they have rejected their God by asking for a king, then brings the tribes near until Saul is taken. Found hiding among the equipment, Saul is presented as the one the LORD has chosen; Samuel writes the behavior of royalty in a book, valiant men go with Saul, and some rebels despise him.
