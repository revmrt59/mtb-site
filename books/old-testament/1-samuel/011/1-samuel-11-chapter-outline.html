1 Samuel 11 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 11 shows Saul, empowered by the Spirit of God, delivering Jabesh Gilead from Nahash the Ammonite, sparing those who had despised him, and seeing his kingdom renewed before the LORD at Gilgal.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 11:1–4 — Nahash Threatens Jabesh Gilead

Nahash the Ammonite besieges Jabesh Gilead and will make a covenant only on condition of putting out every man’s right eye. The elders ask for seven days to seek a deliverer, and the news brings the people of Gibeah to weeping.

2. 1 Samuel 11:5–11 — The Spirit of God Comes upon Saul

When Saul hears the news, the Spirit of God comes upon him and his anger is greatly aroused; he summons Israel with the pieces of a yoke of oxen, and the people come out with one consent. Saul’s army strikes the Ammonites, and Jabesh is saved.

3. 1 Samuel 11:12–15 — Saul Spares His Critics and the Kingdom Is Renewed

When the people want to execute those who had despised Saul, he refuses, because the LORD has accomplished salvation in Israel that day. At Samuel’s call they go to Gilgal and make Saul king before the LORD with great rejoicing.
