1 Samuel 12 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 12 records Samuel’s address at the renewal of the kingdom. He affirms his integrity, recounts the LORD’s righteous acts, exposes the evil of the demand for a king, and calls king and people to fear and serve the LORD, promising not to cease praying for them.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 12:1–5 — Samuel’s Integrity Witnessed

Samuel presents the king and calls the people to witness before the LORD and His anointed whether he has taken anything or oppressed anyone. They testify that he has not, and the LORD and His anointed are witnesses.

2. 1 Samuel 12:6–12 — The LORD’s Righteous Acts and Israel’s Demand

Samuel reasons with the people about the LORD who raised up Moses and Aaron and sent deliverers whenever Israel cried out. Yet when Nahash came against them they demanded a king, though the LORD their God was their king.

3. 1 Samuel 12:13–18 — King and People Under the LORD

Here is the king they have chosen and asked for: if both they and their king fear and obey the LORD, it will be well, but if they rebel, His hand will be against them. Samuel calls on the LORD, who sends thunder and rain in the wheat harvest, and the people greatly fear the LORD and Samuel.

4. 1 Samuel 12:19–25 — Samuel Will Not Cease to Pray

The people ask Samuel to pray for them, confessing the evil of asking for a king. Samuel tells them not to fear but to serve the LORD with all their heart, for He will not forsake His people for His great name’s sake; Samuel will not sin by ceasing to pray for them, but if they still do wickedly, both they and their king will be swept away.
