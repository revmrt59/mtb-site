1 Samuel 13 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 13 records Saul’s failure at Gilgal under Philistine pressure: he offers the burnt offering before Samuel arrives and hears that his kingdom will not continue. Israel’s weakness before the Philistines frames the scene.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 13:1–7 — The Philistines Muster and Israel Trembles

After Jonathan attacks the Philistine garrison at Geba, the Philistines gather a vast army at Michmash. The men of Israel hide in caves, thickets, and pits, some cross the Jordan, and the people with Saul at Gilgal follow him trembling.

2. 1 Samuel 13:8–15 — Saul’s Offering at Gilgal

When Samuel does not come within the time set and the people scatter, Saul offers the burnt offering himself. Samuel arrives and declares that Saul has not kept the commandment of the LORD, so his kingdom will not continue, for the LORD has sought for Himself a man after His own heart.

3. 1 Samuel 13:16–23 — Philistine Raiders and an Unarmed Israel

Saul and Jonathan remain at Gibeah of Benjamin while raiders go out from the Philistine camp in three companies. Israel has no blacksmith, and on the day of battle neither sword nor spear is found except with Saul and Jonathan.
