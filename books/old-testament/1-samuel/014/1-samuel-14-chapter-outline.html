1 Samuel 14 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 14 sets Jonathan’s bold faith, through which the LORD saves Israel at Michmash, against Saul’s rash oath, which weakens the army and nearly costs Jonathan his life. It closes with a summary of Saul’s reign, wars, and family.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 14:1–15 — Jonathan’s Attack on the Garrison

Jonathan and his armorbearer cross secretly toward the Philistine garrison, trusting that nothing restrains the LORD from saving by many or by few. They climb up and strike about twenty men, and a trembling from God spreads through the Philistine camp.

2. 1 Samuel 14:16–23 — The LORD Saves Israel That Day

Saul’s watchmen see the Philistine multitude melting away, and after a hurried consultation with the priest, Saul and the people join the battle. Hebrews who had been with the Philistines and Israelites who had hidden rally to the fight, and the LORD saves Israel that day.

3. 1 Samuel 14:24–35 — Saul’s Rash Oath and the People’s Sin

Saul places the people under an oath not to eat until evening, but Jonathan, not having heard it, tastes honey and is refreshed. The famished people later rush on the spoil and eat with the blood, and Saul builds an altar to the LORD.

4. 1 Samuel 14:36–46 — Jonathan Taken by Lot and Rescued

When God does not answer Saul’s inquiry, Saul seeks the sin by lot, and Jonathan is taken. Saul swears that Jonathan must die, but the people rescue him because he has worked with God that day, and Saul stops pursuing the Philistines.

5. 1 Samuel 14:47–52 — Summary of Saul’s Reign and Family

Saul fights against Israel’s enemies on every side and delivers Israel from the Amalekites. His sons, daughters, wife, and commander Abner are named, and there is fierce war with the Philistines all his days.
