1 Samuel 15 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 15 records Saul’s disobedience in the war against Amalek and the LORD’s rejection of him as king. Samuel declares that to obey is better than sacrifice, and the chapter is framed by the LORD’s grief that He made Saul king.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 15:1–9 — The Command Against Amalek and Saul’s Partial Obedience

Samuel delivers the LORD’s command to execute His judgment on Amalek for what it did to Israel on the way up from Egypt. Saul strikes the Amalekites but spares Agag and the best of the sheep, oxen, and all that is good.

2. 1 Samuel 15:10–23 — To Obey Is Better Than Sacrifice

The LORD tells Samuel that He regrets setting up Saul as king, and Samuel cries out to the LORD all night. When Saul insists he has obeyed and blames the people for the spared livestock, Samuel declares that to obey is better than sacrifice and that the LORD has rejected Saul from being king.

3. 1 Samuel 15:24–31 — The Kingdom Torn from Saul

Saul confesses his sin and begs Samuel to return with him; when Saul seizes the edge of his robe and it tears, Samuel declares that the LORD has torn the kingdom from him and given it to a neighbor who is better than he. The Strength of Israel will not lie or relent, and Samuel turns back with Saul, who worships the LORD.

4. 1 Samuel 15:32–35 — Agag’s Death and Samuel’s Grief

Samuel puts Agag to death before the LORD in Gilgal. Samuel returns to Ramah and no longer goes to visit Saul, yet he mourns for him, and the LORD regrets that He made Saul king.
