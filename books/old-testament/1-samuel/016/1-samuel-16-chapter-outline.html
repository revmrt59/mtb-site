1 Samuel 16 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 16 is the book’s turning point. The LORD, who looks at the heart, sends Samuel to anoint David, the Spirit of the LORD comes upon David and departs from Saul, and David enters Saul’s service as the one who brings him relief.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 16:1–5 — Samuel Sent to Bethlehem

The LORD ends Samuel’s mourning for Saul and sends him with a horn of oil to Jesse the Bethlehemite, under the true purpose of a sacrifice. The trembling elders are assured that he comes peaceably, and Jesse and his sons are consecrated.

2. 1 Samuel 16:6–13 — The LORD Looks at the Heart, and David Is Anointed

Samuel is impressed by Eliab, but the LORD tells him not to look at appearance, for the LORD looks at the heart. After seven of Jesse’s sons pass by, the youngest is brought from the sheep, Samuel anoints David among his brothers, and the Spirit of the LORD comes upon him from that day forward.

3. 1 Samuel 16:14–23 — David Comes to Saul’s Court

The Spirit of the LORD departs from Saul, and a distressing spirit from the LORD troubles him. David, commended as a skillful musician with whom the LORD is, becomes Saul’s armorbearer, and his playing brings Saul relief.
