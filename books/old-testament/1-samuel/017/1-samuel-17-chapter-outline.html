1 Samuel 17 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 17 tells how David, trusting the LORD of hosts, defeats the Philistine champion Goliath so that all may know that the battle is the LORD’s. It closes with Saul asking about the young victor’s family.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 17:1–11 — Goliath Defies the Armies of Israel

The armies face each other across the Valley of Elah, and Goliath of Gath, armed in bronze, challenges Israel to send a man to fight him. Saul and all Israel are dismayed and greatly afraid.

2. 1 Samuel 17:12–30 — David Comes to the Battle Line

David, Jesse’s youngest son, brings provisions to his brothers after forty days of Goliath’s challenge and hears the Philistine’s defiance. He asks what will be done for the man who takes away this reproach from Israel, despite Eliab’s angry rebuke.

3. 1 Samuel 17:31–40 — David Volunteers Before Saul

David tells Saul that the LORD who delivered him from the lion and the bear will deliver him from this Philistine. He sets aside Saul’s armor and goes out with his staff, five smooth stones, and his sling.

4. 1 Samuel 17:41–54 — The Battle Is the LORD’s

Goliath disdains David and curses him by his gods, but David comes in the name of the LORD of hosts and declares that the battle is the LORD’s. He strikes the Philistine with a stone, kills him with his own sword, and Israel pursues the fleeing Philistines.

5. 1 Samuel 17:55–58 — Saul Asks Whose Son David Is

Saul asks Abner whose son the young man is, and Abner brings David before the king with the Philistine’s head in his hand. David answers that he is the son of Jesse the Bethlehemite.
