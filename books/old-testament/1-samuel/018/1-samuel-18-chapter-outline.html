1 Samuel 18 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 18 sets the LORD’s presence with David, seen in his success and in the love of Jonathan, Michal, and all Israel, against Saul’s growing fear and jealousy, which turn to open and hidden attempts on David’s life.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 18:1–5 — Jonathan’s Covenant with David

The soul of Jonathan is knit to the soul of David, and he makes a covenant with him, giving him his robe, armor, sword, bow, and belt. David goes out wherever Saul sends him, behaves wisely, and is set over the men of war.

2. 1 Samuel 18:6–9 — The Women’s Song and Saul’s Jealousy

As the army returns, the women sing that Saul has slain his thousands and David his ten thousands. Saul is very angry and eyes David from that day forward.

3. 1 Samuel 18:10–16 — Saul Fears David Because the LORD Is with Him

When the distressing spirit from God comes upon Saul, he twice tries to pin David to the wall with his spear. Afraid because the LORD is with David, Saul makes him captain over a thousand, and all Israel and Judah love David.

4. 1 Samuel 18:17–30 — Saul’s Snare and David’s Marriage to Michal

Saul offers his daughters to David, hoping the Philistines will kill him, and asks a bride-price of a hundred Philistine foreskins. David brings twice that number and marries Michal, and Saul, seeing that the LORD is with David, becomes his enemy continually.
