1 Samuel 19 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 19 traces Saul’s open intent to kill David and the successive ways David is preserved: Jonathan’s intercession, Michal’s help, and the Spirit of God overpowering Saul and his messengers at Naioth.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 19:1–7 — Jonathan Intercedes for David

Saul tells Jonathan and all his servants to kill David, but Jonathan warns David and speaks well of him to his father. Saul swears by the LORD that David will not be killed, and David returns to Saul’s presence.

2. 1 Samuel 19:8–17 — Michal Helps David Escape

After another victory over the Philistines, Saul again tries to pin David to the wall with a spear. When Saul sends messengers to David’s house, Michal lets him down through a window and deceives them with a household image in the bed.

3. 1 Samuel 19:18–24 — The Spirit of God at Naioth

David flees to Samuel at Ramah, and three groups of Saul’s messengers prophesy when they meet the prophets. Saul himself comes, the Spirit of God comes upon him also, and he prophesies and lies before Samuel all that day and all that night.
