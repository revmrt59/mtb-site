1 Samuel 20 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 20 records how Jonathan, bound to David by covenant before the LORD, confirms his father’s intent to kill David and sends him away in peace. The friendship that protects David also asks lasting kindness for Jonathan’s house.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 20:1–11 — David Seeks Jonathan’s Help

David flees from Naioth and asks Jonathan what sin he has committed that Saul seeks his life. They agree that David will be absent from the New Moon feast so that Saul’s response will reveal his intent, and they go out into the field.

2. 1 Samuel 20:12–23 — The Covenant and the Sign of the Arrows

Jonathan swears before the LORD to disclose his father’s intent and asks David never to cut off his kindness from his house. They renew their covenant, and Jonathan arranges the sign of the arrows.

3. 1 Samuel 20:24–34 — Saul’s Anger at the New Moon Feast

When David’s place is empty on the second day, Saul rages at Jonathan and insists that David must die if Jonathan’s kingdom is to be established. Saul hurls a spear at his own son, and Jonathan leaves the table in fierce anger.

4. 1 Samuel 20:35–42 — David and Jonathan Part in Peace

In the field Jonathan shoots the arrows as agreed and sends the lad back to the city. David and Jonathan weep together and part in peace, bound by the oath they have sworn in the name of the LORD.
