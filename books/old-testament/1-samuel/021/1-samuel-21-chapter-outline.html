1 Samuel 21 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 21 follows David’s flight from Saul to Nob, where he deceives Ahimelech the priest and receives holy bread and Goliath’s sword, and to Gath, where he pretends madness before Achish in order to escape.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 21:1–9 — David at Nob

Claiming that the king has sent him on secret business, David asks Ahimelech the priest for food and receives the holy bread, since no common bread is on hand. Doeg the Edomite, one of Saul’s servants, is there that day, and David takes the sword of Goliath.

2. 1 Samuel 21:10–15 — David Feigns Madness at Gath

David flees to Achish king of Gath, where the king’s servants recall the song of David’s ten thousands. Very much afraid, he pretends madness, and Achish sends him away.
