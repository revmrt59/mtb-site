1 Samuel 22 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 22 contrasts David’s gathering of the distressed at Adullam with Saul’s suspicion of conspiracy, which leads to the slaughter of the priests of Nob. Abiathar’s escape brings the surviving priest under David’s protection.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 22:1–5 — David at Adullam and in Moab

David’s family and everyone in distress, in debt, or discontented gather to him at the cave of Adullam, and he becomes captain over about four hundred men. He places his parents with the king of Moab, and the prophet Gad tells him to go into the land of Judah.

2. 1 Samuel 22:6–10 — Saul Accuses His Servants, and Doeg Informs

Sitting under a tamarisk tree at Gibeah, Saul accuses his Benjamite servants of conspiring with David and Jonathan against him. Doeg the Edomite reports that Ahimelech inquired of the LORD for David and gave him provisions and Goliath’s sword.

3. 1 Samuel 22:11–19 — The Slaughter of the Priests of Nob

Ahimelech defends David’s faithfulness and his own ignorance, but Saul condemns him and all his father’s house. When Saul’s guards refuse to strike the priests of the LORD, Doeg kills eighty-five priests and strikes the city of Nob.

4. 1 Samuel 22:20–23 — Abiathar Escapes to David

Abiathar, a son of Ahimelech, escapes and tells David that Saul has killed the LORD’s priests. David acknowledges his part in the deaths of Abiathar’s father’s household and promises Abiathar safety with him.
