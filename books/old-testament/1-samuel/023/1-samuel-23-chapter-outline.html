1 Samuel 23 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 23 shows David inquiring of the LORD and being guided through danger as he rescues Keilah and escapes Saul’s pursuit in the wilderness. Jonathan strengthens his hand in God, and God does not deliver David into Saul’s hand.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 23:1–6 — David Inquires of the LORD and Saves Keilah

When the Philistines fight against Keilah, David twice inquires of the LORD and is told to go. He strikes the Philistines and saves the inhabitants of Keilah, and Abiathar comes down to him with the ephod.

2. 1 Samuel 23:7–14 — The LORD Warns David to Leave Keilah

Saul believes God has delivered David into his hand by shutting him in a walled town. Through the ephod David learns that Saul will come and that the men of Keilah will hand him over, so he departs, and God does not deliver him into Saul’s hand.

3. 1 Samuel 23:15–18 — Jonathan Strengthens David in God

In the Wilderness of Ziph, Jonathan comes to David and strengthens his hand in God, assuring him that he will be king over Israel. The two make a covenant before the LORD.

4. 1 Samuel 23:19–29 — Escape in the Wilderness of Maon

The Ziphites betray David’s hiding place, and Saul and his men close in on David around a mountain in the Wilderness of Maon. A messenger reports a Philistine raid, Saul turns back, and David goes up to the strongholds of En Gedi.
