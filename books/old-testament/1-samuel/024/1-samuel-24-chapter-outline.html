1 Samuel 24 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 24 records David’s refusal to kill Saul in the cave near En Gedi, his public appeal for the LORD to judge between them, and Saul’s tearful acknowledgment that David will surely be king.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 24:1–7 — David Spares Saul in the Cave

Saul pursues David to the Wilderness of En Gedi and goes into the cave where David and his men are hiding. David secretly cuts off a corner of Saul’s robe, but his heart troubles him, and he restrains his men from harming “the LORD’s anointed.”

2. 1 Samuel 24:8–15 — David’s Appeal to Saul

David calls after Saul, bows to the ground, and shows the corner of the robe as proof that he meant no harm. He asks the LORD to judge between them and to plead his cause.

3. 1 Samuel 24:16–22 — Saul Acknowledges David’s Righteousness

Saul weeps and confesses that David is more righteous than he and will surely be king. At Saul’s request David swears not to cut off his descendants, and the two part, Saul going home and David to the stronghold.
