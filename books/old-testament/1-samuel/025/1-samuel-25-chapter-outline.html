1 Samuel 25 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 25 opens with Samuel’s death and tells how Abigail’s wise intervention keeps David from avenging himself on the foolish Nabal. The LORD Himself judges Nabal, and Abigail becomes David’s wife.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 25:1 — The Death of Samuel

Samuel dies, and all Israel gathers to lament him and bury him at his home in Ramah. David goes down to the Wilderness of Paran.

2. 1 Samuel 25:2–13 — Nabal Insults David’s Men

David sends young men to the wealthy Nabal at sheep-shearing to ask for provision in return for protecting his shepherds. Nabal answers with contempt, and David and four hundred men gird on their swords.

3. 1 Samuel 25:14–31 — Abigail Intercedes with David

Warned by a servant of the danger, Abigail hurries to meet David with abundant provisions without telling her husband. Falling at David’s feet, she takes the blame upon herself and urges him not to shed blood or avenge himself, so that he will have no grief of heart when the LORD makes him ruler over Israel.

4. 1 Samuel 25:32–35 — David Blesses the LORD for Abigail’s Counsel

David blesses the LORD who sent Abigail, and blesses her advice for keeping him from bloodshed and from avenging himself with his own hand. He receives her gift and sends her home in peace.

5. 1 Samuel 25:36–44 — The LORD Strikes Nabal, and David Marries Abigail

When Nabal hears what has happened, his heart dies within him, and about ten days later the LORD strikes him and he dies. David takes Abigail as his wife, along with Ahinoam of Jezreel, while Saul has given Michal, David’s wife, to another man.
