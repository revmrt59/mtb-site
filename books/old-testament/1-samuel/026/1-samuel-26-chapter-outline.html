1 Samuel 26 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 26 records David’s second refusal to kill Saul, this time in Saul’s camp at night. David again leaves the LORD’s anointed to the LORD and receives Saul’s blessing without returning to him.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 26:1–5 — Saul Pursues David Again

The Ziphites again report David’s hiding place, and Saul comes down with three thousand chosen men to the hill of Hachilah. David’s spies confirm his coming, and David sees the place where Saul and Abner lie.

2. 1 Samuel 26:6–12 — David Spares Saul in His Camp

David and Abishai go down by night and find Saul asleep, and Abishai asks to strike him. David refuses to stretch out his hand against the LORD’s anointed and takes the spear and the jug of water, for a deep sleep from the LORD has fallen on the camp.

3. 1 Samuel 26:13–20 — David Rebukes Abner and Appeals to Saul

From a distant hilltop David rebukes Abner for failing to guard the king. He asks Saul why he pursues his servant and protests being driven out from the inheritance of the LORD.

4. 1 Samuel 26:21–25 — Saul Confesses and Blesses David

Saul admits that he has sinned and played the fool and invites David to return. David returns the spear, entrusts himself to the LORD who repays righteousness and faithfulness, and the two go their separate ways.
