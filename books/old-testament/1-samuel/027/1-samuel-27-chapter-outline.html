1 Samuel 27 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 27 records David’s decision to escape Saul by settling among the Philistines. Achish gives him Ziklag, and David conceals his raids on other peoples by leaving no one alive and deceiving Achish.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 27:1–7 — David Takes Refuge with Achish

Convinced in his heart that he will one day perish by Saul’s hand, David goes with his six hundred men to Achish of Gath, and Saul seeks him no more. Achish gives him Ziklag, and David lives in Philistine country a full year and four months.

2. 1 Samuel 27:8–12 — David’s Raids and Deception

David raids the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites, leaving neither man nor woman alive to bring news to Gath. He tells Achish that he has raided the South of Judah and its neighbors, and Achish believes that David has made himself abhorred by his people Israel.
