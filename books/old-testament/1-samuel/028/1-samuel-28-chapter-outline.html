1 Samuel 28 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 28 shows Saul, terrified by the Philistine army and receiving no answer from the LORD, seeking a medium at En Dor. There the verdict of his rejection is spoken again, with the word that Israel will fall and that Saul and his sons will die.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 28:1–2 — David Pressed into Achish’s Service

As the Philistines gather their armies against Israel, Achish tells David that he and his men must go out with him to battle. David answers ambiguously, and Achish makes him one of his chief guardians.

2. 1 Samuel 28:3–7 — Saul Afraid and Unanswered

With Samuel dead and the mediums put out of the land, Saul sees the Philistine army at Shunem and is greatly afraid. When the LORD does not answer him by dreams, by Urim, or by the prophets, he tells his servants to find a woman who is a medium.

3. 1 Samuel 28:8–14 — Saul Consults the Medium at En Dor

Saul goes in disguise by night and swears by the LORD that she will not be punished. When the woman sees Samuel, she cries out and recognizes Saul, and she describes an old man coming up, covered with a mantle.

4. 1 Samuel 28:15–19 — Samuel Announces Saul’s Defeat and Death

Samuel tells Saul that the LORD has departed from him and has torn the kingdom from his hand and given it to David, because Saul did not obey the LORD concerning Amalek. The LORD will deliver Israel to the Philistines, and tomorrow Saul and his sons will be with Samuel.

5. 1 Samuel 28:20–25 — Saul Eats and Departs

Saul falls full length on the ground, terrified and without strength. The woman and his servants persuade him to eat, and after she prepares a meal, he rises and goes away that night.
