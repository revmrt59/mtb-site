1 Samuel 29 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 29 returns to David among the Philistines as the Philistine princes refuse to let him go into battle against Israel, releasing him from the battle in which Saul dies.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 29:1–5 — The Philistine Princes Object to David

As the Philistine armies gather at Aphek, the princes object to the Hebrews passing in review with Achish. They fear that David will turn against them in battle and recall the song of his ten thousands.

2. 1 Samuel 29:6–11 — Achish Sends David Back

Achish tells David that he has found no evil in him but that the lords do not favor him. David protests, but Achish sends him away, and David and his men return to the land of the Philistines while the Philistines go up to Jezreel.
