1 Samuel 30 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 30 tells how David, finding Ziklag burned and his people taken captive, “strengthened himself in the LORD his God,” inquires of Him, and recovers all. He then establishes a fair share of the spoil for those who stayed behind and sends gifts to the elders of Judah.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 30:1–6 — Ziklag Burned and David Strengthened in the LORD

David and his men return to find Ziklag burned and their families taken captive by the Amalekites. They weep until they have no more power to weep, and when the people speak of stoning David, he strengthens himself in the LORD his God.

2. 1 Samuel 30:7–15 — David Inquires of the LORD and Pursues

Through Abiathar and the ephod, David asks whether to pursue and is told that he will surely overtake and recover all. Two hundred men too weary to cross the Brook Besor stay behind, and an abandoned Egyptian servant agrees to lead David to the raiders.

3. 1 Samuel 30:16–20 — David Recovers All

When the servant brings David down to the feasting raiders, David attacks the Amalekites from twilight until the evening of the next day. He recovers all that they had carried away, so that nothing is lacking, small or great.

4. 1 Samuel 30:21–25 — A Share for Those Who Stayed Behind

When wicked and worthless men refuse to share the spoil with the two hundred, David declares that what the LORD has given must be shared alike. From that day it becomes a statute and an ordinance for Israel.

5. 1 Samuel 30:26–31 — Gifts to the Elders of Judah

David sends some of the spoil of the LORD’s enemies as a present to the elders of Judah in all the places where he and his men had roamed.
