1 Samuel 31 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Chapter 31 records the death of Saul and his sons on Mount Gilboa, the fulfillment of the LORD’s announced judgment, and the courageous honor shown to their bodies by the men of Jabesh Gilead.

Chapter Sections

1. 1 Samuel 31:1–7 — Saul and His Sons Die on Mount Gilboa

The Philistines defeat Israel on Mount Gilboa and kill Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua. Severely wounded by the archers, Saul falls on his own sword when his armorbearer will not kill him, and the Israelites abandon their cities to the Philistines.

2. 1 Samuel 31:8–13 — The Men of Jabesh Gilead Honor Saul

The Philistines cut off Saul’s head, put his armor in the temple of the Ashtoreths, and fasten his body to the wall of Beth Shan. The valiant men of Jabesh Gilead take down the bodies, burn them, bury the bones under the tamarisk tree at Jabesh, and fast seven days.
