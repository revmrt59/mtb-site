Ecclesiastes 9 Chapter Outline

Revision 1 · November 26, 2026

Chapter Focus

Ecclesiastes 9 opens the movement “One Event for All; Wisdom and Folly” (9:1–10:20). The death that comes alike to righteous and wicked becomes a summons to receive life now as God’s gift, and the chapter then shows that neither skill nor saving wisdom can secure outcomes or honor.

Chapter Sections

1. Ecclesiastes 9:1–6 — One Event for All

The righteous and the wise “are in the hand of God,” yet “One event happens to the righteous and the wicked.” A living dog is better than a dead lion, for “the dead know nothing” and nevermore have a share “In anything done under the sun.”

2. Ecclesiastes 9:7–10 — Receive Life as God’s Gift

“Go, eat your bread with joy,” Solomon says, “For God has already accepted your works.” Wear white garments, live joyfully with the wife you love, and “Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might,” because work ends at the grave.

3. Ecclesiastes 9:11–12 — Time and Chance

“The race is not to the swift, Nor the battle to the strong,” for “time and chance happen to them all.” Like fish taken in a net or birds caught in a snare, people are caught “in an evil time, When it falls suddenly upon them.”

4. Ecclesiastes 9:13–18 — The Poor Wise Man Forgotten

A poor wise man “by his wisdom delivered the city. Yet no one remembered that same poor man.” Solomon concludes that wisdom is better than strength and “better than weapons of war,” though “the poor man’s wisdom is despised” and “one sinner destroys much good.”
