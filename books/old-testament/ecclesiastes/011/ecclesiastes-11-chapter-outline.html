Ecclesiastes 11 Chapter Outline

Revision 1 · November 26, 2026

Chapter Focus

Ecclesiastes 11 opens the movement “Live Well Before the Days of Darkness” (11:1–12:8). It calls for generous and diligent action even though the future cannot be known, then turns to the young: rejoice in the light while it lasts, knowing that God will judge, an address that continues without a break into 12:1.

Chapter Sections

1. Ecclesiastes 11:1–2 — Generosity Amid an Uncertain Future

“Cast your bread upon the waters, For you will find it after many days,” and “Give a serving to seven, and also to eight,” because no one knows what trouble will come upon the earth.

2. Ecclesiastes 11:3–6 — Working Without Knowing the Outcome

Clouds and a fallen tree follow their course, and “He who observes the wind will not sow.” No one knows the way of the wind or how the bones grow in the womb, so a person should sow morning and evening, “For you do not know which will prosper.”

3. Ecclesiastes 11:7–10 — Rejoice in Youth Before God

“Truly the light is sweet,” and one who lives many years should rejoice in them all while remembering “the days of darkness.” The young man is told to rejoice and walk in the ways of his heart, “But know that for all these God will bring you into judgment,” and to put away sorrow and evil, “For childhood and youth are vanity.”
