Ecclesiastes 12 Chapter Outline

Revision 2 · November 26, 2026

Chapter Focus

Ecclesiastes 12 brings the book to its close as the call to the young continues from 11:9–10 with “Remember now your Creator,” set against a picture of old age and death, and the refrain “Vanity of vanities” returns to close Solomon’s search (12:8). A closing frame then commends the Preacher’s truthful words and states the conclusion that governs the whole book: “Fear God and keep His commandments.”

Chapter Sections

1. Ecclesiastes 12:1–5 — Remember Your Creator Before the Days of Decline

“Remember now your Creator in the days of your youth, Before the difficult days come.” A poem of a household in decline pictures the aging body, until “man goes to his eternal home, And the mourners go about the streets.”

2. Ecclesiastes 12:6–7 — The Silver Cord Loosed and the Spirit’s Return

The call to remember sounds again before the silver cord is loosed, the golden bowl broken, the pitcher shattered, and the wheel broken. Then “the dust will return to the earth as it was, And the spirit will return to God who gave it.”

3. Ecclesiastes 12:8 — The Refrain Closes the Search

“Vanity of vanities,” says the Preacher, “All is vanity.” The words that opened the search in 1:2 now close it.

4. Ecclesiastes 12:9–10 — The Preacher’s Truthful Teaching

The closing voice commends the Preacher, who “still taught the people knowledge,” pondered and set in order many proverbs, and wrote “words of truth.”

5. Ecclesiastes 12:11–12 — Wisdom Given by One Shepherd

“The words of the wise are like goads,” and the words of scholars like “well-driven nails, given by one Shepherd.” Addressed as “my son,” the reader is warned that “Of making many books there is no end, and much study is wearisome to the flesh.”

6. Ecclesiastes 12:13–14 — The Conclusion of the Whole Matter

“Fear God and keep His commandments, For this is man’s all.” God “will bring every work into judgment, Including every secret thing, Whether good or evil.”
