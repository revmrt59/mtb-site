Exodus 2 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 2 answers Pharaoh’s decree with the birth and rescue of Moses, then follows him from Pharaoh’s house to a failed attempt to defend his people and a long exile in Midian. The chapter closes by turning from Moses to Israel’s cry under bondage and to God, who hears, remembers His covenant, and sees His people.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 2:1–10 — Moses Is Hidden, Drawn from the River, and Raised as Pharaoh’s Daughter’s Son

A Levite mother hides her son for three months, places him in an ark of bulrushes among the reeds, and his sister watches from a distance. Pharaoh’s daughter has compassion on the Hebrew child, pays his own mother to nurse him, and names him Moses, “Because I drew him out of the water” (Exodus 2:10).

2. Exodus 2:11–15 — Moses Kills an Egyptian and Flees from Pharaoh

Grown to manhood, Moses goes out to his brethren, kills an Egyptian who is beating a Hebrew, and hides him in the sand. The next day a Hebrew rejects him with “Who made you a prince and a judge over us?” (Exodus 2:14), and when Pharaoh seeks to kill him, Moses flees to Midian and sits down by a well.

3. Exodus 2:16–22 — Moses Finds a Home in Midian

At the well Moses defends the seven daughters of the priest of Midian and waters their flock, and their father Reuel welcomes him to eat bread. Moses settles there, marries Zipporah, and names his son Gershom, saying, “I have been a stranger in a foreign land” (Exodus 2:22).

4. Exodus 2:23–25 — God Hears Israel’s Groaning and Remembers His Covenant

The king of Egypt dies, but Israel’s bondage continues, and their cry goes up to God. God hears their groaning, remembers “His covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob” (Exodus 2:24), looks upon the children of Israel, and acknowledges them.
