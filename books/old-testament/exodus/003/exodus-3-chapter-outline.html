Exodus 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 3 brings Moses from the back of the desert into the presence of God at the burning bush, where the LORD reveals that He has seen His people’s suffering and has come down to deliver them through Moses. The chapter moves from the holy encounter to Moses’ commission, God’s promise and name, and detailed instructions that foresee Pharaoh’s resistance and Israel’s departure.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 3:1–6 — The Angel of the LORD Appears in the Burning Bush

While tending Jethro’s flock at Horeb, Moses sees a bush burning with fire that “was not consumed” (Exodus 3:2) and turns aside to look. God calls him by name, declares the ground holy, and identifies Himself as the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and Moses hides his face in fear.

2. Exodus 3:7–12 — The LORD Sends Moses with the Promise of His Presence

The LORD has seen the oppression of His people and heard their cry, and He has come down to deliver them and bring them to a good land, sending Moses to Pharaoh to bring them out. To Moses’ question “Who am I” (Exodus 3:11), God answers, “I will certainly be with you” (Exodus 3:12), and gives the sign that Israel will serve Him on this mountain.

3. Exodus 3:13–15 — God Reveals His Name

When Moses asks what name to give the people, God answers, “I AM WHO I AM” (Exodus 3:14), and sends Moses in the name of the LORD God of their fathers. He declares, “This is My name forever, and this is My memorial to all generations” (Exodus 3:15).

4. Exodus 3:16–22 — God Foretells Israel’s Belief, Pharaoh’s Refusal, and the Departure

Moses is to gather the elders, tell them that the LORD has visited them, and go with them to ask Pharaoh to let Israel make a three days’ journey to sacrifice. God foretells that Pharaoh will refuse until God strikes Egypt with His wonders, and He promises Israel favor so that they will not leave empty-handed.
