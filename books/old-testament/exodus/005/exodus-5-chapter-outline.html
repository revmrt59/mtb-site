Exodus 5 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 5 records Pharaoh’s first answer to the LORD’s demand: he refuses to acknowledge the LORD and makes Israel’s labor harder to crush their call to worship. The chapter moves from the palace to the brickfields and back to Pharaoh, then shows the beaten officers turning on Moses and Moses taking his distress to the LORD.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 5:1–5 — Pharaoh Refuses to Know the LORD

Moses and Aaron deliver the LORD’s word, “Let My people go, that they may hold a feast to Me in the wilderness” (Exodus 5:1), and Pharaoh answers, “Who is the LORD, that I should obey His voice” (Exodus 5:2). When they plead for a three days’ journey to sacrifice, Pharaoh orders them back to work and complains that they make the people rest.

2. Exodus 5:6–14 — Pharaoh Withholds the Straw and Increases the Burden

The same day Pharaoh commands that the people gather their own straw while making the same quota of bricks, calling their desire to sacrifice idleness. The people scatter through Egypt to gather stubble, and the Israelite officers are beaten when the quota is not met.

3. Exodus 5:15–21 — The Officers Appeal to Pharaoh and Blame Moses and Aaron

The officers cry out to Pharaoh, but he repeats, “You are idle! Idle!” (Exodus 5:17), and holds them to the full quota. Coming out from Pharaoh, they meet Moses and Aaron and accuse them of making Israel abhorrent to Pharaoh and putting a sword in his hand to kill them.

4. Exodus 5:22–23 — Moses Brings His Distress to the LORD

Moses returns to the LORD and asks, “Lord, why have You brought trouble on this people? Why is it You have sent me?” (Exodus 5:22). Since he spoke in the LORD’s name, Pharaoh has done evil to the people, and the LORD has not yet delivered them.
