Exodus 7 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 7 completes the LORD’s renewed commission to Moses and Aaron and then opens the confrontation with Pharaoh through signs and judgments. The chapter moves from God’s announced purpose that the Egyptians shall know that He is the LORD, to Aaron’s rod swallowing the magicians’ rods, to the waters of Egypt turned to blood while Pharaoh’s heart remains hard.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 7:1–7 — The LORD Sends Moses and Aaron to Pharaoh

The LORD makes Moses “as God to Pharaoh” with Aaron as his prophet, and He announces that He will harden Pharaoh’s heart and multiply His signs so that “the Egyptians shall know that I am the LORD” (Exodus 7:5). Moses and Aaron do as the LORD commands, at eighty and eighty-three years old.

2. Exodus 7:8–13 — Aaron’s Rod Swallows the Magicians’ Rods

Aaron casts down his rod before Pharaoh and it becomes a serpent, and Egypt’s magicians do the same with their enchantments. “Aaron’s rod swallowed up their rods” (Exodus 7:12), yet Pharaoh’s heart grows hard, as the LORD had said.

3. Exodus 7:14–25 — The LORD Turns the Waters of Egypt to Blood

At the river the LORD tells Pharaoh, “By this you shall know that I am the LORD” (Exodus 7:17), and the waters of Egypt are turned to blood, so that the fish die and the people cannot drink. When the magicians imitate the sign, Pharaoh’s heart grows hard, and he does not heed them or take the judgment to heart; the Egyptians dig around the river for water to drink, and seven days pass after the LORD strikes the river.
