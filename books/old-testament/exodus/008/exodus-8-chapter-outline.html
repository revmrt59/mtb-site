Exodus 8 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 8 continues the plagues with frogs, lice, and swarms of flies, each pressing Pharaoh with the LORD’s demand, “Let My people go, that they may serve Me” (Exodus 8:1). The chapter traces a pattern of temporary relief followed by renewed hardness, while the magicians reach the end of their power and the LORD begins to set His people apart from Egypt.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 8:1–15 — Frogs Cover the Land and Pharaoh Breaks His Word

Frogs come up from the waters and fill Egypt’s houses, and Pharaoh begs Moses to entreat the LORD, promising to let the people go. Moses lets Pharaoh choose the time so that he may know “that there is no one like the LORD our God” (Exodus 8:10), but when Pharaoh sees relief, he hardens his heart.

2. Exodus 8:16–19 — The Dust Becomes Lice and the Magicians Confess God’s Finger

Aaron strikes the dust and it becomes lice on man and beast throughout Egypt, and the magicians cannot reproduce the sign. They tell Pharaoh, “This is the finger of God” (Exodus 8:19), yet Pharaoh’s heart grows hard, as the LORD had said.

3. Exodus 8:20–32 — Swarms of Flies Strike Egypt but Spare Goshen

The LORD sends swarms of flies on Egypt but sets apart the land of Goshen, “that you may know that I am the LORD in the midst of the land” (Exodus 8:22). Pharaoh offers to let Israel sacrifice in the land and then only a short distance away, Moses insists on the three days’ journey God commands, and once the flies are removed, Pharaoh hardens his heart again.
