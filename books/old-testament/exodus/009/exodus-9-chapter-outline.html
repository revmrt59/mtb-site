Exodus 9 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 9 brings three more plagues on Egypt: a pestilence on the livestock, boils on man and beast, and the heaviest hail Egypt had ever seen. As the judgments intensify, the LORD declares His purpose that His name be proclaimed in all the earth, warns Egypt in time to take shelter, and exposes Pharaoh’s confession as words without fear of the LORD.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 9:1–7 — The LORD Strikes Egypt’s Livestock and Spares Israel’s

The LORD announces a severe pestilence on Egypt’s livestock “in the field” (Exodus 9:3) and sends it at the set time, so that “all the livestock of Egypt died” (Exodus 9:6) but not one of Israel’s animals dies. Pharaoh sends to confirm it, yet his heart becomes hard and he does not let the people go.

2. Exodus 9:8–12 — Boils Break Out on Man and Beast

Moses scatters ashes from a furnace toward heaven, and boils break out on man and beast throughout Egypt. The magicians cannot even stand before Moses, and “the LORD hardened the heart of Pharaoh” (Exodus 9:12), just as He had told Moses.

3. Exodus 9:13–21 — The LORD Declares His Purpose and Warns of Hail

The LORD tells Pharaoh that He has raised him up to show His power, “and that My name may be declared in all the earth” (Exodus 9:16), and announces a very heavy hail for the next day. Those of Pharaoh’s servants who fear the word of the LORD bring their servants and livestock into shelter, while those who disregard it leave them in the field.

4. Exodus 9:22–26 — Hail and Fire Fall on Egypt but Not on Goshen

Moses stretches out his rod, and the LORD sends thunder, hail, and fire, striking people, animals, plants, and trees throughout Egypt. “Only in the land of Goshen, where the children of Israel were, there was no hail” (Exodus 9:26).

5. Exodus 9:27–35 — Pharaoh Confesses Sin and Hardens His Heart Again

Pharaoh says, “I have sinned this time. The LORD is righteous, and my people and I are wicked” (Exodus 9:27), and Moses prays so that Pharaoh may know “that the earth is the LORD’s” (Exodus 9:29) while warning that Pharaoh and his servants still do not fear the LORD God. When the storm stops, Pharaoh sins yet more and hardens his heart, he and his servants.
