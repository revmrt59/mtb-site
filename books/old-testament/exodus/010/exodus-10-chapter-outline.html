Exodus 10 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 10 brings the eighth and ninth plagues, locusts and darkness, as the LORD shows His signs so that Israel will tell its children and grandchildren what He has done. Pharaoh keeps offering terms over who may go and what they may take, confesses sin under pressure, and finally threatens Moses with death, and the chapter ends with Moses announcing that he will never see Pharaoh’s face again.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 10:1–6 — The LORD Warns of Locusts and Gives the Signs a Purpose

The LORD sends Moses to Pharaoh so that Israel may tell “in the hearing of your son and your son’s son” (Exodus 10:2) the signs He has done in Egypt. Moses and Aaron ask Pharaoh how long he will refuse to humble himself and warn that locusts will eat everything the hail has left.

2. Exodus 10:7–11 — Pharaoh Offers to Let Only the Men Go

Pharaoh’s servants urge him to let the men go, asking, “Do you not yet know that Egypt is destroyed?” (Exodus 10:7). Moses insists that young and old, sons and daughters, flocks and herds will go to hold a feast to the LORD, but Pharaoh will allow only the men and drives Moses and Aaron from his presence.

3. Exodus 10:12–20 — Locusts Devour Egypt and Pharaoh Confesses Again

An east wind brings locusts that cover the land and eat every plant and fruit the hail had left. Pharaoh hastily confesses sin and asks forgiveness “only this once” (Exodus 10:17), but after a west wind drives the locusts into the Red Sea, the LORD hardens Pharaoh’s heart.

4. Exodus 10:21–23 — Thick Darkness Covers Egypt While Israel Has Light

Moses stretches out his hand, and darkness “which may even be felt” (Exodus 10:21) covers Egypt for three days, so that no one rises from his place. Yet “all the children of Israel had light in their dwellings” (Exodus 10:23).

5. Exodus 10:24–29 — Pharaoh’s Last Offer and Moses’ Announcement of the Final Break

Pharaoh offers to let the little ones go if the flocks and herds stay behind, but Moses rejects the terms, “not a hoof shall be left behind” (Exodus 10:26), since Israel will learn how to serve the LORD only when they arrive. When the LORD hardens Pharaoh’s heart and Pharaoh threatens Moses with death if he sees his face again, Moses answers, “I will never see your face again” (Exodus 10:29).
