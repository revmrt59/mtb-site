Exodus 11 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 11 announces the last plague, the death of every firstborn in Egypt, and with it the certainty that Pharaoh will finally drive Israel out. The chapter moves from the LORD’s private word to Moses, to Moses’ final warning before Pharaoh, to a closing summary of Pharaoh’s refusal that brings the plague narrative to its end.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 11:1–3 — The LORD Announces One More Plague and Gives Israel Favor

The LORD tells Moses that after one more plague Pharaoh will let Israel go and will “surely drive you out of here altogether” (Exodus 11:1), and He tells the people to ask their neighbors for silver and gold. The LORD gives the people favor in Egypt’s sight, and Moses is very great in the land.

2. Exodus 11:4–8 — Moses Warns Pharaoh of the Death of the Firstborn

Moses declares that about midnight the LORD will go out into Egypt and every firstborn will die, from Pharaoh’s firstborn to the firstborn of the servant at the handmill and of the animals, with a great cry throughout the land. Not a dog will move its tongue against Israel, “that you may know that the LORD does make a difference between the Egyptians and Israel” (Exodus 11:7), and Moses leaves Pharaoh in great anger.

3. Exodus 11:9–10 — Pharaoh’s Refusal Closes the Plague Narrative

The LORD tells Moses that Pharaoh will not heed him, so that His wonders may be multiplied in Egypt. Moses and Aaron have done all these wonders, yet the LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart and he did not let Israel go.
