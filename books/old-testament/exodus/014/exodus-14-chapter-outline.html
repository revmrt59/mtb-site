Exodus 14 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 14 tells how the LORD led Israel to camp by the sea, drew Pharaoh into pursuit, and then saved His people by dividing the waters and overthrowing Egypt’s army. The chapter moves from the LORD’s stated purpose to gain honor over Pharaoh, through Israel’s terror and the commands to stand still and go forward, to the crossing on dry ground and Israel’s fear and faith on the far shore.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 14:1–4 — The LORD Directs Israel to Camp by the Sea

The LORD tells Israel to turn back and camp by the sea before Pi Hahiroth, so that Pharaoh will think the wilderness has closed them in. He will harden Pharaoh’s heart to pursue them and gain honor over him, “that the Egyptians may know that I am the LORD” (Exodus 14:4).

2. Exodus 14:5–9 — Pharaoh Pursues Israel with His Chariots

Pharaoh and his servants regret letting Israel go from serving them, and Pharaoh takes six hundred choice chariots and all the chariots of Egypt. The LORD hardens his heart, and the Egyptian army overtakes Israel camped by the sea, though Israel had gone out with boldness.

3. Exodus 14:10–14 — Israel Cries Out and Moses Calls Them to See the LORD’s Salvation

Seeing the Egyptians, the people cry out to the LORD and accuse Moses of bringing them out to die in the wilderness. Moses answers, “Stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD” (Exodus 14:13), and promises, “The LORD will fight for you” (Exodus 14:14).

4. Exodus 14:15–18 — The LORD Commands Israel to Go Forward

The LORD tells Moses, “Tell the children of Israel to go forward” (Exodus 14:15), and commands him to stretch out his rod over the sea and divide it. He will harden the Egyptians’ hearts to follow, and they will know that He is the LORD when He gains honor over Pharaoh’s army.

5. Exodus 14:19–25 — The Angel and the Cloud Protect Israel as the Sea Is Divided

The Angel of God and the pillar of cloud move behind Israel, darkness to Egypt and light to Israel, and the LORD drives back the sea all night so that Israel walks through on dry ground with the waters as a wall on each side. When the Egyptians follow, the LORD troubles their army and takes off their chariot wheels until they cry, “the LORD fights for them against the Egyptians” (Exodus 14:25).

6. Exodus 14:26–31 — The Sea Returns and Israel Fears and Believes the LORD

At the LORD’s command Moses stretches out his hand, the sea returns, and of Pharaoh’s army that followed Israel into the sea “Not so much as one of them remained” (Exodus 14:28). “So the LORD saved Israel that day” (Exodus 14:30), and when Israel sees His great work, they fear the LORD and believe the LORD and His servant Moses.
