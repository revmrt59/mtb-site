Exodus 15 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 15 turns the deliverance at the sea into worship, as Moses and Israel sing of the LORD who has triumphed over Pharaoh and will bring His redeemed people to His holy dwelling, and Miriam leads the women in reply. The chapter then begins the journey from the sea, where bitter water at Marah becomes a test and the LORD reveals Himself as the One who heals His people.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 15:1–10 — The LORD Has Triumphed over Pharaoh at the Sea

Moses and Israel sing, “I will sing to the LORD, For He has triumphed gloriously!” (Exodus 15:1), praising Him as their strength, song, and salvation. The song celebrates how the LORD’s right hand shattered the enemy, how the waters stood up at the blast of His nostrils, and how Pharaoh’s army sank like lead when He blew with His wind.

2. Exodus 15:11–18 — The Incomparable LORD Leads His Redeemed to His Dwelling

The song asks, “Who is like You, O LORD, among the gods?” (Exodus 15:11), and declares that He has led His redeemed people in mercy to His holy habitation while the nations tremble. He will plant them in the place He has made for His own dwelling, and the song ends, “The LORD shall reign forever and ever” (Exodus 15:18).

3. Exodus 15:19–21 — Miriam Leads the Women in Answering Song

The narrator recalls that Pharaoh’s horses and chariots went into the sea while Israel walked on dry land. Miriam the prophetess takes a timbrel, the women follow her with timbrels and dances, and she answers with the song’s opening words.

4. Exodus 15:22–27 — The LORD Heals the Bitter Water at Marah and Brings Israel to Elim

After three days without water, Israel finds only bitter water at Marah and complains, and the LORD shows Moses a tree that makes the water sweet. There He tests them, promises to put none of Egypt’s diseases on them if they heed His voice, for “I am the LORD who heals you” (Exodus 15:26), and then brings them to Elim with its twelve wells and seventy palm trees.
