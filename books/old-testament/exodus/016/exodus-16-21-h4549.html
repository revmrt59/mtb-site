MELTED - ( masas , Strong's H4549 )

Summary:

The Hebrew verb masas describes the physical process of melting, dissolving, or liquefying under heat, and metaphorically conveys human fear, weakness, or despair. In Exodus 16:21, it vividly illustrates the swift transformation of manna as the desert sun intensified, turning the life-sustaining provision into liquid.

Full Study:

1. Word Picture / Background

- The root masas pictures solidity yielding to liquid, whether through the application of natural heat or under duress.

- In the ancient Near Eastern desert, extreme diurnal temperature shifts meant that anything left exposed to the blazing morning sun would rapidly soften and dissipate.

2. Meaning / Lexical Definition

- To melt, dissolve, liquefy, or run.

- Metaphorically, to waste away, grow faint, or lose courage (as hearts melting in fear).

- The basic semantic range moves from literal physical dissolution to emotional and spiritual collapse.

3. Usage and Word Pictures

- Physically, the word describes substances turning to liquid, such as wax near a fire or frost under sunlight (Psalm 58:8, Psalm 68:2).

- Metaphorically, it frequently portrays the moral courage of enemies draining away when facing God's power, as the inhabitants of Canaan felt when Israel approached (Joshua 2:11, Joshua 14:8).

4. Theological Implications

- The melting manna underscores humanity's absolute dependence on God's daily, timely provision.

- What God provides for the present moment cannot be hoarded against His timing; trusting God requires reliance on His mercies new every morning.

5. Contextual Nuance in the Passage

- In Exodus 16:21, the Israelites gathered manna "every morning, every man according to his eating." When the sun became hot, the remaining portion literally melted (masas).

- The natural environment acted as an enforcement mechanism for God's command, ensuring that disobedience (leaving leftovers until morning, as attempted in verse 20) met with immediate natural consequence.

6. Key Uses in Other Verses (NKJV)

- Exodus 16:21: "So they gathered it every morning, every man according to his eating; and when the sun became hot, it melted."

- Joshua 2:11: "And as soon as we heard these things, our hearts melted; neither did there remain any more courage in anyone because of you..."

- Psalm 68:2: "As smoke is driven away, so drive them away; as wax melts before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God."

- Isaiah 13:7: "Therefore all hands will be limp, every man's heart will melt."

7. Other Relevant Insights

- The transience of the manna points forward to the spiritual reality that earthly sustenance is temporary, ultimately directing the reader to Christ as the true, enduring Bread from heaven (John 6:31–33).
