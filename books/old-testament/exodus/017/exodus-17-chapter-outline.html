Exodus 17 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 17 records two events at Rephidim that answer the people’s question, “Is the LORD among us or not?” (Exodus 17:7). The LORD gives water from the rock when Israel quarrels with Moses and tests Him, and He gives victory when Amalek attacks, which Moses commemorates with an altar named The-LORD-Is-My-Banner.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 17:1–7 — Water from the Rock at Massah and Meribah

At Rephidim there is no water, and the people contend with Moses until he cries out that they are almost ready to stone him. The LORD stands before Moses on the rock in Horeb and Moses strikes it, and water comes out, but the place is named Massah and Meribah because the people tempted the LORD, saying, “Is the LORD among us or not?” (Exodus 17:7).

2. Exodus 17:8–13 — Israel Prevails over Amalek While Moses’ Hands Are Held Up

When Amalek attacks, Joshua leads the fight while Moses stands on the hill with the rod of God, and Israel prevails whenever Moses holds up his hand. As Moses’ hands grow heavy, Aaron and Hur hold them steady until sunset, and Joshua defeats Amalek.

3. Exodus 17:14–16 — A Memorial Written and an Altar Named The-LORD-Is-My-Banner

The LORD tells Moses, “Write this for a memorial in the book” (Exodus 17:14), declaring that He will blot out the remembrance of Amalek. Moses builds an altar and names it The-LORD-Is-My-Banner, for the LORD will have war with Amalek from generation to generation.
