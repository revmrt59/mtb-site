Exodus 18 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 18 brings Jethro, Moses’ father-in-law, to the camp at the mountain of God, where he hears what the LORD has done for Israel, confesses that the LORD is greater than all the gods, and worships with Israel’s leaders. The next day he sees Moses judging the people alone from morning until evening and counsels him to share the work with able, God-fearing men, completing the journey from the sea to Sinai.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 18:1–6 — Jethro Brings Moses’ Wife and Sons to the Mountain of God

Jethro hears all that God has done for Moses and Israel and comes to the wilderness with Zipporah and her two sons, Gershom and Eliezer, whose names recall Moses’ exile and God’s help. He sends word that he is coming to Moses where he is encamped at the mountain of God.

2. Exodus 18:7–12 — Jethro Blesses the LORD and Worships with Israel’s Leaders

Moses tells Jethro all that the LORD has done to Pharaoh and how He delivered Israel through every hardship, and Jethro rejoices and says, “Now I know that the LORD is greater than all the gods” (Exodus 18:11). Jethro offers a burnt offering and sacrifices to God, and Aaron and the elders eat bread with him before God.

3. Exodus 18:13–23 — Jethro Counsels Moses to Share the Work of Judging

Seeing the people stand before Moses from morning until evening, Jethro warns, “The thing that you do is not good” (Exodus 18:17), because the burden will wear out both Moses and the people. He advises Moses to stand before God for the people and teach them God’s ways, and to appoint able men who fear God, men of truth who hate covetousness, to judge the ordinary cases, if God so commands.

4. Exodus 18:24–27 — Moses Appoints Judges and Jethro Returns Home

Moses heeds his father-in-law, chooses able men out of all Israel, and makes them rulers of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. They judge the small cases and bring the hard ones to Moses, and Jethro departs to his own land.
