Exodus 19 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 19 brings Israel to the Wilderness of Sinai, where the LORD reminds them that He bore them on eagles’ wings and brought them to Himself, and calls them to be His special treasure, a kingdom of priests, and a holy nation. The people agree to do all He has spoken and consecrate themselves for two days, and on the third day they meet God at the foot of a mountain wrapped in fire, smoke, and the sound of the trumpet, with boundaries that guard His holiness.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 19:1–2 — Israel Arrives and Camps Before the Mountain

In the third month after leaving Egypt, Israel comes from Rephidim to the Wilderness of Sinai. “Israel camped there before the mountain” (Exodus 19:2).

2. Exodus 19:3–8 — The LORD Calls Israel to Be His Treasured Kingdom of Priests

From the mountain the LORD tells Moses to remind Israel, “I bore you on eagles’ wings and brought you to Myself” (Exodus 19:4), and promises that if they obey His voice and keep His covenant, they will be His special treasure, “a kingdom of priests and a holy nation” (Exodus 19:6). The people answer together, “All that the LORD has spoken we will do” (Exodus 19:8), and Moses brings their words back to the LORD.

3. Exodus 19:9–15 — The People Prepare to Meet God on the Third Day

The LORD says He will come in a thick cloud so the people can hear Him speak with Moses, and He commands them to consecrate themselves and wash their clothes today and tomorrow and be ready for the third day. Moses sets bounds around the mountain that no one may touch, and the people prepare.

4. Exodus 19:16–19 — The LORD Descends on Sinai in Fire

On the third day thunder, lightning, a thick cloud, and a very loud trumpet fill the mountain, and the people tremble as Moses brings them out to meet God. Mount Sinai smokes like a furnace and quakes, “because the LORD descended upon it in fire” (Exodus 19:18), and God answers Moses by voice.

5. Exodus 19:20–25 — Moses Is Sent to Warn the People Not to Break Through

The LORD calls Moses to the top of the mountain and sends him back down to warn the people and the priests not to break through to gaze at the LORD, lest He break out against them. Moses goes down with the command to come up again with Aaron, and he speaks to the people.
