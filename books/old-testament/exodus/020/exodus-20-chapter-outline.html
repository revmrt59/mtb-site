Exodus 20 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 20 records God speaking the Ten Commandments to the people He has just brought out of the house of bondage, first concerning their worship of Him and then concerning their life with one another. The people tremble at His voice and ask Moses to speak for God, and the chapter ends with the opening instructions of the Book of the Covenant: no gods of silver or gold, and an altar where the LORD will come and bless.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 20:1–2 — The LORD Who Brought Israel Out Speaks

God speaks all these words and begins by naming who He is and what He has done: “I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage” (Exodus 20:2). The commandments that follow are given to a people already redeemed.

2. Exodus 20:3–11 — The Commandments Concerning Israel’s Worship of the LORD

Israel is to have no other gods before the LORD, make no carved image to bow down to, and not take His name in vain, for He is “a jealous God” who shows mercy to thousands who love Him (Exodus 20:5–6). They are to “Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy” (Exodus 20:8), resting on the seventh day because the LORD rested and hallowed it after creating the heavens and the earth.

3. Exodus 20:12–17 — The Commandments Concerning Life with One’s Neighbor

Israel is to honor father and mother, with the promise of long days in the land. They must not murder, commit adultery, steal, bear false witness against a neighbor, or covet anything that belongs to a neighbor.

4. Exodus 20:18–21 — The People Fear and Moses Draws Near the Thick Darkness

Seeing the thunder, lightning, trumpet, and smoke, the people tremble, stand far off, and ask Moses to speak to them instead of God. Moses tells them, “Do not fear; for God has come to test you, and that His fear may be before you, so that you may not sin” (Exodus 20:20), and he draws near the thick darkness where God is.

5. Exodus 20:22–26 — No Gods of Silver or Gold, and an Altar for the LORD

The LORD reminds Israel that He has spoken with them from heaven and forbids them to make gods of silver or gold to be with Him. He directs them to build an altar of earth or uncut stone without steps and promises, “In every place where I record My name I will come to you, and I will bless you” (Exodus 20:24).
