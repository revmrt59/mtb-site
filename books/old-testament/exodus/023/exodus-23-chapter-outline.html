Exodus 23 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 23 completes the Book of the Covenant with laws for honest justice and kindness, rhythms of rest for land and workers, and three annual feasts at which Israel appears before the LORD. It closes with the LORD’s promise to send His Angel before Israel and to drive out the nations little by little, with a warning to make no covenant with their gods.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 23:1–9 — Honest Justice and Kindness Even to an Enemy

Israel must not spread false reports, follow a crowd to do evil, pervert justice for rich or poor, or take a bribe, “for a bribe blinds the discerning” (Exodus 23:8). They must return an enemy’s straying animal, help the donkey of one who hates them, and not oppress the stranger, for they know the heart of a stranger.

2. Exodus 23:10–13 — Rest for the Land, Workers, and Animals

Every seventh year the land is to lie fallow so that the poor may eat, and every seventh day Israel is to rest so that animals, the servant’s son, and the stranger may be refreshed. Israel is to be careful in all the LORD has said and not even mention the names of other gods.

3. Exodus 23:14–19 — Three Feasts and the Firstfruits of the Land

Three times a year Israel is to keep a feast to the LORD: Unleavened Bread, Harvest, and Ingathering, and no one is to appear before Him empty. They are to bring the first of their firstfruits into the house of the LORD and not boil a young goat in its mother’s milk.

4. Exodus 23:20–26 — The Angel Who Bears the LORD’s Name Goes Before Israel

The LORD sends an Angel before Israel to keep them in the way and bring them to the place He has prepared, and they must obey His voice, “for My name is in Him” (Exodus 23:21). If they obey the Angel’s voice and do all that the LORD speaks, He will be an enemy to their enemies; they must not bow down to the gods of the land but overthrow them, and as they serve the LORD He will bless their bread and water and take sickness away.

5. Exodus 23:27–33 — The LORD Will Drive Out the Nations Little by Little

The LORD will send His fear and hornets before Israel and drive out the land’s inhabitants “little by little” (Exodus 23:30) until Israel increases enough to inherit the land, and He sets its bounds. Israel must make no covenant with these peoples or their gods, for serving their gods “will surely be a snare to you” (Exodus 23:33).
