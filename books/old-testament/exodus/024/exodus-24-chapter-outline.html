Exodus 24 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 24 brings the covenant at Sinai to its sealing: the people pledge to do all the LORD has said, Moses writes and reads the Book of the Covenant, and the covenant is confirmed with blood. Israel’s leaders then see the God of Israel and eat and drink before Him, and Moses goes up into the cloud of glory for forty days and forty nights.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 24:1–2 — The LORD Summons Moses and the Leaders to Worship

The LORD calls Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders to come up and “worship from afar” (Exodus 24:1). Moses alone is to come near the LORD, and the people are not to go up with him.

2. Exodus 24:3–8 — The Covenant Is Confirmed with Blood

Moses tells the people all the LORD’s words, writes them, builds an altar with twelve pillars, and sprinkles half the blood of the offerings on the altar. After he reads the Book of the Covenant and the people pledge obedience, he sprinkles the blood on the people and says, “This is the blood of the covenant which the LORD has made with you” (Exodus 24:8).

3. Exodus 24:9–11 — Israel’s Leaders See God and Eat Before Him

Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders go up “and they saw the God of Israel” (Exodus 24:10), with a pavement like sapphire under His feet. God does not lay His hand on them, and they eat and drink in His presence.

4. Exodus 24:12–18 — Moses Enters the Cloud of Glory on the Mountain

The LORD calls Moses up to receive the tablets of stone, and Moses goes up with Joshua, leaving Aaron and Hur with the elders. The glory of the LORD rests on Sinai like a consuming fire, and on the seventh day Moses enters the cloud and stays on the mountain forty days and forty nights.
