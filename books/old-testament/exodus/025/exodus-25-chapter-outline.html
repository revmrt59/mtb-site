Exodus 25 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 25 begins the LORD’s instructions on the mountain for a sanctuary built from the people’s willing offerings, “that I may dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8). He gives Moses the pattern for the first furnishings, starting with the ark and mercy seat where He will meet with Moses, then the table for the showbread and the golden lampstand.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 25:1–9 — A Sanctuary from Willing Hearts So the LORD May Dwell Among Them

The LORD asks for an offering from everyone “who gives it willingly with his heart” (Exodus 25:2), naming the metals, cloth, skins, wood, oil, spices, and stones to be given. He states His purpose, “let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8), and commands that it be made according to the pattern He shows Moses.

2. Exodus 25:10–22 — The Ark and the Mercy Seat Where God Will Meet with Moses

Israel is to make an ark of acacia wood overlaid with gold, carried by poles that are never removed, and to place the Testimony inside it. Over it they are to set a mercy seat of pure gold with two cherubim of one piece with it, where the LORD promises, “there I will meet with you, and I will speak with you” (Exodus 25:22).

3. Exodus 25:23–30 — The Table for the Showbread

Israel is to make a table of acacia wood overlaid with gold, with rings and poles for carrying it and dishes, pans, pitchers, and bowls of pure gold. On it they are to “set the showbread on the table before Me always” (Exodus 25:30).

4. Exodus 25:31–40 — The Golden Lampstand and the Pattern Shown on the Mountain

Israel is to make a lampstand of one hammered piece of pure gold, with six branches and bowls like almond blossoms, and seven lamps to give light in front of it. Moses is charged, “see to it that you make them according to the pattern which was shown you on the mountain” (Exodus 25:40).
