Chapter Overview

At a Glance

Chapter 25
Main Idea:

On the mountain the LORD asks for a sanctuary built from willing hearts so that He may dwell among His people, and He gives Moses the pattern for the ark and mercy seat where He will meet with them, the table of showbread, and the golden lampstand.

Setting:

Moses is in the cloud on Mount Sinai for forty days (24:18). Exodus 25 begins the tabernacle instructions of chapters 25–31.

Chapter Outline:

  • Exodus 25:1–9 — A Sanctuary from Willing Hearts So the LORD May Dwell Among Them

  • Exodus 25:10–22 — The Ark and the Mercy Seat Where God Will Meet with Moses

  • Exodus 25:23–30 — The Table for the Showbread

  • Exodus 25:31–40 — The Golden Lampstand and the Pattern Shown on the Mountain

Key Verse:

“And let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them.” (Exodus 25:8)

Go Deeper

The Heart of the Chapter

The purpose behind every detail that follows is stated in one sentence: “let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them” (25:8). The God who brought Israel out of Egypt wants to live in the middle of their camp. The materials will come from “everyone who gives it willingly with his heart” (25:2), and everything must follow the pattern God shows Moses on the mountain. Hebrews says that pattern was “the copy and shadow of the heavenly things” (Hebrews 8:5).

God begins at the center. The ark, a gold-covered chest, will hold the tablets of His covenant. Over it sits the mercy seat, its gold cover, with two cherubim facing it, wings outstretched. There God makes His most remarkable promise: “there I will meet with you, and I will speak with you from above the mercy seat” (25:22). The holy God provides a place of meeting. Then come the table, with bread kept “before Me always” (25:30), and the lampstand of beaten gold shaped like a flowering almond tree, giving light in the holy place. The tabernacle as a whole points to Jesus. In Him God has made His dwelling with us (John 1:14). He is the bread of life and the light of the world, and through His blood we are welcomed into God’s presence.

Chapter Sections

Exodus 25:1–9 — A Sanctuary from Willing Hearts So the LORD May Dwell Among Them

The LORD asks for an offering from everyone whose heart moves him to give, listing the metals, fabrics, skins, wood, oil, spices, and stones needed. He states His purpose, a sanctuary so that He may dwell among them, built exactly according to the pattern He shows Moses.

Exodus 25:10–22 — The Ark and the Mercy Seat Where God Will Meet with Moses

Israel is to make an ark of acacia wood covered with gold, carried by poles that are never removed, with the Testimony placed inside. On it goes a mercy seat of pure gold with two cherubim, where the LORD promises to meet and speak with Moses.

Exodus 25:23–30 — The Table for the Showbread

Israel is to make a table of acacia wood covered with gold, with poles for carrying and golden dishes and bowls. On it the showbread is to be set before the LORD continually.

Exodus 25:31–40 — The Golden Lampstand and the Pattern Shown on the Mountain

Israel is to make a lampstand hammered from one piece of pure gold, with six branches decorated like almond blossoms and seven lamps to give light. Moses is told to make everything according to the pattern shown him on the mountain.

Why This Chapter Matters

Exodus 25 reveals the goal of the whole exodus: God wants to live among His people. He rescued them so that He could dwell with them, and the rest of Scripture follows that desire to the Word who “dwelt among us” (John 1:14) and the day when “the tabernacle of God is with men” (Revelation 21:3).

The chapter also shows that God sets the terms for meeting Him. The design was His, not Israel’s, and the place of meeting was the mercy seat. Paul describes Christ as the One “whom God set forth as a propitiation by His blood” (Romans 3:25), using a word connected with this mercy seat. In Christ, believers receive what the furnishings picture: access to God, nourishment, and light.

Key Themes to Watch For

God dwelling with His people. The sanctuary exists so that the LORD may live among Israel (25:8).

Willing gifts. The materials come from those whose hearts move them to give (25:2).

The heavenly pattern. Everything is made according to the pattern shown on the mountain (25:9, 40).

A place of meeting. God promises to meet and speak with Moses above the mercy seat (25:22).

Bread and light in God’s presence. The table holds bread before the LORD always, and the lampstand gives light (25:30, 37).

Key Words & Concepts

Sanctuary (25:8). A holy place set apart for God’s presence. Here it is the tabernacle, God’s dwelling in the camp.

Ark (25:10). A wooden chest covered with gold that held the Testimony, the tablets of the covenant.

Mercy seat (25:17). The gold cover of the ark, between the cherubim, where God promised to meet with Moses. It points to Christ, through whose blood we are welcomed before God (Romans 3:25; Hebrews 9:5–14).

Cherubim (25:18). Heavenly beings associated with God’s throne and presence, made of gold at each end of the mercy seat.

Showbread (25:30). Bread set on the table before the LORD continually. It pictures Christ, the bread of life who nourishes His people in fellowship with God (John 6:35).

Lampstand (25:31). A golden stand with seven lamps that lit the holy place. It pictures Jesus, the light of the world (John 8:12).
