Exodus 26:1
Exodus 26:1
NKJV

Exodus 26:1 "Moreover you shall make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine woven linen and blue, purple, and scarlet thread; with artistic designs of cherubim you shall weave them.(NKJV)

NLT

Exodus 26:1 “Make the Tabernacle from ten curtains of finely woven linen. Decorate the curtains with blue, purple, and scarlet thread and with skillfully embroidered cherubim. (NLT)

Main Idea

Israel is to make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine woven linen in blue, purple, and scarlet, woven with designs of cherubim. The innermost covering of God’s dwelling is made of the finest materials.

Verse Explanation

Israel is to make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine woven linen in blue, purple, and scarlet, woven with designs of cherubim. The innermost covering of God’s dwelling is made of the finest materials.

“Moreover you shall make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine woven linen and blue, purple, and scarlet thread;”

“The tabernacle” here means the inner dwelling itself, made of “ten curtains” of the finest linen and colored thread. This is the innermost covering, seen from inside the holy places.

“with artistic designs of cherubim you shall weave them.”

“Cherubim” are woven into the curtains. Priests inside the tent would see heavenly guardians all around them. The innermost covering of God’s dwelling is made of the finest materials.

The veil hangs from the clasps, and the ark of the Testimony is placed behind it, for the veil divides the holy place from the Most Holy. The veil separates God’s innermost dwelling, and Hebrews 10:19–20 later speaks of the way through the veil opened by Christ.

“And you shall hang the veil from the clasps.”

The veil hangs beneath “the clasps” that join the two sets of inner curtains (26:6).

“Then you shall bring the ark of the Testimony in there, behind the veil.”

The ark is hidden from view.

“The veil shall be a divider for you between the holy place and the Most Holy.”

The veil is “a divider” between “the holy place” and “the Most Holy.” It separates God’s innermost dwelling from everything else. Only the high priest could pass through, once a year, and not without blood (Leviticus 16:2, 12–15; Hebrews 9:6–7). When Jesus died, “the veil of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom” (Matthew 27:51). Hebrews 10:19–20 says believers have “boldness to enter the Holiest by the blood of Jesus, by a new and living way which He consecrated for us, through the veil, that is, His flesh.” The way into God’s presence that the veil closed is now open through Christ.

A screen is made for the door of the tabernacle, woven in blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen. The entrance to the tent has its own covering.

“You shall make a screen for the door of the tabernacle,”

“A screen” covers “the door,” the entrance of the tent on the east side. It is distinct from the inner veil.

“woven of blue, purple, and scarlet thread, and fine woven linen, made by a weaver.”

The screen is woven in the same colors but “made by a weaver,” with embroidered work rather than the cherubim design of the veil. The entrance to the tent has its own covering.
