Exodus 26 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 26 gives the pattern for the tabernacle itself: its inner curtains, its tent and coverings, and the gold-covered frame that holds it up. The chapter then describes the veil that divides the holy place from the Most Holy, where the ark and mercy seat will stand, and the screen at the tent’s entrance.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 26:1–6 — The Inner Curtains Joined into One Tabernacle

Ten curtains of fine linen in blue, purple, and scarlet, woven with cherubim, are joined in two sets of five. Fifty gold clasps couple the sets together, “so that it may be one tabernacle” (Exodus 26:6).

2. Exodus 26:7–14 — The Tent of Goats’ Hair and Its Coverings

Eleven curtains of goats’ hair, joined with bronze clasps, form a tent over the tabernacle, with the extra length hanging over the back and sides. Above them go a covering of ram skins dyed red and a covering of badger skins.

3. Exodus 26:15–30 — The Gold-Covered Frame of Boards, Sockets, and Bars

Upright boards of acacia wood stand in silver sockets on the south, north, and west sides, with special boards for the corners, and they are held together by bars, with a middle bar running from end to end. Boards and bars are overlaid with gold, and Moses is to raise the tabernacle “according to its pattern which you were shown on the mountain” (Exodus 26:30).

4. Exodus 26:31–35 — The Veil Divides the Holy Place from the Most Holy

A veil woven with cherubim hangs on four gold-covered pillars, and the ark of the Testimony is placed behind it, for the veil is “a divider for you between the holy place and the Most Holy” (Exodus 26:33). The mercy seat is placed on the ark in the Most Holy, and the table and lampstand stand outside the veil.

5. Exodus 26:36–37 — The Screen for the Door of the Tabernacle

A woven screen of blue, purple, and scarlet thread and fine linen is made for the door of the tabernacle. It hangs on five gold-covered pillars set in bronze sockets.
