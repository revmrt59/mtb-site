Exodus 28:1
Exodus 28:1
NKJV

Exodus 28:1 "Now take Aaron your brother, and his sons with him, from among the children of Israel, that he may minister to Me as priest, Aaron and Aaron's sons: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.(NKJV)

NLT

Exodus 28:1 “Call for your brother, Aaron, and his sons, Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. Set them apart from the rest of the people of Israel so they may minister to me and be my priests. (NLT)

Main Idea

Moses is to bring Aaron and his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar to minister to God as priests. God chooses Aaron’s family for the priesthood.

Verse Explanation

Moses is to bring Aaron and his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar to minister to God as priests. God chooses Aaron’s family for the priesthood.

“Now take Aaron your brother, and his sons with him, from among the children of Israel,”

God chooses “Aaron” and “his sons” from among Israel. The priesthood is God’s appointment, not a role anyone may take (Hebrews 5:4).

“that he may minister to Me as priest, Aaron and Aaron’s sons: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.”

They are set apart to minister to God as priests. A priest served before God on behalf of the people. God chooses Aaron’s family for the priesthood.

Study Notes

Nadab and Abihu died for offering unauthorized fire (Leviticus 10:1–2). Eleazar succeeded Aaron as high priest (Numbers 20:25–28).

Verse Explanation

“So Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel on the breastplate of judgment over his heart” when he enters the holy place, as a continual memorial before the LORD. Aaron carries the people on his heart as well as his shoulders.

“So Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel on the breastplate of judgment over his heart,”

Aaron carries the names “over his heart.” The heart was the center of affection and thought. He bears the people on his shoulders in strength (28:12) and on his heart in love.

“when he goes into the holy place, as a memorial before the LORD continually.”

Whenever Aaron enters the holy place, the names go with him as “a memorial before the LORD continually.” Aaron carries the people on his heart as well as his shoulders. The shoulders and the heart together picture Jesus, our living High Priest, who has the strength to carry His people and the love to keep them close. He sympathizes with our weaknesses (Hebrews 4:15–16), and “He always lives to make intercession for them” (Hebrews 7:25).

They rest in the same place as the names.

“So Aaron shall bear the judgment of the children of Israel over his heart before the LORD continually.”

He carries the people’s need for God’s decisions into His presence.

Study Notes

The Urim and Thummim are mentioned rarely and seem to have fallen out of use after the early monarchy. After the exile, some questions had to wait “till a priest could consult with the Urim and Thummim” (Ezra 2:63).
