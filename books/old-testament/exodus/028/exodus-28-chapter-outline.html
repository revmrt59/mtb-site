Exodus 28 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 28 appoints Aaron and his sons to serve the LORD as priests and describes the holy garments made “for glory and for beauty” (Exodus 28:2). The chapter moves from the ephod and breastplate, where Aaron carries Israel’s names on his shoulders and over his heart, to the robe with its bells, the gold plate engraved HOLINESS TO THE LORD, and the garments for Aaron’s sons.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 28:1–5 — Aaron and His Sons Are Set Apart, and Holy Garments Are Commanded

The LORD tells Moses to take Aaron and his sons to minister to Him as priests and to make holy garments for Aaron “for glory and for beauty” (Exodus 28:2). Skilled artisans whom the LORD has filled with the spirit of wisdom are to make the breastplate, ephod, robe, tunic, turban, and sash.

2. Exodus 28:6–14 — The Ephod and the Names on Aaron’s Shoulders

The ephod is woven of gold, blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen, with two onyx stones set on its shoulder straps, each engraved with six names of the sons of Israel. “So Aaron shall bear their names before the LORD on his two shoulders as a memorial” (Exodus 28:12).

3. Exodus 28:15–30 — The Breastplate of Judgment over Aaron’s Heart

The breastplate holds twelve stones in four rows, each engraved with the name of a tribe, and it is fastened to the ephod with gold chains, rings, and a blue cord. Aaron bears the names of the sons of Israel “over his heart” (Exodus 28:29) when he enters the holy place, and with the Urim and Thummim he bears Israel’s judgment before the LORD continually.

4. Exodus 28:31–35 — The Blue Robe with Bells and Pomegranates

The robe of the ephod is all blue, with a reinforced opening for the head and a hem of pomegranates and golden bells. The sound of the bells is heard when Aaron goes into the holy place and comes out, “that he may not die” (Exodus 28:35).

5. Exodus 28:36–38 — The Gold Plate Engraved HOLINESS TO THE LORD

A plate of pure gold engraved HOLINESS TO THE LORD is fastened to the front of Aaron’s turban. Aaron bears the iniquity of Israel’s holy gifts, “that they may be accepted before the LORD” (Exodus 28:38).

6. Exodus 28:39–43 — Garments for the Priests’ Ministry

Aaron’s tunic, turban, and sash are made, and his sons receive tunics, sashes, and hats for glory and beauty, and all are to be anointed, consecrated, and sanctified as priests. Linen trousers are made for them so that they do not incur iniquity and die when they minister.
