Main Idea

Verse Explanation

To hallow Aaron and his sons for the priesthood, Moses is to take one young bull and two rams without blemish. The consecration begins with the sacrifices God requires.

“And this is what you shall do to them to hallow them for ministering to Me as priests:”

“Take one young bull and two rams without blemish,”

Three animals are needed, all “without blemish.” Each has its own part in the ceremony (29:10–28).
