“a perpetual incense before the LORD throughout your generations.”

It is “a perpetual incense,” rising morning and evening without fail. The incense rises continually before God. The incense pictures prayer. David prayed, “Let my prayer be set before You as incense,” linking it with the evening sacrifice (Psalm 141:2), and Revelation 5:8 describes “golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.” Christ “always lives to make intercession” for His people (Hebrews 7:25).

“nor shall you pour a drink offering on it.”

The altar is reserved for the incense God appoints. Nadab and Abihu died when they offered “profane fire” that God had not commanded (Leviticus 10:1–2).

Main Idea

Verse Explanation

When Israel is counted, every man must give a ransom for himself to the LORD so that no plague comes on them. The census is a solemn act before God.

Study Notes

In 2 Samuel 24 David took a census of Israel, and a plague followed (2 Samuel 24:1–15). That account does not mention this ransom or say that it went unpaid.
