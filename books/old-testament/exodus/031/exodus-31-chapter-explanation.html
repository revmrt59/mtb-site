Main Idea

Israel must keep God’s Sabbaths, “for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.” The Sabbath marks Israel as the people God makes holy.

Verse Explanation

Israel must keep God’s Sabbaths, “for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.” The Sabbath marks Israel as the people God makes holy.

“Speak also to the children of Israel, saying: ‘Surely My Sabbaths you shall keep,”

God calls them “My Sabbaths.” Even while building the sanctuary, Israel must keep them. The work on God’s house does not override God’s rest (35:1–3).

“for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations,”

The Sabbath is “a sign” of the covenant between God and Israel, as the rainbow was for Noah (Genesis 9:12–13) and circumcision for Abraham (Genesis 17:11).

“that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.”

God makes His people holy. The Sabbath marks Israel as the people God makes holy.

Exodus 31:17
Exodus 31:17
NKJV

Exodus 31:17 It is a sign between Me and the children of Israel forever; for in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed.' "(NKJV)

NLT

Exodus 31:17 It is a permanent sign of my covenant with the people of Israel. For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, but on the seventh day he stopped working and was refreshed.’” (NLT)

Main Idea

The Sabbath is a sign forever, for in six days the LORD made the heavens and earth, “and on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed.” The Sabbath is rooted in God’s own rest at creation.

Verse Explanation

The Sabbath is a sign forever, for in six days the LORD made the heavens and earth, “and on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed.” The Sabbath is rooted in God’s own rest at creation.

“It is a sign between Me and the children of Israel forever;”

The Sabbath is a lasting sign between God and Israel.

“for in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed.”

The reason is creation. God “rested and was refreshed.” The word refreshed reads literally took breath. God does not grow tired (Isaiah 40:28). The language describes His delight in His finished work. The Sabbath is rooted in God’s own rest at creation. For believers, this rest is found in Jesus, the Lord of the Sabbath (Mark 2:27–28), who gives rest and makes His people holy (Matthew 11:28–30; Hebrews 4:9–10). Believers are called to a deliberate weekly rhythm of resting with Him, with freedom concerning the day (Romans 14:5–6; Colossians 2:16–17).

Study Notes

The Sabbath was the sign of the covenant between the LORD and Israel, and the death penalty for breaking it (31:14–15) belonged to Israel’s covenant setting as a nation under God.
