Exodus 31 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 31 closes the LORD’s instructions for the tabernacle by naming Bezalel, whom He fills with His Spirit, and Aholiab, whom He appoints to work with him, to carry out everything He has commanded. He then gives the Sabbath as the sign that He sanctifies His people and hands Moses the two tablets of the Testimony written with the finger of God.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 31:1–11 — God Fills Bezalel and Appoints Aholiab for the Work

The LORD calls Bezalel of Judah by name and fills him “with the Spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship” (Exodus 31:3). He appoints Aholiab of Dan with him and puts wisdom in the hearts of all the skilled artisans, so that they may make the tabernacle, its furnishings, the priestly garments, the oil, and the incense, just as He commanded.

2. Exodus 31:12–17 — The Sabbath as the Sign That the LORD Sanctifies Israel

The LORD commands Israel to keep His Sabbaths, “for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you” (Exodus 31:13). Whoever profanes it by working is to be put to death, for the Sabbath is a perpetual covenant rooted in the LORD’s rest after creating the heavens and the earth.

3. Exodus 31:18 — The Tablets Written with the Finger of God

When the LORD finishes speaking with Moses on Mount Sinai, He gives him two tablets of the Testimony. They are tablets of stone, “written with the finger of God” (Exodus 31:18).
