Chapter Overview

At a Glance

Chapter 31
Main Idea:

The LORD closes His instructions for the tabernacle by filling Bezalel with His Spirit and appointing Aholiab and skilled workers to build it, by giving the Sabbath as the sign that He sanctifies His people, and by handing Moses the tablets written with the finger of God.

Setting:

Exodus 31 ends the instructions Moses received on Sinai (24:18–31:18), just before the people below make the golden calf (32:1).

Chapter Outline:

  • Exodus 31:1–11 — God Fills Bezalel and Appoints Aholiab for the Work

  • Exodus 31:12–17 — The Sabbath as the Sign That the LORD Sanctifies Israel

  • Exodus 31:18 — The Tablets Written with the Finger of God

Key Verse:

“Surely My Sabbaths you shall keep, for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.” (Exodus 31:13)

Go Deeper

The Heart of the Chapter

God has given the pattern; now He provides the people to build it. “See, I have called by name Bezalel” (31:2), He says, and He has filled him “with the Spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship” (31:3). The first person in Scripture said to be filled with God’s Spirit is a craftsman. God gives Bezalel skill in metal, stone, and wood, appoints Aholiab to work beside him, and puts wisdom in the hearts of all the skilled workers. Their gifts serve one purpose: to make everything exactly as God commanded for His dwelling.

Then God gives the Sabbath as a sign, “that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you” (31:13). Rest is part of Israel’s identity as God’s holy people, rooted in His own rest after creation, when “on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed” (31:17). Under Israel’s covenant, profaning the Sabbath was a capital offense, a measure of how seriously God took it. Finally the LORD gives Moses two tablets of stone, “written with the finger of God” (31:18), His own covenant words in His own hand.

Chapter Sections

Exodus 31:1–11 — God Fills Bezalel and Appoints Aholiab for the Work

The LORD calls Bezalel of Judah by name and fills him with His Spirit, giving him wisdom, understanding, knowledge, and every kind of skill. He appoints Aholiab of Dan to work with him and gives wisdom to all the skilled workers so they can make the tabernacle, its furnishings, the priests’ garments, the oil, and the incense exactly as commanded.

Exodus 31:12–17 — The Sabbath as the Sign That the LORD Sanctifies Israel

The LORD tells Israel to keep His Sabbaths as a sign between Him and them that He is the LORD who makes them holy. Under Israel’s covenant anyone who profanes the day by working is to be put to death, for the Sabbath is a lasting sign rooted in God’s own rest after creation.

Exodus 31:18 — The Tablets Written with the Finger of God

When the LORD finishes speaking with Moses on Sinai, He gives him the two tablets of the Testimony. They are stone tablets written with the finger of God.

Why This Chapter Matters

Exodus 31 shows that the Holy Spirit equips God’s people for every kind of work He calls them to, including skilled work with their hands. Bezalel’s artistry was a gift of the Spirit, given to serve God’s dwelling. In Christ, believers are being built together into a dwelling of God in the Spirit (Ephesians 2:22).

The chapter also places rest at the close of all the work instructions. The God who equips His people to work also calls them to stop and rest, and in that rest they remember that He is the One who makes them holy. Jesus, Lord of the Sabbath, calls the weary to find rest in Him (Matthew 11:28–30), and believers are called to a deliberate weekly rhythm of ceasing to enjoy and worship Him.

Key Themes to Watch For

Called by name. God personally chooses Bezalel for the work (31:2).

Filled with the Spirit for skill. The Spirit of God gives wisdom and craftsmanship for building His dwelling (31:3–6).

Everything as commanded. The workers make all that God commanded, from the ark to the incense (31:6–11).

The Sabbath sign. The Sabbath marks Israel as the people the LORD sanctifies (31:13–17).

God’s written word. The tablets are written with the finger of God (31:18).

Key Words & Concepts

Bezalel (31:2). A craftsman from Judah whom God filled with His Spirit to lead the building of the tabernacle.

Aholiab (31:6). A craftsman from Dan whom God appointed to work alongside Bezalel.

Sign (31:13). A visible mark of a relationship. The Sabbath showed that Israel belonged to the LORD who made them holy.

Sanctifies (31:13). Makes holy and sets apart for Himself.

Finger of God (31:18). An expression for God’s direct authorship. The tablets carried His own covenant words with His full authority.
