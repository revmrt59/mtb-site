Exodus 32 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 32 records Israel breaking the covenant at the foot of the mountain by worshiping a golden calf while Moses is still on the mountain. The chapter moves between the mountain and the camp: Moses intercedes and the LORD relents, Moses comes down and breaks the tablets, judgment falls, and Moses returns to the LORD to plead for the people’s forgiveness.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 32:1–6 — Aaron Makes the Golden Calf and Proclaims a Feast

When Moses delays, the people ask Aaron to make gods to go before them, and he fashions a molded calf from their golden earrings. The people say, “This is your god, O Israel, that brought you out of the land of Egypt!” (Exodus 32:4), and Aaron builds an altar and proclaims, “Tomorrow is a feast to the LORD” (Exodus 32:5).

2. Exodus 32:7–14 — Moses Intercedes and the LORD Relents

The LORD tells Moses that the people have corrupted themselves and that He will consume this stiff-necked people and make a great nation of Moses. Moses pleads for God’s name among the Egyptians and His oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, and “the LORD relented from the harm which He said He would do to His people” (Exodus 32:14).

3. Exodus 32:15–20 — Moses Breaks the Tablets and Destroys the Calf

Moses comes down with the two tablets, “the work of God” (Exodus 32:16), and when he sees the calf and the dancing, he breaks them at the foot of the mountain. He burns the calf, grinds it to powder, scatters it on the water, and makes Israel drink it.

4. Exodus 32:21–24 — Aaron Excuses Himself

Moses asks Aaron what the people did to him that he brought so great a sin upon them. Aaron blames the people’s evil and claims that he cast their gold into the fire “and this calf came out” (Exodus 32:24).

5. Exodus 32:25–29 — The Levites Answer Moses’ Call and Judgment Falls

Seeing the people unrestrained, Moses stands at the camp’s entrance and calls, “Whoever is on the LORD’s side—come to me!” (Exodus 32:26), and the sons of Levi gather to him. At his command they carry out judgment in the camp, about three thousand men fall, and Moses tells the Levites to consecrate themselves to the LORD.

6. Exodus 32:30–35 — Moses Offers Himself for the People’s Forgiveness

Moses returns to the LORD and prays, “if You will forgive their sin—but if not, I pray, blot me out of Your book which You have written” (Exodus 32:32). The LORD answers that He will blot out whoever has sinned against Him, sends Moses to lead the people with His Angel going before them, and plagues the people because of the calf.
