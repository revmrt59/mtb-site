Exodus 33:1
Exodus 33:1
NKJV

Exodus 33:1 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Depart and go up from here, you and the people whom you have brought out of the land of Egypt, to the land of which I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, "˜To your descendants I will give it.'(NKJV)

NLT

Exodus 33:1 The LORD said to Moses, “Get going, you and the people you brought up from the land of Egypt. Go up to the land I swore to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I told them, ‘I will give this land to your descendants.’ (NLT)

Main Idea

The LORD tells Moses to depart with the people and go up to the land He swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. God’s promise of the land still stands after the calf.

Verse Explanation

The LORD tells Moses to depart with the people and go up to the land He swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. God’s promise of the land still stands after the calf.

“Then the LORD said to Moses, ‘Depart and go up from here, you and the people whom you have brought out of the land of Egypt,”

After the golden calf, God tells Moses to lead the people on. He calls them “the people whom you have brought out,” as in 32:7. God’s distance from the people is felt in the words.

“to the land of which I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, ‘To your descendants I will give it.”

God’s oath to the fathers still stands. Moses had appealed to it (32:13). God’s promise of the land still stands after the calf.

Main Idea

Verse Explanation

Israel is to go up to a land flowing with milk and honey, “for I will not go up in your midst, lest I consume you on the way,” because they are stiff-necked. The land is promised, but God’s own presence is withheld because of their sin.

“Go up to a land flowing with milk and honey;”

The good land is still theirs to enter.

“for I will not go up in your midst, lest I consume you on the way, for you are a stiff-necked people.”

But God will not travel among them. The reason is mercy and judgment together. Because they are “stiff-necked,” God’s holy presence among them would destroy them. The land is promised, but God’s own presence is withheld because of their sin.
