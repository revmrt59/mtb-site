Exodus 33 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 33 turns on one question after the golden calf: will the LORD Himself still go with His people? The LORD offers an Angel to lead Israel into the land but refuses to go in their midst, the people mourn, Moses meets with the LORD at the tent outside the camp, and his pleading leads to the LORD’s promise of His presence and to Moses’ request, “Please, show me Your glory” (Exodus 33:18).

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 33:1–6 — The LORD Will Send an Angel but Will Not Go Among Israel

The LORD tells Moses to lead the people to the promised land and promises His Angel to drive out its inhabitants, “for I will not go up in your midst, lest I consume you on the way” (Exodus 33:3). When the people hear this bad news they mourn, and at the LORD’s command they strip off their ornaments at Mount Horeb.

2. Exodus 33:7–11 — The LORD Speaks with Moses at the Tent Outside the Camp

Moses pitches a tent far outside the camp and calls it the tabernacle of meeting, and the people watch and worship as the pillar of cloud descends at its door. “So the LORD spoke to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend” (Exodus 33:11), and Joshua remains at the tent.

3. Exodus 33:12–17 — Moses Pleads for the LORD’s Presence

Moses asks the LORD, “show me now Your way, that I may know You” (Exodus 33:13), and the LORD answers, “My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest” (Exodus 33:14). Moses insists that only the LORD’s going with them sets Israel apart from all other peoples, and the LORD agrees because Moses has found grace in His sight.

4. Exodus 33:18–23 — Moses Asks to See the LORD’s Glory

Moses asks, “Please, show me Your glory” (Exodus 33:18), and the LORD promises to make all His goodness pass before him and proclaim His name, but says, “no man shall see Me, and live” (Exodus 33:20). He will place Moses in the cleft of the rock and cover him with His hand, and Moses will see His back but not His face.
