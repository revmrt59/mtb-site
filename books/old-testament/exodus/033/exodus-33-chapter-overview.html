Chapter Overview

At a Glance

Chapter 33
Main Idea:

After the golden calf, the LORD offers to send an Angel but refuses to go among His stubborn people, and Moses, who speaks with Him face to face as a friend, pleads until the LORD promises, “My Presence will go with you,” and then asks to see His glory.

Setting:

Exodus 33 follows the golden calf and Moses’ offer to be blotted out for the people (32:1–35). Israel is still camped at Mount Horeb.

Chapter Outline:

  • Exodus 33:1–6 — The LORD Will Send an Angel but Will Not Go Among Israel

  • Exodus 33:7–11 — The LORD Speaks with Moses at the Tent Outside the Camp

  • Exodus 33:12–17 — Moses Pleads for the LORD’s Presence

  • Exodus 33:18–23 — Moses Asks to See the LORD’s Glory

Key Verse:

“And He said, ‘My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.’” (Exodus 33:14)

Go Deeper

The Heart of the Chapter

God tells Moses to lead the people on to the land. He will keep His promise and send His Angel to drive out their enemies, “for I will not go up in your midst, lest I consume you on the way” (33:3). They can have the land without God. The people call this “bad news” (33:4) and mourn, stripping off their ornaments.

Moses keeps meeting with God at a tent pitched far outside the camp, and there “the LORD spoke to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend” (33:11). From that friendship Moses pleads. He wants to know God’s ways “that I may know You” (33:13). God answers, “My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest” (33:14), but Moses presses further: “If Your Presence does not go with us, do not bring us up from here” (33:15). God’s presence is what makes Israel different from every other people. God agrees, because Moses has found grace in His sight.

Then Moses asks for more: “Please, show me Your glory” (33:18). God promises to make all His goodness pass before him and proclaim His name. No one can see His face and live, so He will hide Moses in the cleft of the rock and cover him with His hand.

Chapter Sections

Exodus 33:1–6 — The LORD Will Send an Angel but Will Not Go Among Israel

The LORD tells Moses to lead the people to the promised land and promises to send His Angel ahead of them, but He will not go among them, or He might destroy them on the way. The people mourn at this news and, at the LORD’s command, take off their ornaments at Mount Horeb.

Exodus 33:7–11 — The LORD Speaks with Moses at the Tent Outside the Camp

Moses pitches a tent far outside the camp, and the people watch and worship as the pillar of cloud comes down at its door. There the LORD speaks with Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend, while young Joshua stays at the tent.

Exodus 33:12–17 — Moses Pleads for the LORD’s Presence

Moses asks the LORD to show him His ways so that he may know Him, and the LORD promises that His Presence will go with Moses and give him rest. Moses insists that only God’s going with them sets Israel apart from all other peoples, and the LORD agrees because Moses has found grace in His sight.

Exodus 33:18–23 — Moses Asks to See the LORD’s Glory

Moses asks to see the LORD’s glory, and the LORD promises to make His goodness pass before him and proclaim His name, though no one can see His face and live. He will set Moses in the cleft of the rock and cover him with His hand, and Moses will see His back but not His face.

Why This Chapter Matters

Exodus 33 asks the most important question in the book: what good is the promised land without the Lord Himself? Moses refused to settle for God’s gifts without God’s presence. That presence is what sets God’s people apart, and it is what the rest of Exodus moves toward as the glory fills the tabernacle (40:34).

The chapter also shows the difference between knowing about God and knowing Him. Moses already spoke with God as a friend, and still he longed to know Him more and to see His glory. In Christ, the glory of God is seen in the face of Jesus (2 Corinthians 4:6), and on the mountain of the Transfiguration Moses saw that glory and spoke with Jesus about His coming departure (Luke 9:31).

Key Themes to Watch For

The threatened loss of God’s presence. The LORD offers an Angel but refuses to go among the people (33:1–6).

Friendship with God. The LORD speaks with Moses face to face, as a friend (33:11).

Presence and rest. God promises that His Presence will go with them and give rest (33:14–17).

What sets God’s people apart. Only God’s presence distinguishes Israel from all other peoples (33:16).

Seeking God’s glory. Moses asks to see God’s glory, and God reveals His goodness within limits (33:18–23).

Key Words & Concepts

Stiff-necked (33:3, 5). Stubborn and resistant to God, like an animal that refuses the yoke.

Ornaments (33:4–6). Jewelry and finery. Taking them off showed mourning and repentance.

Tabernacle of meeting (33:7). Here, a tent Moses pitched outside the camp where he met with God, before the tabernacle was built.

My Presence (33:14). God Himself going with His people. Moses refused to go on without Him.

The cleft of the rock (33:22). A crevice where God sheltered Moses and covered him with His hand while His glory passed by.
