Exodus 34 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 34 answers Moses’ plea as the LORD calls him back up the mountain with new tablets, passes before him, and proclaims His name as merciful and gracious, forgiving yet just. The LORD renews the covenant with commands for exclusive worship and Israel’s appointed feasts, the words are written again, and Moses comes down with his face shining from speaking with Him.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 34:1–4 — Moses Cuts New Tablets and Goes Up Sinai

The LORD tells Moses to cut two tablets of stone like the first ones, on which He will write the words that were on the tablets Moses broke. Moses rises early and goes up Mount Sinai alone, carrying the two tablets.

2. Exodus 34:5–9 — The LORD Proclaims His Name

The LORD descends in the cloud and proclaims, “The LORD, the LORD God, merciful and gracious, longsuffering, and abounding in goodness and truth” (Exodus 34:6), forgiving sin yet by no means clearing the guilty. Moses bows and worships and asks the LORD to go among His stiff-necked people, pardon their sin, and take them as His inheritance.

3. Exodus 34:10–17 — The LORD Renews the Covenant and Forbids Rival Worship

The LORD declares, “Behold, I make a covenant” (Exodus 34:10), and promises to do marvels and drive out the nations. Israel must make no covenant with the land’s inhabitants, must tear down their altars, and must make no molded gods, “for the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God” (Exodus 34:14).

4. Exodus 34:18–26 — The Feasts, the Firstborn, and the Sabbath Renewed

The LORD renews the commands for the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the redemption of the firstborn, weekly rest even in plowing and harvest, and the Feasts of Weeks and Ingathering. All Israel’s men are to appear before Him three times a year, with the firstfruits brought to His house.

5. Exodus 34:27–28 — The Words of the Covenant Are Written Again

The LORD tells Moses, “Write these words” (Exodus 34:27), because according to them He has made a covenant with Moses and Israel. Moses stays with the LORD forty days and forty nights without bread or water, and the words of the covenant, the Ten Commandments, are written on the tablets.

6. Exodus 34:29–35 — Moses’ Face Shines and He Veils It

Moses comes down with the tablets, not knowing that the skin of his face shone because he had talked with the LORD, and Aaron and the people are afraid to come near him. He gives them all the LORD’s commandments and then veils his face, removing the veil whenever he goes in to speak with the LORD.
