“These are the words which the LORD has commanded you to do:”

Moses begins with God’s commands. Chapters 35–40 record Israel carrying out what God instructed in chapters 25–31.

Exodus 35:2
Exodus 35:2
NKJV

Exodus 35:2 Work shall be done for six days, but the seventh day shall be a holy day for you, a Sabbath of rest to the LORD. Whoever does any work on it shall be put to death.(NKJV)

NLT

Exodus 35:2 You have six days each week for your ordinary work, but the seventh day must be a Sabbath day of complete rest, a holy day dedicated to the LORD. Anyone who works on that day must be put to death. (NLT)

Main Idea

Work is to be done for six days, but the seventh is “a holy day for you, a Sabbath of rest to the LORD,” and whoever works on it will be put to death. Rest comes before the work on the sanctuary begins.

Verse Explanation

Work is to be done for six days, but the seventh is “a holy day for you, a Sabbath of rest to the LORD,” and whoever works on it will be put to death. Rest comes before the work on the sanctuary begins.

“Work shall be done for six days, but the seventh day shall be a holy day for you, a Sabbath of rest to the LORD.”

The first command is the Sabbath. Before any work on the sanctuary begins, God’s people are reminded to rest (31:12–17). Rest comes before the work.

“Whoever does any work on it shall be put to death.”

The covenant penalty is repeated. In Israel’s setting, breaking the Sabbath was a capital offense, because the Sabbath was the sign that the LORD makes His people holy.

None.

Exodus 35:3
Exodus 35:3
NKJV

Exodus 35:3 You shall kindle no fire throughout your dwellings on the Sabbath day."(NKJV)

NLT

Exodus 35:3 You must not even light a fire in any of your homes on the Sabbath.” (NLT)

Main Idea

Israel must kindle no fire in their dwellings on the Sabbath day. Even the holy work of building must stop for God’s rest.

Verse Explanation

Israel must kindle no fire in their dwellings on the Sabbath day. Even the holy work of building must stop for God’s rest.

“You shall kindle no fire throughout your dwellings on the Sabbath day.”

No fire is to be kindled on the Sabbath. Fire was needed for metalwork and cooking. Even the holy work of building must stop for God’s rest. For believers, the Sabbath points to rest with Jesus, the Lord who gives rest and makes His people holy. They are called to a deliberate weekly rhythm of resting with Him, with freedom about the day (Romans 14:5–6).

Study Notes

The fire prohibition and its penalty (35:2) belong to Israel’s covenant setting.

God has filled them with skill for engraving, design, embroidery in blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen, and weaving. The leaders are equipped to direct every kind of craft.

“He has filled them with skill to do all manner of work of the engraver and the designer and the tapestry maker,”

“in blue, purple, and scarlet thread, and fine linen, and of the weaver—those who do every work and those who design artistic works.”
