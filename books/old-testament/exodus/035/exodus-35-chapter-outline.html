Exodus 35 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 35 begins the building of the tabernacle after the covenant is renewed: Moses first repeats the Sabbath command, then calls for offerings from willing hearts and for skilled workers to make everything the LORD commanded. Men and women respond with gold, cloth, spun yarn, stones, and spices, and Moses announces that the LORD has filled Bezalel with His Spirit and given him and Aholiab skill and the ability to teach.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 35:1–3 — Moses Repeats the Sabbath Command Before the Work Begins

Moses gathers the whole congregation and gives the LORD’s command that the seventh day be “a holy day for you, a Sabbath of rest to the LORD” (Exodus 35:2). Whoever works on it is to be put to death, and no fire is to be kindled in their dwellings on the Sabbath.

2. Exodus 35:4–19 — Moses Calls for Willing Offerings and Skilled Workers

Moses tells the people, “Whoever is of a willing heart, let him bring it as an offering to the LORD” (Exodus 35:5), naming the materials needed. He calls every gifted artisan to come and make all that the LORD commanded, from the tabernacle and its furnishings to the holy garments for the priests.

3. Exodus 35:20–29 — Men and Women Bring Their Gifts with Willing Hearts

Everyone whose heart is stirred and whose spirit is willing brings the LORD’s offering: gold jewelry, cloth, skins, silver, bronze, and wood, while skilled women spin yarn and the rulers bring precious stones, spices, and oil. “The children of Israel brought a freewill offering to the LORD” (Exodus 35:29) for all the work the LORD commanded through Moses.

4. Exodus 35:30–35 — The LORD Fills Bezalel and Aholiab for the Work and for Teaching

Moses announces that the LORD has called Bezalel by name and filled him with the Spirit of God for every kind of skilled work. The LORD has also “put in his heart the ability to teach” (Exodus 35:34), in him and in Aholiab, and filled them with skill for engraving, design, embroidery, and weaving.
