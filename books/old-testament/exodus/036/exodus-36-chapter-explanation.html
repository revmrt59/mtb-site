NLT

Main Idea

Verse Explanation

The skilled artisans make ten curtains of fine linen in blue, purple, and scarlet, woven with cherubim. The building begins as God commanded in Exodus 26.

Study Notes

Exodus 36:8–38 repeats almost word for word the instructions of 26:1–37, now in the past tense. The repetition shows that the willing gifts and the artisans’ skill served exactly what the LORD appointed. The explanations at 26:1–37 apply here.

Exodus 36:35 For the inside of the Tabernacle, Bezalel made a special curtain of finely woven linen. He decorated it with blue, purple, and scarlet thread and with skillfully embroidered cherubim. (NLT)

Main Idea

The veil is made of blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen, worked with cherubim. The veil that will guard the Most Holy is made.

Verse Explanation

The veil is made of blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen, worked with cherubim. The veil that will guard the Most Holy is made.

“And he made a veil of blue, purple, and scarlet thread, and fine woven linen; it was worked with an artistic design of cherubim.”

The veil that will guard the Most Holy Place is made as instructed (26:31). It is distinct from the screen at the door (36:37).
