NLT

Exodus 37:1 Next Bezalel made the Ark of acacia wood — a sacred chest 45 inches long, 27 inches wide, and 27 inches high. (NLT)

Main Idea

Verse Explanation

Bezalel makes the ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide and high. The chief craftsman personally makes the holiest furnishing.

“Then Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood;”

“Bezalel” himself makes the ark. The chief craftsman personally makes the holiest furnishing.

“two and a half cubits was its length, a cubit and a half its width, and a cubit and a half its height.”

The measurements match God’s instructions in 25:10.

Study Notes

Exodus 37:1–29 records the making of the furnishings described in 25:10–40 and 30:1–10, 22–38. The explanations given there apply here. The chapter shows the pattern becoming usable furnishings.
