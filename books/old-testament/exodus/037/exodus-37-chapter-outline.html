Exodus 37 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 37 records Bezalel making the furnishings for the tent according to the pattern given in Exodus 25 and 30: the ark and mercy seat, the table, the lampstand, and the incense altar. The chapter closes with the holy anointing oil and pure incense, completing the furnishings and supplies made here, while the tabernacle itself is set up in Exodus 40.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 37:1–9 — Bezalel Makes the Ark and the Mercy Seat

Bezalel makes the ark of acacia wood overlaid with pure gold, with rings and poles for carrying it. He makes the mercy seat of pure gold with two cherubim of one piece with it, their wings spread above and their faces toward the mercy seat.

2. Exodus 37:10–16 — The Table and Its Utensils

Bezalel makes the table of acacia wood overlaid with gold, with a frame, rings, and poles. He makes its dishes, cups, bowls, and pitchers of pure gold.

3. Exodus 37:17–24 — The Golden Lampstand

The lampstand is made of one hammered piece of pure gold, with six branches and bowls like almond blossoms. Bezalel makes its seven lamps, wick-trimmers, and trays from a talent of pure gold.

4. Exodus 37:25–29 — The Incense Altar, Anointing Oil, and Incense

Bezalel makes the square incense altar of acacia wood overlaid with gold, with its horns, molding, rings, and poles. He also makes “the holy anointing oil and the pure incense of sweet spices” (Exodus 37:29).
