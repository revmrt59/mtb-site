Chapter Overview

At a Glance

Chapter 37
Main Idea:

Bezalel makes the furnishings for God’s dwelling exactly according to the pattern: the ark and mercy seat, the table for the showbread, the golden lampstand, and the altar of incense, together with the holy anointing oil and pure incense.

Setting:

Exodus 37 continues the building of the tabernacle (chapters 35–40). Each item follows the instructions God gave on the mountain in chapters 25 and 30.

Chapter Outline:

  • Exodus 37:1–9 — Bezalel Makes the Ark and the Mercy Seat

  • Exodus 37:10–16 — The Table and Its Utensils

  • Exodus 37:17–24 — The Golden Lampstand

  • Exodus 37:25–29 — The Incense Altar, Anointing Oil, and Incense

Key Verse:

“The cherubim spread out their wings above, and covered the mercy seat with their wings. They faced one another; the faces of the cherubim were toward the mercy seat.” (Exodus 37:9)

Go Deeper

The Heart of the Chapter

Bezalel, the craftsman God filled with His Spirit, now makes the most sacred objects of all. He begins with the ark, a chest of acacia wood covered with pure gold inside and out, with rings and poles for carrying. On it he sets the mercy seat of pure gold, with two cherubim formed from the same piece. “The cherubim spread out their wings above, and covered the mercy seat with their wings” (37:9), their faces turned toward it. This is the place where God promised to meet with His people (25:22).

Next comes the table, also covered in gold, with its golden dishes, cups, bowls, and pitchers. Then the lampstand, hammered from a single talent of pure gold, its six branches shaped like flowering almond branches holding seven lamps. Then the small golden altar where incense will rise before the veil. Finally Bezalel prepares “the holy anointing oil and the pure incense of sweet spices” (37:29). The chapter is a quiet, careful record of obedience. What God described in words on the mountain now exists in gold and wood, ready for the day His glory will fill the tent. Each piece points to Jesus: the mercy seat to the access we have through His blood, the bread to the One who nourishes us, the light to the light of the world, and the incense to His ongoing intercession.

Chapter Sections

Exodus 37:1–9 — Bezalel Makes the Ark and the Mercy Seat

Bezalel makes the ark of acacia wood covered with pure gold, with rings and poles for carrying it. He makes the mercy seat of pure gold with two cherubim of one piece with it, their wings spread above it and their faces turned toward it.

Exodus 37:10–16 — The Table and Its Utensils

Bezalel makes the table of acacia wood covered with gold, with its frame, rings, and carrying poles. He makes its dishes, cups, bowls, and pitchers of pure gold.

Exodus 37:17–24 — The Golden Lampstand

Bezalel hammers the lampstand from a single piece of pure gold, with six branches decorated like almond blossoms. He makes its seven lamps, wick-trimmers, and trays, using a talent of pure gold for the lampstand and all its utensils together.

Exodus 37:25–29 — The Incense Altar, Anointing Oil, and Incense

Bezalel makes the small square altar of incense of acacia wood covered with gold, with its horns, molding, rings, and poles. He also prepares the holy anointing oil and the pure incense of fragrant spices.

Why This Chapter Matters

Exodus 37 shows that God’s design is carried out faithfully, down to the last detail. The furnishings match the instructions because God’s way of meeting His people is not left to human invention. Faithful service often looks like this chapter: careful, patient work that does exactly what God asked.

The furnishings also tell the story of how sinners come to a holy God. The mercy seat, the bread, the light, and the incense all point beyond themselves to Jesus. Through His blood we have access to God (Hebrews 10:19), He is the bread of life and the light of the world (John 6:35; 8:12), and He always lives to intercede for His people (Hebrews 7:25).

Key Themes to Watch For

The pattern carried out. Each furnishing is made as God commanded in chapters 25 and 30 (37:1–29).

The place of meeting. The ark and mercy seat form the center of God’s dwelling (37:1–9).

Bread and light before God. The table and lampstand furnish the holy place (37:10–24).

Incense and holy oil. The incense altar, anointing oil, and pure incense prepare for worship (37:25–29).

Key Words & Concepts

Mercy seat (37:6). The gold cover of the ark, between the cherubim, where God promised to meet with His people.

Cherubim (37:7). Heavenly beings made of gold at the ends of the mercy seat, their wings covering it.

Talent (37:24). A large measure of weight. The lampstand and its tools were made from a talent of pure gold.

The perfumer (37:29). A skilled blender of spices. The anointing oil and incense were made by the perfumer’s art.
