NLT

Main Idea

Verse Explanation

Bezalel makes the bronze laver and its base “from the bronze mirrors of the serving women who assembled at the door of the tabernacle of meeting.” The women’s gift and service become part of the sanctuary.

“He made the laver of bronze and its base of bronze,”

The “laver” for the priests’ washing is made (30:17–21).

“from the bronze mirrors of the serving women who assembled at the door of the tabernacle of meeting.”

The bronze came from “the bronze mirrors” of women who served “at the door of the tabernacle of meeting.” Mirrors of polished bronze were valued possessions. These women gave them for God’s house. The text records their service and their gift but gives the mirrors no further meaning. The laver’s purpose is washing, a picture of the cleansing Christ gives (Ephesians 5:25–27).

Study Notes

1 Samuel 2:22 also mentions women who served at the door of the tabernacle of meeting. The text does not explain what their service involved.
