Exodus 39:1
Exodus 39:1
NKJV

Exodus 39:1 Of the blue, purple, and scarlet thread they made garments of ministry, for ministering in the holy place, and made the holy garments for Aaron, as the LORD had commanded Moses.(NKJV)

NLT

Exodus 39:1 The craftsmen made beautiful sacred garments of blue, purple, and scarlet cloth — clothing for Aaron to wear while ministering in the Holy Place, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. (NLT)

Main Idea

From blue, purple, and scarlet thread the workers make garments for ministering in the holy place and the holy garments for Aaron, “as the LORD had commanded Moses.” The refrain of obedience that runs through the chapter begins.

Verse Explanation

From blue, purple, and scarlet thread the workers make garments for ministering in the holy place and the holy garments for Aaron, “as the LORD had commanded Moses.” The refrain of obedience that runs through the chapter begins.

“Of the blue, purple, and scarlet thread they made garments of ministry, for ministering in the holy place,”

The workers make “garments of ministry,” clothing for service in the holy place.

“and made the holy garments for Aaron, as the LORD had commanded Moses.”

They make Aaron’s “holy garments” “as the LORD had commanded Moses.” This phrase returns seven times in the garment account (39:1, 5, 7, 21, 26, 29, 31). Aaron had made the calf according to the people’s demand (32:1–5). Now the garments for his priestly service are made according to the LORD’s word.

Study Notes

Exodus 39:1–31 records the making of the garments described in 28:1–43. The explanations given there apply here.
