Exodus 40:34–35 — The Glory of the LORD Fills the Tabernacle

Study Notes

None.

Exodus 40:34
Exodus 40:34
NKJV

Exodus 40:34 Then the cloud covered the tabernacle of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.(NKJV)

NLT

Exodus 40:34 Then the cloud covered the Tabernacle, and the glory of the LORD filled the Tabernacle. (NLT)

Main Idea

“Then the cloud covered the tabernacle of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.” The goal of the book is reached as God’s glory fills the dwelling His people prepared.

Verse Explanation

“Then the cloud covered the tabernacle of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.” The goal of the book is reached as God’s glory fills the dwelling His people prepared.

“Then the cloud covered the tabernacle of meeting,”

The “cloud” that led Israel from Egypt (13:21) now “covered the tabernacle.”

“and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.”

This is the goal of the book. God brought Israel out of Egypt to dwell among them (29:45–46). Now He does. The filling is God’s act, not the people’s achievement. The God whose presence was threatened after the calf (33:3) now dwells in the midst of His forgiven people. In Christ, God’s glory came to dwell with His people in human flesh: “the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld His glory” (John 1:14).

Study Notes

The same thing happened when Solomon’s temple was dedicated (1 Kings 8:10–11).
