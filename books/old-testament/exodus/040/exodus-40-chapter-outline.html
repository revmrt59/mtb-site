Exodus 40 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Exodus 40 brings the book to its goal: the LORD commands Moses to set up and anoint the tabernacle, Moses does everything as the LORD commanded, and then the cloud covers the tent and “the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle” (Exodus 40:34). The book closes with the LORD dwelling among His people and leading them by cloud and fire throughout all their journeys.

Chapter Sections

1. Exodus 40:1–8 — The LORD Commands Moses to Set Up the Tabernacle

The LORD tells Moses to set up the tabernacle on the first day of the first month. He is to place the ark behind the veil, the table, lampstand, and incense altar in the tent, the altar of burnt offering and the laver outside, and the court all around.

2. Exodus 40:9–15 — The LORD Commands the Anointing of the Tabernacle and the Priests

Moses is to anoint the tabernacle, its furnishings, the altar, and the laver so that they are holy. He is to wash, clothe, and anoint Aaron and his sons, “for their anointing shall surely be an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations” (Exodus 40:15).

3. Exodus 40:16–33 — Moses Raises the Tabernacle as the LORD Commanded

“Thus Moses did; according to all that the LORD had commanded him, so he did” (Exodus 40:16): on the first day of the first month of the second year he raises the tabernacle and places the Testimony in the ark, the ark behind the veil, and the table, lampstand, and gold altar in the tent. He sets the altar of burnt offering and the laver before the door, raises the court, and “So Moses finished the work” (Exodus 40:33).

4. Exodus 40:34–35 — The Glory of the LORD Fills the Tabernacle

“Then the cloud covered the tabernacle of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle” (Exodus 40:34). Moses is not able to enter the tent because the cloud rests on it and the glory fills it.

5. Exodus 40:36–38 — The Cloud and Fire Lead Israel Throughout Their Journeys

Whenever the cloud is taken up from the tabernacle, Israel sets out, and when it remains, they stay. The cloud of the LORD is over the tabernacle by day and fire by night, in the sight of all Israel, “throughout all their journeys” (Exodus 40:38).
