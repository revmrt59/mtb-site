POWER - ( 'oz , Strong's H5797 )

Summary:

The Hebrew word 'oz represents strength, power, might, and security, most frequently used to describe the sovereign, protective, and majestic nature of God. In Habakkuk 3:4, it highlights the infinite and unapproachable omnipotence of Yahweh, which is so vast that even His most dazzling outward physical revelations serve to veil or hide the true, unlimited fullness of His divine power.

Full Study:

1. Word Picture / Background

The noun 'oz derives from the root verb 'azaz, which means to be strong, to prevail, or to make secure. In the ancient Near Eastern context, strength was not an abstract concept but was deeply tied to physical survival, military victory, and structural defense. Therefore, 'oz carries the concrete imagery of a fortress, a strong tower, or a well-defended refuge. When applied to God, it presents Him not merely as possessing power, but as being the ultimate safe-haven and invincible stronghold for His people.

2. Meaning / Lexical Definition

The primary definition of 'oz is physical or material strength, might, and power. It encompasses:

  • Inherent majesty, glory, and splendor, especially when associated with God's kingly rule.

  • Political, social, or military dominance and power.

  • Protection, safety, and security, functioning synonymously with a defense or refuge.

  • The boldness, praise, or strength of voice raised in worship.

3. Usage and Word Pictures

The Old Testament employs 'oz in several evocative ways to depict both divine and human realities:

  • The Power of the King: It describes the sovereign rule of Yahweh. His throne is established in strength, and He is clothed with majesty and strength (Psalm 93:1).

  • The Fortress of the Believer: God is depicted as the source of personal protection. He is the "strength" of His people and a "strong tower" against the enemy (Psalm 61:3).

  • The Ark of God: The Ark of the Covenant, the physical manifestation of God's presence among His people, is called "the ark of Your strength" (Psalm 132:8).

  • Human Boldness and Praise: It can refer to the physical strength of a person or even a "strong" face showing boldness, but it is also used of the loud praise of God’s people, which acts as a spiritual bulwark against adversaries (Psalm 8:2).

4. Theological Implications

Theological usage of 'oz reinforces that true, permanent power belongs exclusively to God. Human power is fleeting, fragile, and dependent, whereas God’s power is uncreated, self-sustaining, and eternal. Furthermore, His power is never detached from His covenant faithfulness. He does not wield His omnipotence capriciously; rather, He utilizes His supreme 'oz to deliver, sustain, and defend His covenant people. Thus, the revelation of His power demands a response of absolute trust, humility, and praise.

5. Contextual Nuance in the Passage

In Habakkuk 3:4, the prophet experiences a dramatic, terrifying vision of God marching from Teman in holy judgment and redemption. The prophet writes, "His brightness was like the light; He had rays flashing from His hand, And there His power was hidden."

  • The "rays flashing from His hand" represent the lightning-like, brilliant manifestations of God's glory in the physical world.

  • The paradox of the passage lies in the phrase "And there His power ('oz) was hidden." Rather than the flashing light being the full exposure of God's strength, it is actually the veil that hides it.

  • The absolute, infinite 'oz of God is so intense that direct exposure would utterly consume creation. Therefore, even His most glorious, blinding physical revelations are merely a shroud—a hiding place—for the far greater, unapproachable omnipotence that lies behind them. For the believer, this is deeply comforting: the resources of God's power on our behalf are always infinitely greater than what is visible to the human eye.

6. Key Uses in Other Verses (NKJV)

  • Exodus 15:2 - "The LORD is my strength and song, And He has become my salvation; He is my God, and I will praise Him; My father’s God, and I will exalt Him."

  • Psalm 29:11 - "The LORD will give strength to His people; The LORD will bless His people with peace."

  • Psalm 62:11 - "God has spoken once, Twice I have heard this: That power belongs to God."

  • Psalm 96:6 - "Honor and majesty are before Him; Strength and beauty are in His sanctuary."

  • Proverbs 18:10 - "The name of the LORD is a strong tower; The righteous run to it and are safe."

7. Other Relevant Insights

In the Greek translation of the Old Testament (the Septuagint), 'oz is most frequently translated using the terms ischys (mighty strength, power) and dynamis (inherent power, force). In Habakkuk 3:4, the translators used the term krataia, emphasizing a firmly established, ruling power that is sovereign over all creation. This highlights that the hidden power of God in Habakkuk is not a chaotic or unpredictable energy, but a stable, ruling, and purposeful authority.
