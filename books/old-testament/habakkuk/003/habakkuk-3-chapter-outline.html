Habakkuk 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Habakkuk 3 is “A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet” (Habakkuk 3:1), in which the prophet asks the LORD to revive His work and in wrath to remember mercy, then recalls God coming in glory and marching out to save His people. Trembling at the invasion ahead, Habakkuk resolves that even if every crop and herd fails, he will rejoice in the God of his salvation, who is his strength and makes his feet like deer’s feet on the heights.

Chapter Sections

1. Habakkuk 3:1–2 — Habakkuk’s Prayer for Mercy in Wrath

Habakkuk says he has heard the LORD’s speech and was afraid. He prays, “O LORD, revive Your work in the midst of the years!” (Habakkuk 3:2), and asks that in wrath God would remember mercy.

2. Habakkuk 3:3–7 — God Comes from Teman in Glory

God comes from Teman, the Holy One from Mount Paran, and His glory covers the heavens while the earth is full of His praise. Pestilence goes before Him, the everlasting mountains are scattered at His coming, and the tents of Cushan and the curtains of Midian tremble.

3. Habakkuk 3:8–15 — The LORD Marches Out to Save His People

Habakkuk asks whether the LORD was angry with the rivers and the sea when He rode on His chariots of salvation, as the mountains tremble, the deep lifts its voice, and the sun and moon stand still. He went forth “for the salvation of Your people, For salvation with Your Anointed” (Habakkuk 3:13), striking the head of the house of the wicked and walking through the sea with His horses.

4. Habakkuk 3:16–19 — Trembling, Loss, and Joy in the God of Salvation

When Habakkuk hears these things, his body trembles, and he waits so that he might rest in the day of trouble when the invader comes up against the people. Though fig tree, vine, olive, field, flock, and herd may all fail, he declares, “Yet I will rejoice in the LORD, I will joy in the God of my salvation” (Habakkuk 3:18), for the LORD God is his strength and will make his feet like deer’s feet on his high hills.
