Haggai 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Haggai 1 records the LORD’s first message through Haggai in King Darius’s second year, answering the people’s claim that the time has not come to build His house by calling them twice to “Consider your ways!” (Haggai 1:5), explaining their failed harvests and drought as His response to the neglect of His house, and commanding them to build. The chapter ends as the leaders and the remnant obey, the LORD assures them, “I am with you” (Haggai 1:13), and He stirs their spirits so that they begin the work.

Chapter Sections

1. Haggai 1:1–4 — The People Say the Time Has Not Come

On the first day of the sixth month in the second year of King Darius, the word of the LORD comes by Haggai to Zerubbabel the governor and Joshua the high priest, reporting that the people say, “The time has not come, the time that the LORD’s house should be built” (Haggai 1:2). The LORD asks, “Is it time for you yourselves to dwell in your paneled houses, and this temple to lie in ruins?” (Haggai 1:4).

2. Haggai 1:5–11 — Consider Your Ways

Twice the LORD says, “Consider your ways!” (Haggai 1:7), pointing to much sown and little harvested, food and drink that do not satisfy, and wages put into a bag with holes, and He commands them to bring wood and build the temple so that He may take pleasure in it and be glorified. He explains that He blew away what they brought home because His house lies in ruins while each of them runs to his own house, and that He called for the drought on the land, the crops, people, livestock, and all the labor of their hands.

3. Haggai 1:12–15 — The Remnant Obeys and the LORD Stirs Them

Zerubbabel, Joshua, and all the remnant of the people obey the voice of the LORD and the words of Haggai, and the people fear the presence of the LORD. Haggai, the LORD’s messenger, tells them, “I am with you, says the LORD” (Haggai 1:13), and the LORD stirs up the spirit of the leaders and the people, who come and work on His house on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month.
