Obadiah Book Outline

Revision 2 • October 23, 2026

Major Movements

I. The LORD Will Bring Edom Down (Obadiah 1:1–9)

The vision opens with a report from the LORD: a messenger has been sent among the nations to rise up against Edom. Though Edom dwells in the clefts of the rock and sets its nest among the stars, the pride of its heart has deceived it: “From there I will bring you down.” Its hidden treasures will be searched out, its allies will deceive it, and its wise men and mighty men will fail until everyone from the mountains of Esau is cut off.

II. Violence Against Your Brother Jacob (Obadiah 1:10–14)

The LORD names the reason for the judgment: violence against Edom’s brother Jacob. His indictment intensifies from complicity to active betrayal: in the day strangers entered Jerusalem’s gates, Edom stood aloof as one of them, and the oracle’s repeated “you should not have” presses the charge through gazing, rejoicing, and speaking proudly to entering the gate, laying hands on its substance, and cutting off and delivering up those who escaped.

III. The Day of the LORD and the Kingdom (Obadiah 1:15–21)

Verse 15 widens Edom’s judgment into the Day of the LORD, which the oracle proclaims is near upon all the nations: as Edom has done, it will be done to it, and “as you drank on My holy mountain, so shall all the nations drink continually.” But on Mount Zion there will be deliverance and holiness; the house of Jacob will be a fire, the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau stubble, and the returning captives of Israel and Jerusalem will possess the land of their inheritance. Saviors will come to Mount Zion to judge the mountains of Esau, and the kingdom shall be the LORD’s.
