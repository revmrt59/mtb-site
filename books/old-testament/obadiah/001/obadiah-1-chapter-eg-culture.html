East Gate Culture in Obadiah Chapter 1

HONOR

Culture Statement

HONOR: Through His Eyes. Relationships are protected when we ascribe heaven's value to the people around us. Like Jesus, we choose to interact with others, not as they deserve, but according to their God given value.

Living It in Obadiah

Obadiah 1 confronts Edom for treating Israel-called in the text "your brother Jacob"-with violence, plunder, and gloating. The prophet's anger at betrayal shows that God honors the vulnerable and holds people responsible when they exploit or rejoice in another's ruin. Apply this by refusing to celebrate others' suffering, protecting those who are weak (especially kin and neighbors), and choosing words and actions that reflect the worth God gives every person rather than using others for advantage.

Israel

Culture Statement

WE STAND WITH ISRAEL: We love what the Lord loves. The Lord loves Israel, and we love Israel. We take seriously, the Lord's promise to bless hose who bless Israel, and curse those who curse Israel.

Living It in Obadiah

Obadiah 1 makes God's concern for Israel plain: He announces judgment on Edom for attacking and betraying Jacob and promises vindication and restoration for God's people. The chapter calls us to share God's care for Israel by praying for their protection, opposing cruelty done to them, and standing with the oppressed whom God has promised to defend-trusting God's justice rather than taking part in gloating or exploitation.
