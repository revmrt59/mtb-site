Obadiah 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Obadiah’s single chapter is the whole vision. The LORD announces that He will bring proud Edom down for its violence against its brother Jacob, widens that judgment into the Day of the LORD upon all the nations, and ends with deliverance on Mount Zion and the declaration that the kingdom shall be the LORD’s.

Chapter Sections

1. Obadiah 1:1–4 — The LORD Will Bring Proud Edom Down

The vision opens with a report from the LORD that the nations are summoned to rise up against Edom. Though Edom dwells in the clefts of the rock and sets its nest among the stars, the pride of its heart has deceived it, and the LORD will bring it down.

2. Obadiah 1:5–9 — Edom Plundered, Betrayed, and Cut Off

Edom will be stripped more thoroughly than by thieves or grape-gatherers, and its hidden treasures will be searched out. Its allies will deceive it, its wise men will be destroyed, and its mighty men will be dismayed until everyone from the mountains of Esau is cut off.

3. Obadiah 1:10–14 — Violence Against Your Brother Jacob

For violence against its brother Jacob, shame will cover Edom. In the day strangers entered Jerusalem’s gates, Edom stood aloof as one of them, and the repeated charge presses from gazing and rejoicing to plundering and handing over those who escaped.

4. Obadiah 1:15–16 — The Day of the LORD upon All the Nations

The day of the LORD upon all the nations is near: “As you have done, it shall be done to you.” As they drank on the LORD’s holy mountain, so all the nations will drink continually.

5. Obadiah 1:17–18 — Deliverance and Holiness on Mount Zion

On Mount Zion there will be deliverance and holiness, and the house of Jacob will possess their possessions. The house of Jacob will be a fire, the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau stubble with no survivor.

6. Obadiah 1:19–21 — The Kingdom Shall Be the LORD’s

God’s people will possess the lands around them in every direction; the captives of Israel will possess the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath, and the captives of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad will possess the cities of the South. Saviors will come to Mount Zion to judge the mountains of Esau, and the kingdom shall be the LORD’s.
