Proverbs 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 1 opens the book by naming its source, its purpose, and its motto, that the fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge. It then sets two voices before the son: sinners who entice him toward violent gain, and Wisdom, who cries out in public and warns of the end of refusing her.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 1:1–7 — The Purpose of Proverbs and the Fear of the LORD

The book presents the proverbs of Solomon, given so that the simple, the young, and even the wise may gain wisdom, instruction, prudence, and understanding. Its motto is that the fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction.

2. Proverbs 1:8–9 — Hear Your Father and Mother

The son is called to hear his father’s instruction and not forsake his mother’s teaching, which will be a graceful ornament on his head and chains about his neck.

3. Proverbs 1:10–19 — Refuse the Enticement of Sinners

When sinners invite the son to lie in wait for blood and share one purse, he must not consent or walk in their path. Their feet run to evil, but they lie in wait for their own blood, for greed takes away the life of its owners.

4. Proverbs 1:20–23 — Wisdom Calls the Simple to Turn

Wisdom cries aloud in the streets and at the city gates, asking how long the simple, the scorners, and the fools will love their ways. She calls them to turn at her rebuke and promises to pour out her spirit and make her words known.

5. Proverbs 1:24–33 — The End of Refusing Wisdom

Because they refused her call and despised her counsel, Wisdom will not answer when calamity comes, and they will eat the fruit of their own way. The turning away of the simple will slay them, but whoever listens to her will dwell safely.
