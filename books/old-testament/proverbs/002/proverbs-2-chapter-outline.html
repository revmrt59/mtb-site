Proverbs 2 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 2 promises that the son who seeks wisdom as hidden treasure will find the fear of the LORD, because the LORD gives wisdom and guards the upright. That wisdom will deliver him from evil men and from the immoral woman, so that he walks in the way of goodness.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 2:1–6 — Seek Wisdom, and the LORD Will Give It

If the son receives his father’s words, cries out for understanding, and seeks wisdom as silver and hidden treasure, he will understand the fear of the LORD and find the knowledge of God. For the LORD gives wisdom, and from His mouth come knowledge and understanding.

2. Proverbs 2:7–11 — The LORD Guards the Upright

The LORD stores up sound wisdom for the upright, shields those who walk uprightly, and guards the paths of justice. When wisdom enters the heart, discretion and understanding will keep the son.

3. Proverbs 2:12–15 — Deliverance from the Way of Evil Men

Wisdom will deliver him from the man who speaks perverse things and from those who leave upright paths to walk in darkness and delight in evil.

4. Proverbs 2:16–19 — Deliverance from the Immoral Woman

Wisdom will also deliver him from the seductress who forsakes the companion of her youth and forgets the covenant of her God. Her house leads down to death, and none who go to her return.

5. Proverbs 2:20–22 — The Upright Will Dwell in the Land

So the son may walk in the way of goodness, for the upright will dwell in the land, but the wicked will be cut off from the earth.
