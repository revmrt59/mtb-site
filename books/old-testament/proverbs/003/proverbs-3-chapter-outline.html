Proverbs 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 3 calls the son to keep his father’s commands, to trust the LORD with all his heart, to honor Him and accept His correction, and to treasure wisdom as a tree of life. It closes with practical righteousness toward neighbors under the LORD’s blessing and curse.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 3:1–4 — Keep My Commands, Mercy, and Truth

The son is to keep his father’s commands, which will add length of days and peace, and to bind mercy and truth around his neck, finding favor with God and man.

2. Proverbs 3:5–8 — Trust in the LORD with All Your Heart

He is to trust in the LORD with all his heart and not lean on his own understanding, acknowledging Him in all his ways so that He will direct his paths. He must not be wise in his own eyes but fear the LORD and depart from evil.

3. Proverbs 3:9–10 — Honor the LORD with Your Possessions

He is to honor the LORD with his possessions and the firstfruits of all his increase, and his barns will be filled with plenty.

4. Proverbs 3:11–12 — The LORD’s Loving Correction

He must not despise the LORD’s chastening, for the LORD corrects the one He loves, as a father the son in whom he delights.

5. Proverbs 3:13–18 — Wisdom Is a Tree of Life

Happy is the one who finds wisdom, whose gain is better than silver and gold and more precious than rubies. Her ways are pleasantness and peace, and she is a tree of life to those who take hold of her.

6. Proverbs 3:19–20 — By Wisdom the LORD Founded the Earth

The LORD founded the earth by wisdom and established the heavens by understanding.

7. Proverbs 3:21–26 — The LORD Will Be Your Confidence

The son is to keep sound wisdom and discretion, which will bring him safety by day and sweet sleep by night. He need not fear sudden terror, for the LORD will be his confidence.

8. Proverbs 3:27–31 — Do Good to Your Neighbor

He must not withhold good when it is in his power, put off a neighbor in need, devise evil against a neighbor, quarrel without cause, or envy the oppressor.

9. Proverbs 3:32–35 — The LORD’s Curse and Blessing

The perverse are an abomination to the LORD, but His secret counsel is with the upright. He curses the house of the wicked, blesses the home of the just, scorns the scornful, and gives grace to the humble.
