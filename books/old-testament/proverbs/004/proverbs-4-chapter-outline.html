Proverbs 4 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 4 passes a father’s instruction to his sons as he received it from his own father: get wisdom above all, walk the path of the just rather than the way of the wicked, and keep the heart with all diligence.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 4:1–4 — Instruction Across Three Generations

A father calls his children to hear his teaching, recalling how his own father taught him to keep his commands and live.

2. Proverbs 4:5–9 — Get Wisdom: The Principal Thing

Wisdom is the principal thing, so the son is to get wisdom and understanding, love and exalt her, and she will preserve, honor, and crown him.

3. Proverbs 4:10–19 — Two Paths

The father has led his son in right paths and warns him not to enter the path of the wicked, who cannot sleep unless they have done evil. The path of the just is like the shining sun that shines ever brighter, but the way of the wicked is like darkness.

4. Proverbs 4:20–27 — Keep Your Heart with All Diligence

The son is to keep his father’s words in his heart, for they are life and health. Above all he must keep his heart with all diligence, guard his mouth and eyes, ponder the path of his feet, and turn from evil.
