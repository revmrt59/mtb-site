Proverbs 5 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 5 warns the son against the immoral woman, whose words are sweet but whose end is bitter and deadly, and calls him to rejoice in the wife of his youth, remembering that the LORD sees every way.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 5:1–6 — Sweet Lips and a Bitter End

The father calls his son to attention, for the lips of an immoral woman drip honey but her end is bitter as wormwood. Her feet go down to death, and her ways are unstable.

2. Proverbs 5:7–14 — Stay Far from Her Door

The son must keep far from her house, lest he give his honor and wealth to strangers. At the last he would mourn that he hated instruction and was on the verge of total ruin.

3. Proverbs 5:15–20 — Rejoice with the Wife of Your Youth

The son is to drink water from his own cistern and rejoice with the wife of his youth, being always satisfied with her love. Why should he be enraptured by an immoral woman?

4. Proverbs 5:21–23 — The LORD Sees, and Sin Entraps

The ways of man are before the eyes of the LORD. The wicked are caught by their own iniquities and die for lack of instruction.
