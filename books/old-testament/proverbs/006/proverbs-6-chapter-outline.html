Proverbs 6 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 6 gathers practical warnings about rash pledges, laziness, the worthless troublemaker, and the seven things the LORD hates, and then returns to the commandment as a lamp that guards the son from adultery.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 6:1–5 — Get Free from a Rash Pledge

If the son has become surety for a neighbor, he is to go and humble himself without delay to deliver himself, like a gazelle from the hunter.

2. Proverbs 6:6–11 — Go to the Ant, You Sluggard

The sluggard is to learn from the ant, which gathers its food in harvest without a ruler. A little sleep and folding of the hands will bring poverty like a prowler.

3. Proverbs 6:12–15 — The Worthless Troublemaker

The worthless person walks with a perverse mouth and devious gestures, devises evil, and sows discord, and calamity will come on him suddenly.

4. Proverbs 6:16–19 — Seven Things the LORD Hates

Six things the LORD hates, and seven are an abomination to Him, from a proud look and a lying tongue to one who sows discord among brethren.

5. Proverbs 6:20–23 — The Commandment Is a Lamp

The son is to bind his parents’ commands on his heart, for they will lead and keep him. The commandment is a lamp, the law a light, and reproofs of instruction the way of life.

6. Proverbs 6:24–35 — Fire in the Bosom

That teaching will keep him from the seductress whose beauty he must not desire. As no one can carry fire in his bosom without being burned, whoever goes in to a neighbor’s wife will not go unpunished, and the husband’s jealousy will accept no ransom.
