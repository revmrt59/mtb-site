Proverbs 7 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 7 urges the son to treasure his father’s words and then watches a young man devoid of understanding walk step by step into the house of a seductress, which is the way to death.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 7:1–5 — Treasure My Words

The son is to keep his father’s commands as the apple of his eye and call wisdom his sister, so that they may keep him from the seductress.

2. Proverbs 7:6–12 — The Simple Young Man Near Her Corner

From his window the father sees among the simple a young man passing near her corner at twilight. She meets him dressed as a harlot, loud and rebellious, lurking at every corner.

3. Proverbs 7:13–20 — The Seductress’s Invitation

She seizes and kisses him and invites him to her perfumed bed, claiming she has paid her vows and her husband is away on a long journey.

4. Proverbs 7:21–23 — Like an Ox to the Slaughter

With flattering lips she causes him to yield, and he follows her at once, like an ox to the slaughter or a bird hastening to the snare, not knowing it will cost his life.

5. Proverbs 7:24–27 — Her House Is the Way to Hell

The father calls his children not to turn aside to her ways, for she has cast down many, and her house is the way to hell.
