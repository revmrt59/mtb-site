Proverbs 8 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

In Proverbs 8 Wisdom calls openly to all people, describes her truth, riches, and rule, and declares her presence with the LORD before and at creation, closing with the promise that whoever finds her finds life.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 8:1–11 — Wisdom’s Open Call

Wisdom takes her stand at the heights, the crossroads, and the gates and calls to all people, including the simple and fools. Her words are true and righteous, and her instruction is better than silver, gold, and rubies.

2. Proverbs 8:12–21 — Wisdom’s Character and Rule

Wisdom dwells with prudence and hates pride and evil. By her kings reign and rulers decree justice; she loves those who love her and grants enduring riches and righteousness.

3. Proverbs 8:22–26 — Before the World Began

The LORD possessed Wisdom at the beginning of His way, and she was established from everlasting, before the depths, the mountains, and the earth.

4. Proverbs 8:27–31 — Beside Him at Creation

When the LORD prepared the heavens and set the limits of the sea, Wisdom was beside Him as a master craftsman, daily His delight, rejoicing in His world and delighting in the sons of men.

5. Proverbs 8:32–36 — Whoever Finds Me Finds Life

Wisdom calls her children to listen, for blessed is the one who watches daily at her gates. Whoever finds her finds life and favor from the LORD, but all who hate her love death.
