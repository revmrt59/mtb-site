Proverbs 9 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 9 sets Wisdom’s banquet and Folly’s counterfeit invitation side by side, with sayings in between on correcting the scoffer and the fear of the LORD as the beginning of wisdom.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 9:1–6 — Wisdom’s House and Banquet

Wisdom builds her house, prepares her meat and wine, and sends her maidens to invite the simple to eat her bread, forsake foolishness, and live.

2. Proverbs 9:7–9 — Correcting the Scoffer, Teaching the Wise

Whoever corrects a scoffer gets shame, but a wise man who is rebuked will love the one who rebukes him and grow wiser still.

3. Proverbs 9:10–12 — The Fear of the LORD Is the Beginning of Wisdom

The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom and the knowledge of the Holy One is understanding. Through Wisdom days are multiplied, and each person bears the result of being wise or scoffing.

4. Proverbs 9:13–18 — Folly’s Counterfeit Invitation

The foolish woman sits at her door and calls the simple with the same words as Wisdom, offering stolen water and bread eaten in secret. But her guests are in the depths of hell.
