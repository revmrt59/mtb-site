Proverbs 10 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 10 opens Solomon’s collection with short contrasts between the wise and the foolish, the righteous and the wicked, and the diligent and the lazy, applying the two ways especially to work, wealth, and speech.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 10:1–5 — A Wise Son and Diligent Hands

The proverbs of Solomon begin with the joy a wise son brings and the grief a foolish son brings. Righteousness delivers from death, the LORD provides for the righteous, and the diligent hand makes rich while the son who sleeps in harvest causes shame.

2. Proverbs 10:6–11 — Blessing on the Righteous, Violence in the Mouth of the Wicked

Blessings rest on the righteous and their memory is blessed, while the wicked are marked by violence and a rotting name. The wise receive commands and walk securely, but the prating fool falls, and the mouth of the righteous is a well of life.

3. Proverbs 10:12–21 — Words That Wound and Words That Feed

Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all sins. Wisdom is on the lips of the understanding, many words invite sin, the restrained lips are wise, and the lips of the righteous feed many.

4. Proverbs 10:22–30 — The Everlasting Foundation of the Righteous

The blessing of the LORD makes rich without sorrow. The hope of the righteous will be gladness and they have an everlasting foundation, while the wicked pass away like a whirlwind and the lazy man vexes those who send him.

5. Proverbs 10:31–32 — The Mouth of the Righteous Brings Forth Wisdom

The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom and knows what is acceptable, but the perverse tongue will be cut out.
