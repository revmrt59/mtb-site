Proverbs 12 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 12 contrasts the stability of the righteous with the overthrow of the wicked and gathers its largest cluster on speech, truthful and lying lips, words that pierce and words that heal, before closing with diligence and the way of life.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 12:1–12 — The Root of the Righteous Cannot Be Moved

Whoever loves instruction loves knowledge, and the root of the righteous cannot be moved. An excellent wife is her husband’s crown, the house of the righteous stands, the righteous man regards the life of his animal, and he who tills his land is satisfied.

2. Proverbs 12:13–23 — Truthful Lips and a Lying Tongue

The wicked are ensnared by their lips, but the fruit of the mouth satisfies with good. Some words pierce like a sword while the tongue of the wise promotes health; truthful lips endure, and lying lips are an abomination to the LORD.

3. Proverbs 12:24–28 — Diligence, a Good Word, and the Way of Life

The diligent will rule and the lazy man will be put to forced labor. Anxiety weighs the heart down but a good word makes it glad, and in the way of righteousness is life.
