Proverbs 13 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 13 contrasts the teachable son with the scoffer and gathers sayings on guarding the mouth, wealth gained honestly or dishonestly, hope and the life-giving word, companions, and loving discipline.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 13:1–6 — The Teachable Son and the Guarded Mouth

A wise son heeds his father, but a scoffer does not listen to rebuke. He who guards his mouth preserves his life, the diligent are made rich, and righteousness guards the blameless.

2. Proverbs 13:7–11 — Riches, Pride, and Honest Gain

Some make themselves rich yet have nothing, and others make themselves poor yet have great riches. Pride brings strife, and wealth gained dishonestly dwindles while what is gathered by labor increases.

3. Proverbs 13:12–19 — Hope Fulfilled and the Fountain of Life

Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but a desire fulfilled is a tree of life. Those who fear the commandment are rewarded, the law of the wise is a fountain of life, and the one who regards rebuke is honored.

4. Proverbs 13:20–25 — Companions, Inheritance, and Discipline

He who walks with the wise will be wise, but the companion of fools will be destroyed. A good man leaves an inheritance to his grandchildren, and he who loves his son disciplines him promptly.
