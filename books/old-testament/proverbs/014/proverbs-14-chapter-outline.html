Proverbs 14 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 14 opens with the wise woman who builds her house and gathers sayings on the way that seems right but ends in death, prudence and mercy, the fear of the LORD as confidence and a fountain of life, and righteousness that exalts a nation.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 14:1–7 — The Wise Woman Builds Her House

The wise woman builds her house, but the foolish tears it down. He who walks uprightly fears the LORD, a faithful witness does not lie, and the son is to leave the presence of a foolish man.

2. Proverbs 14:8–15 — The Way That Seems Right

The prudent understand their way, while fools mock at sin. There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death; the heart has hidden sorrows, and the prudent consider their steps while the simple believe every word.

3. Proverbs 14:16–25 — Prudence, Mercy to the Poor, and a True Witness

The wise fear and depart from evil while the fool rages, and the simple inherit folly. Happy is the one who has mercy on the poor, in all labor there is profit, and a true witness delivers souls.

4. Proverbs 14:26–27 — The Fear of the LORD Is a Fountain of Life

In the fear of the LORD there is strong confidence and refuge for His children, and it is a fountain of life that turns one from the snares of death.

5. Proverbs 14:28–35 — Righteousness Exalts a Nation

A king’s honor is in a multitude of people. Whoever oppresses the poor reproaches his Maker, the righteous have refuge in death, and righteousness exalts a nation while the king favors a wise servant.
