Proverbs 15 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 15 closes Solomon’s first group of sayings with gentle and healing speech, the LORD who sees every heart, worship from an upright heart, contentment, and the fear of the LORD, with humility before honor.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 15:1–7 — Gentle Words and the LORD’s Watchful Eyes

A soft answer turns away wrath, and a wholesome tongue is a tree of life. The eyes of the LORD are in every place, keeping watch on the evil and the good.

2. Proverbs 15:8–11 — Worship and the Heart Before the LORD

The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but the prayer of the upright is His delight. Even hell and destruction lie open before Him, so much more the hearts of men.

3. Proverbs 15:12–19 — A Merry Heart and a Little with the Fear of the LORD

A merry heart makes a cheerful face and has a continual feast. Better a little with the fear of the LORD than great treasure with trouble, and better a dinner of herbs where love is than a fatted calf with hatred.

4. Proverbs 15:20–24 — Counsel, a Timely Word, and the Upward Way

Plans are established by many counselors, and a word spoken in due season is good. The way of life winds upward for the wise.

5. Proverbs 15:25–33 — Before Honor Is Humility

The LORD destroys the house of the proud, defends the widow, and hears the prayer of the righteous. The ear that heeds rebuke abides among the wise, and the fear of the LORD is the instruction of wisdom, with humility before honor.
