Proverbs 16 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 16 opens the second group of Solomon’s sayings with the LORD’s rule over human plans, followed by sayings on the righteous king, wisdom and humility, pleasant words, the troublemaker, and self-control, closing with the lot whose decision is from the LORD.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 16:1–9 — The LORD Directs Human Plans

The preparations of the heart belong to man, but the LORD weighs the spirits, and those who commit their works to Him will have their thoughts established. Atonement is provided in mercy and truth, and though a man plans his way, the LORD directs his steps.

2. Proverbs 16:10–15 — The King’s Righteous Rule

The king must not transgress in judgment, for a throne is established by righteousness, and honest weights are the LORD’s. Kings delight in righteous lips, and the light of the king’s face is life.

3. Proverbs 16:16–24 — Wisdom, Humility, and Pleasant Words

Wisdom is better than gold, and pride goes before destruction, so it is better to be humble with the lowly. Whoever trusts in the LORD is happy, and pleasant words are like a honeycomb.

4. Proverbs 16:25–30 — The Way of Death and the Troublemaker

There is a way that seems right but ends in death. The ungodly man digs up evil, the perverse man sows strife, and the whisperer separates friends.

5. Proverbs 16:31–33 — Gray Hair, Self-Control, and the Lot

Gray hair is a crown of glory in the way of righteousness, and he who rules his spirit is better than one who takes a city. The lot is cast, but its every decision is from the LORD.
