Proverbs 17 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 17 gathers sayings on peace in the household and the LORD who tests hearts, the fool and the value of rebuke, strife and the faithful friend, and the quiet speech that marks understanding.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 17:1–6 — Quiet Homes and Tested Hearts

A dry morsel with quietness is better than a house of feasting with strife. The LORD tests the hearts, whoever mocks the poor reproaches his Maker, and grandchildren are the crown of the old.

2. Proverbs 17:7–13 — Rebuke, Love That Covers, and the Fool’s Folly

He who covers a transgression seeks love, but repeating a matter separates friends. Rebuke reaches the wise more than a hundred blows reach a fool, and it is safer to meet a bear robbed of her cubs than a fool in his folly.

3. Proverbs 17:14–20 — Strife, Justice, and the Faithful Friend

Strife begins like releasing water, so it should be stopped early. Justifying the wicked and condemning the just are both an abomination to the LORD, and a friend loves at all times while a brother is born for adversity.

4. Proverbs 17:21–28 — A Merry Heart and Restrained Words

A foolish son grieves his parents, while a merry heart does good like medicine. A bribe perverts justice, and he who has knowledge spares his words; even a fool is counted wise when he holds his peace.
