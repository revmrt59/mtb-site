Proverbs 18 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 18 contrasts the isolated man and the fool’s mouth with the name of the LORD as a strong tower, teaches fair hearing in disputes, and ends with the power of the tongue, the gift of a wife, and the friend who sticks closer than a brother.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 18:1–8 — Isolation and the Fool’s Mouth

The one who isolates himself seeks his own desire, and the fool delights only in expressing himself. The fool’s lips bring contention and are the snare of his soul, and the talebearer’s words go down into the inmost body.

2. Proverbs 18:9–12 — The Strong Tower and the Strong City

The name of the LORD is a strong tower where the righteous run and are safe, while the rich trust in wealth as their strong city. Before destruction the heart is haughty, and before honor is humility.

3. Proverbs 18:13–19 — Hearing a Matter and Settling Disputes

Answering before hearing is folly and shame, and the first to plead his cause seems right until another examines him. The lot causes contentions to cease, and an offended brother is harder to win than a strong city.

4. Proverbs 18:20–24 — The Tongue, a Wife, and a Faithful Friend

Death and life are in the power of the tongue. He who finds a wife finds a good thing and obtains favor from the LORD, and there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother.
