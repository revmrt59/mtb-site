Proverbs 19 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 19 gathers sayings on integrity and the false witness, discretion and the household, kindness to the poor and the LORD’s counsel that stands, and the fear of the LORD set against the lazy man and the scoffer.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 19:1–9 — Integrity, Fickle Friends, and the False Witness

Better the poor who walk in integrity than the perverse fool, and the man whose folly twists his way frets against the LORD. Wealth makes many friends while the poor are abandoned, and the false witness will not go unpunished.

2. Proverbs 19:10–14 — Discretion, the King’s Favor, and the Household

Discretion makes a man slow to anger, and his glory is to overlook a transgression. The king’s favor is like dew, a foolish son ruins his father, and a prudent wife is from the LORD.

3. Proverbs 19:15–21 — Pity for the Poor and the LORD’s Counsel

He who has pity on the poor lends to the LORD, who will repay. Parents are to chasten a son while there is hope, and though there are many plans in a man’s heart, the LORD’s counsel will stand.

4. Proverbs 19:22–29 — The Fear of the LORD and Judgment for Scoffers

Kindness is what is desired in a man, and the fear of the LORD leads to life. The lazy man will not lift his hand to his mouth, the son who mistreats his parents brings shame, and judgments are prepared for scoffers.
