Proverbs 20 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 20 gathers sayings on wine and strife, the heart no one can cleanse alone, honest dealing and guarded lips, waiting for the LORD rather than taking revenge, and the spirit of man as the lamp of the LORD.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 20:1–6 — Wine, Strife, and the Faithful Man

Wine is a mocker and strong drink a brawler. It is honorable to stop striving, the lazy man will not plow, counsel in the heart is deep water, and a faithful man is hard to find.

2. Proverbs 20:7–12 — Who Can Say His Heart Is Clean?

The righteous man walks in integrity, and the king scatters evil with his eyes. No one can say he has made his heart clean, diverse weights are an abomination to the LORD, and the LORD has made the hearing ear and the seeing eye.

3. Proverbs 20:13–19 — Honest Dealing and Guarded Lips

The one who loves sleep comes to poverty. Lips of knowledge are a precious jewel, bread gained by deceit turns to gravel, plans are established by counsel, and the talebearer is to be avoided.

4. Proverbs 20:20–25 — Wait for the LORD

The one who curses his parents will have his lamp put out. The son must not say he will repay evil but wait for the LORD to save him, for a man’s steps are of the LORD, and rash vows are a snare.

5. Proverbs 20:26–30 — The King, the Lamp of the LORD, and Cleansing Blows

A wise king sifts out the wicked and is preserved by mercy and truth. The spirit of man is the lamp of the LORD, searching the inner depths, and blows that hurt cleanse away evil.
