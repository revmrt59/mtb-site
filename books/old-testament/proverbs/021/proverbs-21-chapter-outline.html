Proverbs 21 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 21 is framed by the LORD’s rule over kings and battles, and between those frames gathers sayings on justice above sacrifice, the dangers of pride and haste, the cry of the poor, and the generous righteous set against the lazy and the proud.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 21:1–3 — The LORD Weighs the Hearts

The king’s heart is in the LORD’s hand, and the LORD weighs every heart. To do righteousness and justice is more acceptable to Him than sacrifice.

2. Proverbs 21:4–8 — Pride, Haste, and Ill-Gotten Treasure

A proud heart is sin, haste leads to poverty, and treasures gained by a lying tongue are a fleeting fantasy. The violence of the wicked destroys them because they refuse to do justice.

3. Proverbs 21:9–19 — Justice and the Cry of the Poor

It is better to dwell on a housetop corner or in the wilderness than with a contentious woman. When the scoffer is punished the simple learn, the one who shuts his ears to the poor will not be heard, and justice is a joy to the just.

4. Proverbs 21:20–29 — The Righteous Give and Do Not Spare

The wise keep treasure while the fool squanders it, and he who follows righteousness and mercy finds life and honor. Whoever guards his mouth keeps his soul from trouble, the proud man’s name is scoffer, and the lazy man covets while the righteous gives freely.

5. Proverbs 21:30–31 — Deliverance Is of the LORD

No wisdom, understanding, or counsel can stand against the LORD. The horse is prepared for battle, but deliverance is of the LORD.
