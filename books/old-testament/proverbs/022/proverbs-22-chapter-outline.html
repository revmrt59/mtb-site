Proverbs 22 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 22 closes Solomon’s second group of sayings with a good name, training, generosity, and justice for the poor, and at 22:17 opens the words of the wise with a call to hear and trust the LORD and the first warnings that follow.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 22:1–5 — A Good Name and the Maker of All

A good name is better than great riches, and the LORD is the Maker of rich and poor alike. The prudent foresee evil, and humility and the fear of the LORD bring riches, honor, and life.

2. Proverbs 22:6–16 — Training a Child and Justice for the Poor

A child is to be trained in the way he should go, and the borrower is servant to the lender. The generous are blessed, the scoffer is to be cast out, the lazy man makes excuses, and whoever oppresses the poor to gain riches will come to poverty.

3. Proverbs 22:17–21 — The Words of the Wise

The son is called to incline his ear to the words of the wise so that his trust may be in the LORD, and to learn the certainty of the words of truth.

4. Proverbs 22:22–29 — Do Not Rob the Poor or Move the Landmark

He must not rob the poor, for the LORD will plead their cause, nor befriend an angry man, become surety for debts, or remove the ancient landmark. The man who excels in his work will stand before kings.
