Proverbs 23 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 23 continues the words of the wise with instructions on appetite and riches, the fatherless and their Redeemer, discipline and hope, a father’s appeal for his son’s heart, and the book’s fullest portrait of drunkenness.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 23:1–3 — Restraint at a Ruler’s Table

When eating with a ruler, the son is to consider carefully what is before him and not desire his deceptive delicacies.

2. Proverbs 23:4–8 — Riches That Fly Away and the Miser’s Table

He must not wear himself out to be rich, for riches make themselves wings and fly away. Nor should he eat the bread of a miser, whose heart is not with him.

3. Proverbs 23:9–12 — The Fatherless and Their Mighty Redeemer

He should not speak in the hearing of a fool, nor remove the ancient landmark or enter the fields of the fatherless, for their Redeemer is mighty. He is to apply his heart to instruction.

4. Proverbs 23:13–18 — Correction, a Rejoicing Father, and a Sure Hope

Correction delivers a child’s soul from hell, and a father rejoices when his son’s heart is wise. The son must not envy sinners but be zealous for the fear of the LORD, for there is a hereafter and his hope will not be cut off.

5. Proverbs 23:19–28 — My Son, Give Me Your Heart

The son is to guide his heart in the way, avoid drunkards and gluttons, honor his father and mother, and buy the truth. He is to give his father his heart, for the harlot is a deep pit.

6. Proverbs 23:29–35 — Who Has Woe?

Woe, sorrow, and wounds belong to those who linger at the wine. At the last it bites like a serpent, confuses the mind, and leaves the drunkard seeking another drink.
