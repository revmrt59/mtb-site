Proverbs 24 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 24 completes the words of the wise with wisdom that builds a house, courage to rescue the perishing, the righteous who rise again, and refusal to gloat or fret, followed by a short appendix of further sayings ending at the field of the lazy man.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 24:1–6 — Wisdom Builds a House

The son must not envy evil men. Through wisdom a house is built and its rooms filled, and wise counsel gives strength in war.

2. Proverbs 24:7–12 — Strength in Adversity and Rescue for the Perishing

The fool has no word at the gate, the one who plots evil is called a schemer, and the scoffer is an abomination to men. Strength is small that faints in adversity, so the son is to deliver those drawn toward death, for He who weighs the hearts knows.

3. Proverbs 24:13–16 — Sweet Wisdom and the Righteous Who Rise Again

Wisdom is sweet like honey to the soul and gives hope that will not be cut off. The righteous may fall seven times and rise again, but the wicked fall by calamity.

4. Proverbs 24:17–22 — Do Not Gloat or Fret; Fear the LORD

He must not rejoice when his enemy falls or fret because of evildoers, whose lamp will be put out. He is to fear the LORD and the king.

5. Proverbs 24:23–29 — Further Sayings of the Wise

These things also belong to the wise: partiality in judgment is not good, and those who rebuke the wicked are blessed. Work is to be prepared before building a house, and no one is to repay another as he has been treated.

6. Proverbs 24:30–34 — The Field of the Lazy Man

The wise man passes the field of the lazy man, overgrown with thorns and its wall broken down, and learns that a little sleep and slumber bring poverty like a prowler.
