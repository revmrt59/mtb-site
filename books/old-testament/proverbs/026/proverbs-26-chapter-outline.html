Proverbs 26 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 26 gathers three portraits: the fool, to whom honor does not belong, the lazy man and his excuses, and the meddler, deceiver, talebearer, and hypocrite whose hidden hatred will be exposed.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 26:1–12 — Honor Does Not Fit a Fool

Honor fits a fool no better than snow in summer, and a rod is for his back. Wisdom discerns when to answer a fool and when not to, a proverb in a fool’s mouth is useless, and a fool returns to his folly; yet there is more hope for a fool than for one wise in his own eyes.

2. Proverbs 26:13–16 — The Lazy Man

The lazy man imagines a lion in the road, turns on his bed like a door on its hinges, will not lift his hand to his mouth, and thinks himself wiser than seven sensible men.

3. Proverbs 26:17–22 — Meddlers, Deceivers, and Talebearers

Meddling in another’s quarrel is like taking a dog by the ears, and deceiving a neighbor is no joke. Without a talebearer strife ceases, as a fire goes out without wood.

4. Proverbs 26:23–28 — Hidden Hatred Revealed

Fervent lips with a wicked heart are like silver dross over clay, and the one who disguises hatred will have his wickedness revealed before the assembly. Whoever digs a pit will fall into it.
