Proverbs 27 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 27 closes the comparisons of Hezekiah’s collection with sayings on humility about tomorrow, faithful rebuke, friends and neighbors, the heart revealed and never satisfied, and a closing call to tend one’s flocks because riches do not last.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 27:1–6 — Humility and Faithful Wounds

No one knows what a day may bring, so let another praise you. A fool’s wrath and jealousy are crushing, and the wounds of a friend are faithful while an enemy’s kisses deceive.

2. Proverbs 27:7–14 — Friends, Neighbors, and Prudence

The sweetness of a friend gives delight by hearty counsel, and a near neighbor is better than a distant brother. The prudent foresee evil, and a loud early blessing may be counted a curse.

3. Proverbs 27:15–22 — Iron Sharpens Iron

A contentious woman is like a continual dripping. As iron sharpens iron, so a man sharpens his friend; as water reflects a face, so the heart reveals the man; and the eyes of man are never satisfied.

4. Proverbs 27:23–27 — Know the State of Your Flocks

Be diligent to know the state of your flocks, for riches and crowns do not last, while the flocks will provide clothing, fields, and food for the household.
