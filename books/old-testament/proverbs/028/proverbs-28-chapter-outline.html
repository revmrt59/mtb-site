Proverbs 28 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 28 opens the last part of Hezekiah’s collection with direct contrasts between the righteous and the wicked, attention to God’s law, the mercy that follows confession, the effects of rulers, and the danger of hastening to be rich.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 28:1–11 — Keeping the Law and Walking in Integrity

The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are bold as a lion. Those who keep the law contend with the wicked, those who seek the LORD understand all, and the poor who walk in integrity are better than the perverse rich.

2. Proverbs 28:12–18 — Confession, Reverence, and Rulers

He who covers his sins will not prosper, but whoever confesses and forsakes them will have mercy, and happy is the one who is always reverent. A wicked ruler over the poor is like a roaring lion, but whoever walks blamelessly will be saved.

3. Proverbs 28:19–28 — Hastening to Be Rich and Trusting the LORD

A faithful man abounds with blessings, but he who hastens to be rich will not go unpunished. Rebuke wins more favor than flattery, he who trusts in the LORD will be prospered, and he who gives to the poor will not lack.
