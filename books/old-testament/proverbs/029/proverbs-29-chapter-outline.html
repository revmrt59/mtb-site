Proverbs 29 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 29 closes Solomon’s collections with the danger of a hardened neck, the effects of righteous and wicked rulers, discipline and the need for God’s revelation, and the freedom of trusting the LORD rather than fearing man.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 29:1–7 — The Hardened Neck and Righteous Rule

He who hardens his neck after rebuke will suddenly be destroyed. The people rejoice when the righteous rule, the king establishes the land by justice, and the righteous consider the cause of the poor.

2. Proverbs 29:8–14 — Wise Men, Truthful Rulers, and the Poor

Scoffers set a city aflame, but the wise turn away wrath, and the fool vents all his feelings. A ruler who listens to lies corrupts his servants, but the king who judges the poor with truth will have an established throne.

3. Proverbs 29:15–21 — Correction and Revelation

The rod and rebuke give wisdom, and the corrected son gives rest. Where there is no revelation the people cast off restraint, but happy is he who keeps the law.

4. Proverbs 29:22–27 — Pride, the Fear of Man, and Trust in the LORD

An angry man stirs up strife, and pride brings a man low. The fear of man brings a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe, and justice comes from the LORD; the unjust and the upright are an abomination to each other.
