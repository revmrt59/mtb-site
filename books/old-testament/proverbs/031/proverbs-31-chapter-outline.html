Proverbs 31 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Proverbs 31 closes the book with a queen mother’s counsel to King Lemuel to guard his strength and judge for the poor, followed by the acrostic portrait of the woman of valor whose worth rests on the fear of the LORD.

Chapter Sections

1. Proverbs 31:1–7 — A Mother’s Counsel to a King

King Lemuel’s mother teaches him not to give his strength to women or to wine, lest he forget the law and pervert justice for the afflicted.

2. Proverbs 31:8–9 — Open Your Mouth for the Speechless

He is to open his mouth for those who cannot speak, judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy.

3. Proverbs 31:10–12 — Who Can Find a Virtuous Wife?

A virtuous wife is worth far more than rubies; her husband trusts her, and she does him good all her days.

4. Proverbs 31:13–27 — Her Strength, Diligence, and Kindness

She works willingly with her hands, provides for her household, buys a field and plants a vineyard, and extends her hand to the poor. Strength and honor are her clothing, she speaks with wisdom and kindness, and she watches over her household.

5. Proverbs 31:28–31 — A Woman Who Fears the LORD Shall Be Praised

Her children and husband rise up and praise her. Charm is deceitful and beauty passing, but a woman who fears the LORD shall be praised, and her works praise her in the gates.
