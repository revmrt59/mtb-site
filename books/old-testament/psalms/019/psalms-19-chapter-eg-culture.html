EG Culture - Psalms 19

We are People of the Word

Culture Statement

The Holy Bible is the only infallible and final authority for Christian faith and life. The Holy Scriptures are timeless, inerrant and are to be read personally, discerned corporately, and walked out in community. We interpret all Scripture through the life and teachings of Jesus, trusting Holy Spirit to reveal truth and transform us as we submit ourselves fully to God’s revealed Word.

Living It in Psalms 19

David does not treat God's Word merely as information to understand. He describes it as perfect, sure, right, pure, clean, true, and more desirable than gold. God's Word restores the soul, makes the simple wise, rejoices the heart, enlightens the eyes, warns His servants, and leads to great reward. To be People of the Word is to treasure Scripture, submit to it, and allow it to continually transform how we think, desire, speak, and live. As we treasure God's Word, we are ultimately treasuring Jesus Christ, the Word made flesh (John 1:14), for all Scripture testifies of Him and leads us into a deeper knowledge of Him.

We are a House of Prayer

Culture Statement

Jesus identified His Father’s house as a House of Prayer. We assemble to seek the heart of the Father and partner with Him in faith, inquiry, intercession, supplication, petition, lament, and thanksgiving. We contend for mercy, righteousness, healing, and justice, believing that God transforms lives, cities, and nations through a praying Church.

Living It in Psalms 19

Psalm 19 moves from observing God's glory to praying for personal transformation. David asks God to reveal hidden faults, restrain him from deliberate sin, and make the words of his mouth and the meditation of his heart acceptable. This helps explain why David was called "a man after God's own heart." He continually invited God to search, cleanse, correct, and shape his heart according to God's own desires.

A House of Prayer does more than ask God to change circumstances. It invites Him to search, cleanse, guard, and transform our hearts. As we continually bring our hearts before Him in prayer, we become men and women after God's own heart.

Daily Secret Place

Culture Statement

When we set apart time and space to meet with God, Holy Spirit responds to our expectancy with His Presence. When we make the secret place our priority we become the place where Heaven touches Earth.

Living It in Psalms 19

It is easy to picture David alone beneath the night sky, quietly reflecting on the God whose glory the heavens declare. While the psalm does not tell us the exact setting in which it was written, it carries the atmosphere of the secret place. David pays attention to what God is revealing through creation, delights in the perfection and life-giving power of His Word, and then opens his inner life before Him in prayer.

Psalm 19 beckons us to the Secret Place, where admiration becomes meditation, meditation becomes prayer, and prayer becomes transformation as we encounter God's greatness in creation, delight in His truth through His Word, and welcome His searching light into our hearts.

We are a Tabernacle of David

Culture Statement

We were created to delight the Father and host His presence. As we embrace His ways, our lives become continual offerings of praise-both personally and together. We live with unhindered access to God, ministering to Him first and carrying His presence wherever we go. As His dwelling is restored among us, we become a people through whom the nations are drawn to seek the Lord.

Living It in Psalms 19

The heavens continually declare God’s glory without ceasing, and David desires his own life to join that worship. His closing prayer asks that both his spoken words and inward meditations become acceptable offerings to the Lord. Like creation, we were made to display God’s glory. Worship is not limited to songs; it includes the words of our mouths, the meditation of our hearts, and the lives we offer to Him as pleasing sacrifices before the Lord.

HEALTH || Trickle Down

Culture Statement

We are healthy when our thoughts, feelings, and desires reflect God’s. When we care for our spiritual, physical, financial, emotional, and interpersonal health, we are empowered to influence our communities for the better. Health flows from the top down and from the inside out.

Living It in Psalms 19

David understands that spiritual health begins beneath the surface. He asks God to cleanse him from hidden faults, protect him from sins that could gain control over him, and make his inner meditation pleasing to God. Health requires more than correcting outward behavior; it requires allowing God’s Word to expose, cleanse, and reorder the thoughts and desires of the heart.
