Psalms 19 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Psalm 19 moves from the glory of God declared by the heavens and the sun to the perfect law of the LORD that restores the soul, and ends with David’s prayer for cleansing, for protection from presumptuous sins, and for words acceptable to the LORD, his strength and his Redeemer.

Chapter Sections

1. Psalm 19:1–6 — The Heavens Declare the Glory of God

The heavens and the firmament proclaim God’s glory day after day and night after night without audible words, and their witness reaches to the end of the world. The sun runs its course like a bridegroom and a strong man, and nothing is hidden from its heat.

2. Psalm 19:7–11 — The Law of the LORD Is Perfect

The law, testimony, statutes, commandment, fear, and judgments of the LORD restore, instruct, gladden, and enlighten. They are more to be desired than gold and sweeter than honey, warning God’s servant and bringing great reward.

3. Psalm 19:12–14 — A Prayer for Cleansing and Acceptable Words

David asks to be cleansed from secret faults and kept back from presumptuous sins. He prays that the words of his mouth and the meditation of his heart may be acceptable to the LORD, his strength and his Redeemer.
