Psalms 20 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Psalm 20 is a prayer of God’s people for their anointed king, likely before battle, followed by a confident declaration that the LORD saves His anointed and a closing plea for the LORD to save.

Chapter Sections

1. Psalm 20:1–5 — The People Pray for the King

The people ask the LORD to answer the king in the day of trouble, send him help from the sanctuary, remember his offerings, and fulfill his purpose. They promise to rejoice in his salvation and set up their banners in the name of their God.

2. Psalm 20:6–8 — The LORD Saves His Anointed

The speaker now knows that the LORD saves His anointed and will answer him with the saving strength of His right hand. Some trust in chariots and horses, but God’s people remember the name of the LORD and stand upright.

3. Psalm 20:9 — Save, LORD!

The psalm closes with the plea for the LORD to save and for the King to answer when they call.
