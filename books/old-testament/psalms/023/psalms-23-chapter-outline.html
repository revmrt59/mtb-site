Psalms 23 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Psalm 23 is David’s confession of trust in the LORD as his Shepherd. It moves from the shepherd’s provision and guidance, through the dark valley where the LORD’s presence removes fear, to the host’s table and a lifetime in the house of the LORD, testifying to a whole life kept by His goodness and mercy.

Chapter Sections

1. Psalm 23:1–3 — The LORD Is My Shepherd

David confesses that the LORD is his shepherd, so that he lacks nothing essential. The LORD makes him lie down in green pastures, leads him beside still waters, restores his soul, and leads him in the paths of righteousness for His name’s sake.

2. Psalm 23:4 — Through the Valley, You Are with Me

Though he walks through the valley of the shadow of death, David will fear no evil, because the LORD is with him. Turning to speak directly to the LORD, he finds comfort in His rod and His staff.

3. Psalm 23:5–6 — The LORD’s Table and the LORD’s House

The LORD welcomes David as His guest, preparing a table before him in the presence of his enemies, anointing his head with oil, and filling his cup to overflowing. Goodness and mercy will follow him all the days of his life, and he will dwell in the house of the LORD forever.
