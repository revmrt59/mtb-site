Psalms 52 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Psalm 52, set by its title at Doeg the Edomite’s report to Saul, exposes the boastful, deceitful man who trusts in his riches, announces God’s judgment on him, and contrasts him with David, who flourishes like a green olive tree by trusting in the mercy of God.

Chapter Sections

1. Psalm 52:1–4 — The Boastful Man and His Deceitful Tongue

Under a title recalling Doeg’s report to Saul, David confronts the mighty man who boasts in evil, though the goodness of God endures continually. His tongue devises destruction like a sharp razor, and he loves evil, lying, and devouring words.

2. Psalm 52:5–7 — God Will Uproot the Wicked

God will destroy him forever and uproot him from the land of the living. The righteous will see and fear and point to the man who did not make God his strength but trusted in the abundance of his riches.

3. Psalm 52:8–9 — Like a Green Olive Tree in the House of God

David is like a green olive tree in the house of God, trusting in the mercy of God forever and ever. He will praise God for what He has done and wait on His name in the presence of His saints.
