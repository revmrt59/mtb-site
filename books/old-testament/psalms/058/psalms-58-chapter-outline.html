Psalms 58 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Psalm 58 is a prayer for God to establish justice. It exposes those who fail to judge uprightly and the entrenched evil of the wicked, asks God to break the power that sustains their oppression, and ends with the confession that there is a reward for the righteous and a God who judges in the earth.

Chapter Sections

1. Psalm 58:1–5 — Unjust Judges Exposed

David asks whether those who judge truly speak righteousness and judge uprightly, and answers that in heart they work wickedness and weigh out violence in the earth. The wicked go astray from birth, speaking lies, and are like a deaf cobra that will not heed the charmer.

2. Psalm 58:6–9 — A Prayer for God to Break the Power of the Wicked

David calls on God to break the teeth and fangs of the wicked and to make their strength flow away like water, their arrows break in pieces, and their lives melt away like a snail. Before the cooking pots can feel the heat of the thorns, God will sweep them away as with a whirlwind.

3. Psalm 58:10–11 — Surely He Is God Who Judges in the Earth

The righteous will rejoice when they see God’s vengeance on the wicked. People will then say, “Surely there is a reward for the righteous; Surely He is God who judges in the earth.”
