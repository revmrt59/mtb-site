EG Culture - Psalms 117

We are a Tabernacle of David

Culture Statement

We were created to delight the Father and host His presence. As we embrace His ways, our lives become continual offerings of praise—both personally and together. We live with unhindered access to God, ministering to Him first and carrying His presence wherever we go. As His dwelling is restored among us, we become a people through whom the nations are drawn to seek the Lord.

Living It in Psalms 117

“Praise the LORD, all you Gentiles! Laud Him, all you peoples!” (Psalm 117:1).

Israel's worship was never intended to be limited to Israel. Even as God's people praised Him, they called the nations to join them. Worship was both an offering to the LORD and an invitation for others to know and honor Him.

This reflects the heart of a Tabernacle of David. We minister to the Lord because He is worthy of our love and praise, but His heart is for the nations. We want our worship and the life of God's presence among us to draw others to Him. Our desire is not simply to enjoy His presence ourselves, but to see people everywhere come to know the God we worship.

We Stand with Israel

Culture Statement

We love what the Lord loves. The Lord loves Israel, and we love Israel. We take seriously the Lord’s promise to bless those who bless Israel, and curse those who curse Israel.

Living It in Psalms 117

“For His merciful kindness is great toward us, And the truth of the LORD endures forever” (Psalm 117:2).

The nations are commanded to praise the LORD because of His merciful kindness toward “us,” referring first to Israel. This is remarkable. The Gentiles are invited to worship God because of His enduring love and faithfulness to His people.

Paul explains this connection in Romans 15:8–11. Christ came to confirm God's promises to Israel's fathers so that the Gentiles might glorify God for His mercy. God's faithfulness to Israel became the means through which His mercy reached the nations.

We stand with Israel because we love what God loves and honor His purposes for His people. We do not forget the people through whom God brought His promises and ultimately His Son into the world. As Gentile believers, we have particular reason to rejoice in God's enduring faithfulness to Israel, because through Christ that faithfulness has brought mercy to us.

Unity

Culture Statement

We choose submission to the Holy Spirit and one another over our personal preferences, giftings, and anointings. Our hearts’ desires are to know God and walk in His ways. We understand that the greatest blessings and anointings are found when we are united together in Christ.

Living It in Psalms 117

“Praise the LORD, all you Gentiles! Laud Him, all you peoples!” (Psalm 117:1).

The psalm calls people from every nation and background to worship the same LORD. Though separated by nationality, language, and culture, they are united in the One they are called to praise.

Paul quotes this psalm in Romans 15:11 within his appeal for believers to receive one another just as Christ received them (Romans 15:7). Through Christ, Jews and Gentiles are brought together and united in worship of the same God.

This speaks directly to our culture of unity. Our unity is not built on having the same backgrounds, personalities, preferences, or traditions. It comes from belonging to Jesus Christ and sharing a common desire to know, honor and worship Him. We welcome and receive those whom Christ has received, making room for people from every background to worship and pursue the Lord together.
