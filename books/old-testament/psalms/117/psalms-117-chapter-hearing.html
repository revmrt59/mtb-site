Psalm 117:1
NKJV

Psalms 117:1 Praise the LORD, all you Gentiles! Laud Him, all you peoples!(NKJV)

NLT

Psalm 117:1 Praise the LORD, all you nations.Praise him, all you people of the earth. (NLT)

Understand: What the verse says

The shortest psalm in the Bible opens with a call for every nation and people on earth, not just Israel, to praise the LORD. Paul later quotes this verse to show that God always planned for the Gentiles to join in worshiping Him through Christ.

Observe: Something to notice

God wants me to see His heart for every nation and people. He desires to be known, loved, and worshiped throughout the earth, and His purpose has always been to bring the nations to Himself.

Hear: What God may be speaking to me

“Share My heart for the nations. I want you to worship Me, but I also want you to help others know Me, welcome those who are different from you, and rejoice as people come to My Son.”

Psalm 117:2
NKJV

Psalms 117:2 For His merciful kindness is great toward us, And the truth of the LORD endures forever. Praise the LORD!(NKJV)

NLT

Psalm 117:2 For his unfailing love for us is powerful;the LORD’s faithfulness endures forever. Praise the LORD! (NLT)

Understand: What the verse says

The reason all nations should praise God is that His faithful love toward His people is great and His truth, meaning His reliability in keeping His promises, never ends. The psalm closes as it began, with "Praise the LORD," showing that God's steady love and faithfulness are the heart of all true worship.

Observe: Something to notice

God wants me to know His heart and character. His love is mighty and overflowing with mercy, and His faithfulness never changes. I can trust Him completely because He is always true to Himself and His promises.

Hear: What God may be speaking to me

“See how great My love is toward you. You can trust My heart, rest in My faithfulness, and draw near to Me without fear. Let the revelation of My love and faithfulness fill your heart with praise.”
