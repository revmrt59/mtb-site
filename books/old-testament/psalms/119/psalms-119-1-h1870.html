Summary:

WAY - ( derek , Strong’s [1870])

Summary:

A path, road, or journey. It metaphorically describes a person's course of life, habitual conduct, and moral direction, emphasizing the specific track upon which one chooses to walk.

Full Study:

1. Hebrew Roots / Derivation

Derived from the verb darak (Strong’s [1869]), which means "to tread," "to trample," or "to march."

It carries the physical image of a path that is created or marked out by the constant "treading" of feet over time, representing an established route.

2. Meaning / Lexical Definition

A way, road, distance, journey, or manner.

In the Hebrew mind, a "way" is not just a physical location but a "mode of action" or "custom."

It signifies the entire direction of a person's life—their choices, habits, and the ultimate destination toward which they are moving.

3. Usage in Hebrew Culture & Word Pictures

* The Well-Trod Path: In the ancient Near East, a derek was a road beaten firm by the feet of travelers and animals, representing a clear, established route through a wilderness.

* The Archer’s Bow: The root darak is also used for "treading" or "bending" a bow, suggesting a sense of tension, direction, and specific aim toward a target.

* The Winepress: It described the act of "treading" grapes in a press, representing the active, physical process of producing a result through consistent and intentional movement.

4. Theological Implications

The "way" is never neutral; it is either the way of the righteous or the way of the wicked (Psalm 1).

To be "undefiled in the way" means to keep one's life-path unpolluted by the "customs" of the world, remaining on the track established by God’s Word.

It highlights that holiness is not a single act but a "journey" or a "walk" that requires constant movement in the right direction.

5. Contextual Nuance in Psalm 119:1

In the NKJV of Psalm 119:1, the psalmist declares, "Blessed are the undefiled in the way (derek), who walk in the law of the LORD."

The "way" is immediately defined and restricted by the "law of the LORD" (torah).

This shows that the "path" of a blessed life is not one of a person's own making, but one that is paved with the statutes and commandments of God.

6. Key Uses in Other Verses (NKJV)

* Genesis 6:12: “...for all flesh had corrupted their way (derek) on the earth.”

* Psalm 1:6: “For the LORD knows the way (derek) of the righteous, but the way (derek) of the ungodly shall perish.”

* Psalm 25:4: “Show me Your ways (derek), O LORD; teach me Your paths.”

* Proverbs 3:6: “In all your ways (derek) acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths.”

* Isaiah 53:6: “All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned, every one, to his own way (derek);”

7. Other Relevant Insights

This word highlights that "blessedness" (true well-being) is a result of where one chooses to "tread" daily.

It suggests that the "way" of a believer is a distinct "manner of life" that stands in contrast to the "unprofitable" paths of the world mentioned in Titus 3.

By staying "undefiled" in the way, the believer ensures their "walk" is fruitful and aligned with the "faithful saying" of God’s revelation.
