Summary:

WORD - ( dabar , Strong’s [1697])

Summary:

The objective revelation of God’s will. It signifies the substance, matter, or speech of the LORD, serving as the definitive standard by which a person’s life-path is measured, corrected, and purified.

Full Study:

1. Hebrew Roots / Derivation

Derived from the verb dabar (Strong’s [1696]), which means "to speak" or "to arrange."

In its root sense, it suggests an orderly arrangement of thoughts or a sequence of speech that carries specific weight and purpose. It is not merely a sound, but the "matter" behind the sound.

2. Meaning / Lexical Definition

A word, matter, thing, cause, or speech.

In the Old Testament, it refers to the external, objective communication of God to man.

Unlike imrah (which emphasizes the poetic "utterance"), dabar emphasizes the "substance" or "thing" spoken—the actual truth or command that God has established.

3. Usage in Hebrew Culture & Word Pictures

- The Arranged Matter: From the root "to arrange," it pictured a king organizing his thoughts and decrees into a logical order for his kingdom to follow.

- The Substantial Event: In the Hebrew mind, a dabar was not just a vibration in the air; it was an event or an object. To "speak a word" was to "set a matter" into existence.

- The Divine Decree: It pictured the prophet standing in the council of the LORD to hear the dabar and then "arranging" that truth before the people as a solid foundation for their lives.

4. Theological Implications

The dabar of God is the agency of creation (Psalm 33:6) and the foundation of moral order.

It represents the "faithful saying" of God that is completely reliable and settled in heaven.

Theologically, it is the instrument of "cleansing" because it provides a perfect, external mirror that reveals the internal "defilement" of the human heart.

5. Contextual Nuance in Psalm 119:9

In the NKJV of Psalm 119:9, the question is asked: "How can a young man cleanse his way? By taking heed according to Your word (dabar)."

The dabar is presented as the "cleaning agent." Just as a path is cleared of debris, the "way" (life-path) is cleared of sin by "taking heed" (guarding) according to the "matter" God has spoken.

It shows that "cleansing" is not a subjective feeling but a practical alignment with the "arranged" truth of God's revelation.

6. Key Uses in Other Verses (NKJV)

- Genesis 15:1: “...the word (dabar) of the LORD came to Abram in a vision...”

- Exodus 20:1: “And God spoke all these words (dabar—plural), saying:”

- Psalm 33:6: “By the word (dabar) of the LORD the heavens were made,”

- Psalm 119:105: “Your word (dabar) is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”

- Isaiah 40:8: “The grass withers, the flower fades, but the word (dabar) of our God stands forever.”

7. Other Relevant Insights

This word highlights that "blessedness" (verse 1) is maintained through a "diligent" (verse 4) interaction with what God has "arranged" for us.

It suggests that a "watchful" (Mark 13:37) life is one that "guards" the dabar as a precious treasure.

By using dabar here, the psalmist emphasizes that the "cleansing" comes from the total "substance" of God's revealed truth, which is "profitable" (Titus 3:8) for all of life.
