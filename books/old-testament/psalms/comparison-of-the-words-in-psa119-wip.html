Great question—Psalm 119 is built around a rich vocabulary describing God’s Word from multiple angles. In the NKJV, there are 8 primary English terms used throughout the psalm.

Here is the complete list:

Core NKJV Terms in Psalm 119

  1. Law
    Refers to God’s instruction as a whole—His revealed will and teaching (torah idea).

  2. Testimonies
    Emphasizes God’s witness—what He has declared as true and binding.

  3. Ways
    Refers to the paths or manner of life shaped by God’s Word.

  4. Precepts
    Detailed instructions—specific charges that require careful attention.

  5. Statutes
    Fixed, prescribed boundaries—things engraved or established.

  6. Commandments
    Authoritative commands requiring obedience.

  7. Judgments (sometimes “ordinances”)
    God’s rulings or decisions—what He determines as right.

  8. Word
    A general term for God’s spoken or revealed message.

Additional Term Also Used

  1. Word (as “Promise”)
    In some verses, “word” carries the sense of a spoken promise (Hebrew imrah), and is sometimes translated distinctly as:

  • “Word” (promise-oriented usage)

Helpful Summary Insight (your style)

Psalm 119 is not just repeating itself—it is turning the diamond of God’s Word so you see it from every angle:

  • As instruction (law)

  • As witness (testimonies)

  • As path (ways)

  • As details (precepts)

  • As fixed boundaries (statutes)

  • As commands (commandments)

  • As rulings (judgments)

  • As spoken revelation and promise (word)

Each term adds a layer, showing that the Word of God is not one-dimensional—it governs belief, behavior, direction, and relationship with God.
