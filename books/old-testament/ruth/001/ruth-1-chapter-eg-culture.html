East Gate Culture in Ruth 1

East Gate exists to elevate and empower sons and daughters to be the place where heaven touches earth.

We are committed to teaching you how to hear God’s voice, connect with Him daily, and walk with Him in obedience.

We see from this chapter on the importance of hearing God’s voice and walking with Him in obedience. The events took place in the “days of the judges” when there was “no king in Israel” and “everyone did what was right in his own eyes.” (Judges 21:25).

The Scripture seems to indicate that Elimelech led his family to Moab based on the immediate need and pressures to feed his family rather than at the voice or direction of the Lord.

In this chapter, we see the long-term consequences of responding out of fear, immediate need, or pressure. God was merciful to redeem the situation, but how much pain could have been avoided, had Elimelech taking the time to “hear, connect, and obey”.
