Ruth 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Ruth 1 sets out the need that the rest of the book answers. Famine and three deaths in Moab empty Naomi’s household; on the road home Ruth the Moabitess binds herself to Naomi, her people, and her God before any redeemer appears, and the two women reach Bethlehem at the beginning of barley harvest.

Chapter Sections

1. Ruth 1:1–5 — Famine, Moab, and Naomi Bereaved

In the days when the judges ruled, famine drives Elimelech’s family from Bethlehem, Judah, to the country of Moab, where Elimelech dies and his sons marry Orpah and Ruth. After the family has lived there about ten years, Mahlon and Chilion also die, leaving Naomi without husband or sons.

2. Ruth 1:6–7 — The LORD Gives Bread, and Naomi Sets Out for Home

Naomi hears in Moab that “the LORD had visited His people by giving them bread.” She arises with her two daughters-in-law, and they set out on the way to return to the land of Judah.

3. Ruth 1:8–14 — Naomi Releases Her Daughters-in-Law

On the road Naomi urges both women to return to their mothers’ houses, praying that the LORD will deal kindly with them and grant them rest in the house of a husband. She has no sons to offer them; after more weeping Orpah kisses her mother-in-law, but Ruth clings to her.

4. Ruth 1:15–18 — Ruth’s Vow of Allegiance to Naomi and Her God

When Naomi urges her to follow Orpah back to her people and her gods, Ruth vows to share Naomi’s road, her people, her God, her death, and her burial, sealing the vow with an oath in the LORD’s name. Seeing her determined, Naomi stops urging her.

5. Ruth 1:19–22 — Naomi’s Lament and Return at Barley Harvest

All Bethlehem is stirred at their arrival, and Naomi asks to be called Mara, for “the Almighty has dealt very bitterly with me.” She says the LORD has brought her home empty, yet she returns with Ruth the Moabitess at the beginning of barley harvest.
