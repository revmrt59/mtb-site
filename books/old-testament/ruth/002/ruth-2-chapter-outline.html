Ruth 2 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Ruth 2 brings Ruth, through her own initiative and God’s hidden providence, into the field of Boaz, a relative of Elimelech’s family. His protection and generosity toward the Moabite gleaner open new hope when Naomi recognizes him as one of their close relatives.

Chapter Sections

1. Ruth 2:1–3 — Ruth Comes to Glean in the Field of Boaz

The narrator introduces Boaz, a man of great wealth of the family of Elimelech. Ruth asks Naomi’s leave to glean after one in whose sight she may find favor, and she “happened to come to the part of the field belonging to Boaz.”

2. Ruth 2:4–7 — Boaz Greets His Reapers and Asks About Ruth

Boaz arrives from Bethlehem and greets his reapers in the LORD’s name. His overseer identifies the young Moabite woman who came back with Naomi and reports that she asked permission to glean and has worked since morning.

3. Ruth 2:8–13 — Boaz’s Protection and Blessing for the Foreigner

Boaz tells Ruth to stay in his field near his young women, assures her safety, and offers her water. When she asks why he would notice a foreigner, he cites what she has done for Naomi and blesses her as one who has come for refuge under the wings of the LORD God of Israel, and she thanks him for his comfort and kind words.

4. Ruth 2:14–17 — A Place at the Meal and Grain Left on Purpose

At mealtime Boaz invites Ruth to eat with the reapers, and she eats until she is satisfied and keeps some back. He orders his young men to let her glean among the sheaves and to let grain fall purposely for her, and by evening she has about an ephah of barley.

5. Ruth 2:18–23 — Naomi Recognizes Boaz as a Close Relative

Ruth brings home her barley and the food she kept back, and when Naomi hears the name of Boaz, she blesses him of the LORD and says, “This man is a relation of ours, one of our close relatives.” Ruth stays close by his young women through the end of barley and wheat harvest and dwells with her mother-in-law.
