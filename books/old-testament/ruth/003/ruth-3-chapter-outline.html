Ruth 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Ruth 3 moves the story from favor toward redemption. At Naomi’s direction Ruth makes an honorable appeal to Boaz at the threshing floor for marriage and the protection of a close relative, and he pledges to act while honoring the claim of a nearer relative.

Chapter Sections

1. Ruth 3:1–5 — Naomi Seeks Security for Ruth

Naomi resolves to seek security for Ruth and tells her that Boaz, their relative, is winnowing barley at the threshing floor that night. She instructs Ruth to prepare herself, go down, uncover his feet, and lie down, and Ruth agrees to do all she says.

2. Ruth 3:6–9 — Ruth’s Appeal at the Threshing Floor

Ruth does as Naomi instructed, and at midnight Boaz is startled to find a woman lying at his feet. She names herself his maidservant and asks, “Take your maidservant under your wing, for you are a close relative.”

3. Ruth 3:10–13 — Boaz Blesses Ruth and Pledges to Act

Boaz blesses Ruth for showing greater kindness in not going after young men and promises to do all she asks, for all the people of his town know that she is a virtuous woman. Because a relative closer than he has the prior right, he swears by the LORD to perform the duty himself if that man will not.

4. Ruth 3:14–18 — Ruth Returns with Barley to Wait

Ruth lies at his feet until morning and rises before anyone can be recognized, and Boaz sends her home with six ephahs of barley so that she will not go back to Naomi empty-handed. Naomi tells her to sit still, for “the man will not rest until he has concluded the matter this day.”
