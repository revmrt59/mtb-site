Ruth 4 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Ruth 4 completes the romance of redemption. At the city gate Boaz publicly redeems Elimelech’s land and acquires Ruth the Moabitess as his wife, and the LORD gives them a son whose birth restores Naomi’s family and leads, through the closing genealogy, to David.

Chapter Sections

1. Ruth 4:1–2 — Boaz Gathers the Close Relative and the Elders at the Gate

Boaz sits down at the city gate, calls aside the close relative of whom he had spoken, and seats ten of the city’s elders as witnesses.

2. Ruth 4:3–6 — The Nearer Relative Declines to Redeem

Boaz sets before the relative the land that belonged to Elimelech, and he agrees to redeem it. When Boaz explains that the purchase also involves Ruth the Moabitess, the wife of the dead, “to perpetuate the name of the dead through his inheritance,” the relative declines, “lest I ruin my own inheritance.”

3. Ruth 4:7–10 — Boaz Redeems Before Witnesses

By the custom of the sandal the nearer relative yields his right to Boaz. Boaz then declares before the elders and all the people that he has bought all that was Elimelech’s, Chilion’s, and Mahlon’s, and has acquired Ruth the Moabitess as his wife, so that the name of the dead will not be cut off.

4. Ruth 4:11–12 — The Witnesses Bless Boaz’s House

The people at the gate and the elders answer, “We are witnesses.” They pray that the LORD will make the woman coming into his house like Rachel and Leah and his house like the house of Perez.

5. Ruth 4:13–17 — The LORD Gives a Son, and Naomi’s Family Is Restored

Boaz takes Ruth as his wife, and the LORD gives her conception, and she bears a son. The women bless the LORD, who has not left Naomi without a close relative, praise Ruth’s love as better than seven sons, and name the child Obed, the father of Jesse, the father of David.

6. Ruth 4:18–22 — The Genealogy from Perez to David

The book closes with “the genealogy of Perez,” which runs through Boaz and Obed to Jesse and David.
