Song Of Solomon 1 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Song of Solomon 1 opens the journey into intimacy with the King’s welcome: His love awakens the bride’s desire, and He brings her into His chambers before her love is mature. The chapter then moves from her shame and neglected vineyard, through her question about where He feeds His flock, to His praise, the fragrance of His table, and their first exchange of delight.

Chapter Sections

1. Song of Solomon 1:1–4 — Drawn into the King’s Chambers

The title presents “The song of songs, which is Solomon’s,” and the bride’s longing for His kisses and His name leads to the reason behind it: “The king has brought me into his chambers.” She joins the virgins who loved Him before her and agrees from her own experience, “Rightly do they love you.”

2. Song of Solomon 1:5–8 — Dark but Lovely, and the Way to the Flock

Before the daughters of Jerusalem she confesses that she is “dark, but lovely,” scorched by the sun and worn out keeping her brothers’ vineyards, while “my own vineyard I have not kept.” She asks where He feeds His flock and makes it rest at noon, and she is told to follow the footsteps of the flock and feed her little goats beside the shepherds’ tents.

3. Song of Solomon 1:9–11 — Praised and Adorned by the Beloved

The Beloved answers her shame with open delight, comparing her to a filly among Pharaoh’s chariots and admiring her cheeks and neck. The promise of gold ornaments with silver studs presents her beauty and honor as gifts given to her.

4. Song of Solomon 1:12–14 — Fragrance at the King’s Table

While the King is at His table, her spikenard sends forth its fragrance, the worship His presence draws out of her. She holds her Beloved close as a bundle of myrrh through the night and delights in Him as a cluster of henna blooms in the vineyards of En Gedi.

5. Song of Solomon 1:15–17 — Love Received and Returned

He says, “Behold, you are fair, my love!” and she answers, “Behold, you are handsome, my beloved!” in the first exchange of praise in the Song. The green bed and the beams of cedar and fir picture the rest and lasting fellowship she now shares with Him.
