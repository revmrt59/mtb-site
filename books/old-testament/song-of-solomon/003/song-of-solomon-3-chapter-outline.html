Song Of Solomon 3 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Song of Solomon 3 closes the call to follow and opens the revelation of the King. The bride seeks the Beloved by night, passes the watchmen, finds and holds Him, and the second charge guards that recovered communion; then a royal procession from the wilderness turns her gaze to Solomon’s guarded couch, his palanquin, and his crowning on the day of his wedding.

Chapter Sections

1. Song of Solomon 3:1–3 — Seeking Him by Night

Missing the One she sent away, she seeks Him on her bed and does not find Him, so she rises and searches the streets and squares of the city. The watchmen find her, and she asks, “Have you seen the one I love?”

2. Song of Solomon 3:4–5 — Found, Held, and the Second Charge

Just past the watchmen she finds Him, holds Him, and will not let Him go until she has brought Him into her mother’s house, the place where her life began. The charge not to stir up nor awaken love until it pleases returns as the second rest.

3. Song of Solomon 3:6–8 — Out of the Wilderness: Solomon’s Guarded Couch

A fragrant procession rises from the wilderness like pillars of smoke, perfumed with myrrh and frankincense. It is Solomon’s couch, surrounded by sixty valiant men whose swords are ready against fear in the night.

4. Song of Solomon 3:9–11 — The Palanquin and the Wedding Crown

Solomon has made himself a palanquin of Lebanon wood, with pillars of silver, support of gold, a seat of purple, and an interior paved with love. The daughters of Zion are called to go out and see the King wearing the crown his mother gave him “On the day of his wedding, The day of the gladness of his heart.”
