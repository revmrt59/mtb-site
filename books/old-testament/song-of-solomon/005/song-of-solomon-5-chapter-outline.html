Song Of Solomon 5 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Song of Solomon 5 begins with the Beloved receiving the garden the bride has offered and then tests that surrender in a new night scene. Comfort delays her answer to His knock, He is gone when she opens, the watchmen wound her as she searches, and the daughters’ question draws out her fullest praise of Him.

Chapter Sections

1. Song of Solomon 5:1 — The Beloved Receives His Garden

He answers her invitation at once: “I have come to my garden, my sister, my spouse,” gathering its myrrh and spice and eating and drinking its fruit. The closing call, “Eat, O friends!” carries the fruit of the surrendered garden beyond the two of them.

2. Song of Solomon 5:2–3 — The Knock at Night

Her heart is awake when He knocks and calls, “Open for me, my sister, my love,” His head wet with the dew of the night. She has taken off her robe and washed her feet, and her comfort makes her slow to rise.

3. Song of Solomon 5:4–6 — Myrrh on Her Hands at the Lock

When He puts His hand by the latch, her heart yearns for Him, and she rises with myrrh dripping from her hands onto the handles of the lock. She opens, but He has turned away and gone; she seeks Him and calls, and He gives no answer.

4. Song of Solomon 5:7–8 — Wounded by the Watchmen

The watchmen who go about the city strike her, wound her, and take away her veil. Still she charges the daughters of Jerusalem that if they find her Beloved, they will tell Him she is lovesick.

5. Song of Solomon 5:9–16 — Her Witness to the Beloved

The daughters ask what makes her Beloved more than another, and she answers with praise of Him from His head of finest gold to His lips dripping liquid myrrh and His countenance like Lebanon. She ends, “Yes, he is altogether lovely. This is my beloved, And this is my friend.”
