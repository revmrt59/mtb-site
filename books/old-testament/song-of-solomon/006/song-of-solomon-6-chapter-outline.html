Song Of Solomon 6 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Song of Solomon 6 completes the test of chapter 5 and opens the bride’s restoration. Her witness leads the daughters to seek Him with her and ends in the second declaration of belonging; then the Beloved renews His praise without reproach and singles her out as the only one, grace makes her swift and willing, and she is called back as the Shulamite to be seen.

Chapter Sections

1. Song of Solomon 6:1–3 — The Garden and the Second Declaration

Moved by her praise, the daughters ask where her Beloved has gone so that they may seek Him with her. She knows that He has gone to His garden, and she declares, “I am my beloved’s, And my beloved is mine.”

2. Song of Solomon 6:4–7 — Praise Renewed Without Reproach

He calls her as beautiful as Tirzah, as lovely as Jerusalem, and as awesome as an army with banners, and He says nothing of her delay at the door. Her eyes overcome Him, and He takes up again the images of His earlier praise.

3. Song of Solomon 6:8–10 — The Only One Who Looks Forth as the Morning

Among sixty queens, eighty concubines, and virgins without number, His dove is the only one; the daughters see her and call her blessed, and the queens and concubines praise her. An unnamed voice then asks who she is who looks forth as the morning, fair as the moon, clear as the sun, and awesome as an army with banners.

4. Song of Solomon 6:11–12 — Down to the Garden of Nuts

The bride goes down to the garden of nuts to see whether the vine has budded and the pomegranates have bloomed. Before she is even aware, she finds herself made like the chariots of her noble people, swift and willing to move.

5. Song of Solomon 6:13 — The Shulamite Called Back to Be Seen

She is called back by name, “Return, return, O Shulamite,” so that others may look upon her. What they see in her is likened to “the dance of the two camps.”
