Song Of Solomon 7 Chapter Outline

Chapter Focus

Song of Solomon 7 continues the movement of restoration and partnership. The King praises the Shulamite from her sandaled feet to her head and comes to the palm for its fruit; she takes up His words, rests in the third declaration, “I am my beloved’s, And his desire is toward me,” and invites Him into the fields, with fruits new and old laid up for Him.

Chapter Sections

1. Song of Solomon 7:1–6 — Praised from Her Sandaled Feet to Her Head

Addressing her as “O prince’s daughter,” the King praises her feet in sandals and moves upward through her waist, neck, eyes, and head until a king is held captive by her tresses. He exclaims, “How fair and how pleasant you are, O love, with your delights!”

2. Song of Solomon 7:7–9 — The Palm Tree and the Best Wine

He compares her stature to a palm tree and resolves to go up and take hold of its branches and its fruit. In the middle of 7:9 she takes up His image of the best wine and turns it toward Him: “The wine goes down smoothly for my beloved, Moving gently the lips of sleepers.”

3. Song of Solomon 7:10–13 — Resting in His Desire, She Invites Him to the Fields

She declares the third mark of belonging, “I am my beloved’s, And his desire is toward me.” From that rest she invites Him to go out with her to the fields, villages, and vineyards to see what is in bloom, and she offers Him fruits new and old “Which I have laid up for you, my beloved.”
